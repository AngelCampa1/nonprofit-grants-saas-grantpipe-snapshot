# GrantPipe Product Marketing Context

GrantPipe is a compliance-first grant management system. It connects donor records, grants, restricted funds, compliance deadlines, evidence, reporting, and fund accounting in one workspace.

## Positioning Rule

Lead with compliance-first grant management system. Use grant management
software built for compliance as the SEO phrase where useful. Use the broader
workspace as proof and scope: donor records, restricted funds, compliance,
evidence, reporting, and fund accounting stay connected to grant work. Do not
cut those product promises, and do not make "operating system" the first public
category phrase.

## Grant Opportunity Positioning

GrantPipe supports a multi-source grant pipeline:

- Grants.gov federal opportunity search is included.
- State/local, private foundation, community foundation, corporate, association, and other opportunities can be manually tracked or imported from CSV.
- Foundation prospect context can use public nonprofit filings where available.

Claim boundary: GrantPipe does not claim an Instrumentl-scale proprietary private foundation, corporate, association, or state/local opportunity database. Grants.gov remains the live searchable opportunity source. Non-federal opportunities are first-class tracked/imported records, and public foundation data is funder/prospect context rather than guaranteed current deadlines.

## Pricing Copy

Use these plan-inclusion phrases consistently:

- Grants.gov federal opportunity search included.
- Manual/imported non-federal opportunity tracking.
- Foundation prospect context from public nonprofit filings where available.

## Audience

Primary audience: Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance leads at mid-sized nonprofits with roughly $500K-$10M annual budgets.

Write from the builder perspective. Do not claim nonprofit operator experience, fabricate testimonials, or imply proprietary private grant data unless a licensed source is added later.
