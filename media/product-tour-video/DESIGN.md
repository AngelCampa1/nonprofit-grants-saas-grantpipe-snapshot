# GrantPipe Product Tour Video Design

## Style Prompt

Create a professional product walkthrough for GrantPipe: calm, credible,
precise, and product-led. The video should feel like a guided SaaS walkthrough
for nonprofit leaders who need confidence that donors, grants, restricted
funds, compliance work, and reporting context can live in one operating
workspace. Use real app footage as the hero. Motion should clarify the product,
not decorate it.

## Colors

- `#fbfaf6` - warm paper background.
- `#ffffff` - product and card surfaces.
- `#047857` - primary emerald for brand, CTA, progress, and active states.
- `#0e1a16` - deep ink for headlines and high-emphasis text.
- `#5b6e66` - muted ink for supporting text.
- `#d99a18` - rare muted gold accent only for trial or deadline highlights.

## Typography

- Heading: Spectral, Georgia fallback.
- Body: Manrope, system sans-serif fallback.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, ui-monospace fallback.

## Voice Direction

Warm, confident female SaaS narrator. Neutral American accent. Calm, clear,
intelligent, approachable, medium-low pitch, steady pace, subtle energy,
polished studio delivery. Use HyperFrames Kokoro `af_nova` unless a better
local voice is explicitly selected during iteration.

## Motion

- Use restrained transitions, short vertical lifts, and gentle zoom windows.
- Keep the app footage readable. Avoid fast pans or excessive overlays.
- Cursor movement should feel deliberate and human, not frantic.
- Keep product footage clean. Use voiceover for explanation; avoid annotation
  layers during app footage unless a future pass has a precise reason.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make gold a dominant brand color.
- Do not use loud gradients, generic SaaS blue, neon effects, or hype motion.
- Do not use rectangular focus frames, box callouts around app UI, persistent
  chapter labels, lower thirds, diagram boxes, or overlay furniture that makes
  the product look arbitrarily annotated.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, customer counts, user counts, nonprofit
  experience, revenue claims, or benchmark proof.
- Do not show passwords, secrets, local env values, or production data.
- Do not reuse `media/explainer-video` as the source project; this is separate.
