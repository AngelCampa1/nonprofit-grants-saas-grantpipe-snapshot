# GrantPipe Launch Video Design

## Style Prompt

GrantPipe's launch video should feel like a calm operating room for nonprofit teams: warm, credible, precise, and product-led. The visual system uses a warm paper canvas, white software surfaces, emerald as the primary signal, and deep ink for text. Motion is deliberate and composed, with product screens gliding into place rather than bursting or bouncing.

## Colors

- `#fbfaf6` - warm paper background.
- `#ffffff` - product and card surfaces.
- `#047857` - primary emerald for brand, CTA, and active states.
- `#0e1a16` - deep ink for headlines and high-emphasis text.
- `#5b6e66` - muted ink for supporting copy.
- `#d99a18` - rare muted gold accent only for small launch/deadline highlights.

## Typography

- Heading: Spectral, Georgia fallback.
- Body: Manrope, system sans-serif fallback.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, ui-monospace fallback.

## Motion

- Use steady fades, short vertical lifts, and product-screen parallax.
- Keep easing practical: `power2.out`, `power3.out`, `power2.inOut`.
- Avoid frantic cuts; leave enough time to read silent copy.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make gold a dominant brand color.
- Do not use loud gradients, generic SaaS blue, or neon effects.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, user counts, nonprofit experience, or launch metrics.
- Do not use placeholder product UI when real screenshots are available.
- Do not rely on voiceover; the video must work silently.
