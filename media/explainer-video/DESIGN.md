# GrantPipe Explainer Video Design

## Style Prompt

Extend the GrantPipe launch-video system into a voice-led explainer: calm, credible, precise, and product-led. The visual system should feel like a quiet operating room for nonprofit teams, with warm paper canvas, white software surfaces, emerald as the primary signal, and deep ink for text. Motion is deliberate and composed, with product screens gliding into place rather than bursting or bouncing.

## Colors

- `#fbfaf6` - warm paper background.
- `#ffffff` - product and card surfaces.
- `#047857` - primary emerald for brand, CTA, and active states.
- `#0e1a16` - deep ink for headlines and high-emphasis text.
- `#5b6e66` - muted ink for supporting copy.
- `#d99a18` - rare muted gold accent only for trial/launch highlights.

## Typography

- Heading: Spectral, Georgia fallback.
- Body: Manrope, system sans-serif fallback.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, ui-monospace fallback.

## Voice Direction

Default voice target: warm, confident female SaaS narrator in her late 30s. Neutral American accent. Calm, clear, intelligent, approachable, medium-low pitch, steady pace, subtle energy, polished studio delivery.

Fallback used for local generation: HyperFrames Kokoro `af_nova` as the closest available US female presenter voice. The selected render uses an energized `af_nova` take with a modest speed and pitch lift; `af_heart` remains as the deeper A/B alternate.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make gold a dominant brand color.
- Do not use loud gradients, generic SaaS blue, or neon effects.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, user counts, nonprofit experience, or launch metrics.
- Do not use placeholder product UI when real screenshots are available.
- Do not make the piece feel like founder hype, influencer content, or enterprise robot narration.
