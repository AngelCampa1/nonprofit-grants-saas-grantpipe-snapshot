## 7. Billing & Subscription Tier Management

**Plan Tier Rename & Reprice**

GrantPipe's plan tiers have been renamed and repriced to better reflect customer segment and capabilities:

| Old Name   | New Name    | Monthly | Annual | Notes            |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------- | ------ | ---------------- |
| Foundation | Starter     | $49     | $468   | Annual saves 20% |
| Growth     | Growth      | $99     | $948   | Annual saves 20% |
| Enterprise | Audit-Ready | $199    | $1,908 | Annual saves 20% |
| Enterprise | Enterprise  | Custom  | Custom | Contact founder  |

Annual prices are shown as monthly equivalents on the pricing page: Starter $39/mo, Growth $79/mo, and Audit-Ready $159/mo billed annually.

**Grandfathering Strategy**

Existing subscribers on the old Stripe price IDs remain grandfathered on their original pricing. No automatic migration occurs:

1. **Existing subscriptions** - orgs with active subscriptions tied to old price IDs (`price_live_...foundation...`, `price_live_...enterprise...`) keep their existing subscription and billing.
2. **New signups** - orgs created after the tier rename use the new Stripe price IDs and new pricing.
3. **Stripe subscription_id** - the database migration renames `plan_tier` values (`foundation` → `starter`, `enterprise` → `audit_ready`) but does NOT touch `subscription_id`. Grandfathered orgs will continue charging against their original Stripe subscription objects - no manual Stripe changes needed.
4. **Manual upgrades/downgrades** - if a grandfathered org upgrades or downgrades to a different tier, the Stripe subscription is updated to the new price for that tier. If they remain on their current tier (no tier change), the old price persists indefinitely.

**Before Going Live - Operational Prerequisites**

1. **Create 6 new Stripe Products & Prices (live mode):**
