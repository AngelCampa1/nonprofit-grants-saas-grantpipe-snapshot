# Claim Safety Review

Reviewed on 2026-05-15 against `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

## Approved Claims

- GrantPipe is a compliance-first grant management system.
- GrantPipe includes donor records, grants, restricted funds, compliance deadlines, evidence, reporting, and fund accounting in one workspace.
- Grants.gov federal opportunity search is included.
- Non-federal opportunities can be manually tracked or imported from CSV.
- Foundation prospect context can use public nonprofit filings where available.
- Primary audience is Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance leads at mid-sized nonprofits with roughly $500K-$10M annual budgets.
- GrantPipe is built from the founder-builder perspective.

## Removed or Avoided Claims

- No customer logos.
- No user counts.
- No testimonials.
- No review score claims.
- No award claims.
- No nonprofit operator experience claims.
- No "best" or unsupported leadership claims.
- No claim that GrantPipe has a proprietary private foundation, corporate, association, or state/local opportunity database.
- No guarantee that SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, or G2 will award a badge.

## Platform-Specific Risk Checks

SaaSHub:

- Competitors are listed to avoid slower review.
- Product is described as live SaaS, not a waitlist.

AlternativeTo:

- GrantPipe is marked proprietary/paid/free-trial, not open source.
- Alternatives are real buyer substitutes.

BetaList:

- GrantPipe is positioned as recently launched only if that framing is credible under BetaList's recency rule.
- Startup copy does not claim press coverage, traction, user counts, or customer logos.
- The submission uses the main domain and direct signup path, not a free-hosted page.

Product Hunt:

- First comment asks for feedback, not upvotes.
- Launch copy does not overstate novelty or traction.

G2:

- Badge copy says Users Love Us is dependent on 20 reviews with 4.0+ average.
- Review request asks for honest feedback, not positive reviews.
