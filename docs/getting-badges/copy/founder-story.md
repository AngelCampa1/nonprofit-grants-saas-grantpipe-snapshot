# Founder Story

Use this where a directory asks why the product exists or who made it.

## Short Version

```text
GrantPipe was built by Angel Campa, a Principal SDET and founder building vertical SaaS for grant-funded nonprofits. The product comes from a builder's view of the operational gap: donor records, grant awards, restricted funds, deadlines, and evidence often live in separate systems even though staff need them together.
```

## Medium Version

```text
I built GrantPipe after studying the way grant-funded nonprofits are forced to stitch together donor CRMs, grant spreadsheets, accounting files, calendars, and shared folders. The product is designed around a simple operating problem: when reporting week or compliance follow-up arrives, the team should not have to rebuild the story of a grant from five places.

GrantPipe brings donor records, active grants, restricted funds, compliance deadlines, evidence, reporting, and fund accounting into one workspace for nonprofit staff.
```

## Product Hunt Maker Version

```text
I am Angel Campa, founder of GrantPipe and Principal SDET. I am building GrantPipe from the builder perspective, not by claiming nonprofit operator experience.

The product exists because grant-funded teams often have the right data, but it is split across donor CRMs, spreadsheets, accounting files, calendars, and folders. GrantPipe keeps those records together so reporting and compliance follow-up start from the operating record instead of a scramble.
```
