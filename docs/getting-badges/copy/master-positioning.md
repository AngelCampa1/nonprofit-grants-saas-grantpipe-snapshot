# Master Positioning

Use this as the source of truth when filling profile forms.

## One-Line Positioning

```text
GrantPipe is a compliance-first grant management system.
```

## Plain-English Description

```text
GrantPipe brings donor records, active grants, restricted funds, compliance deadlines, evidence, reporting, and fund accounting into one workspace.
```

## Audience

```text
Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance leads at mid-sized nonprofits with roughly $500K-$10M annual budgets.
```

## Category

```text
Nonprofit CRM
Grant management software
Grant compliance software
Donor management software
Restricted fund tracking
Fund accounting workflow
```

## What GrantPipe Replaces

```text
Disconnected donor CRMs, grant spreadsheets, reporting calendars, shared folders, accounting side files, and consultant-heavy CRM builds.
```

## Feature Language

Use:

- Donor CRM
- Grant pipeline tracking
- Grants.gov federal opportunity search
- Manual/imported non-federal opportunity tracking
- Foundation prospect context from public nonprofit filings where available
- Restricted-fund views
- Compliance calendar
- Evidence bundles
- Reporting workflows
- Fund accounting
- Activity history

Avoid:

- Proprietary private grant database
- Guaranteed foundation opportunities
- Customer-loved
- Trusted by nonprofits
- Award-winning
- Built by nonprofit operators
- Best nonprofit CRM

## Buyer Problem

```text
Grant-funded nonprofits often manage the donor relationship in one tool, active grants in a spreadsheet, fund restrictions in accounting files, reporting deadlines in calendars, and evidence in folders. GrantPipe keeps those records connected so reporting deadlines and compliance follow-up do not require rebuilding the story from scratch.
```
