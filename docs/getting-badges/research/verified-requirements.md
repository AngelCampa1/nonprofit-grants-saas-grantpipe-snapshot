# Verified Platform Requirements

Verified on 2026-05-15.

## SaaSHub

Source: [SaaSHub Submit Product](https://www.saashub.com/services/submit)

Requirements and notes:

- Submit a live product website URL.
- Add categories.
- List competitors. SaaSHub says submissions without competitors are slowed down and moved lower in the queue.
- Verify the product with an email address on the product domain for higher priority.
- Submitted products go through approval.
- Rejected product types include unreleased products, waitlist-only landing pages, software development agencies, free-subdomain products, and non-English products.

- Ready to submit.
- Use `https://grantpipe.com/` and a `grantpipe.com` verification email.
- Include competitors from `copy/master-positioning.md`.

## Product Hunt

Sources: [How to post a product](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product), [Product Hunt featuring guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines), [access to post](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/481909-how-can-i-get-access-to-post), [categories](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/8104478-how-to-add-a-category-to-a-product), [scheduling](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/2724119-how-to-schedule-a-post)

Requirements and notes:

- Use a personal account. Company accounts cannot post, vote, or comment.
- Use the direct product URL.
- Name should be just the product name.
- Add a short tagline.
- Pick only a few strongly related topics. Product Hunt says up to 3 categories helps discovery.
- Recommended thumbnail is 240x240.
- Recommended gallery size is 1270x760.
- Gallery needs 2+ images before it is viewable.
- Videos must be YouTube links.
- Description limit is 260 characters.
- Add makers by Product Hunt username.
- Add a first comment.
- Create draft or schedule. Scheduling is available within 30 days. Confirm exact go-live timing in the Product Hunt UI before launch.
- Featuring criteria emphasize useful, live, practical, well-crafted digital products.

GrantPipe status:

- Draft copy is ready.
- Needs YouTube upload, launch date, outreach list, and gallery derivatives before scheduling.

## BetaList

Sources: [BetaList submission guidelines](https://betalist.com/criteria), [submission terms](https://betalist.com/terms/submissions), [support FAQ](https://betalist.com/support), [landing page guide](https://guides.betalist.com/designing-your-landing-page)

Requirements and notes:

- BetaList is for early adopters discovering upcoming and recently launched internet startups.
- Product should be unreleased, private beta, or recently launched.
- Product must be a technology startup.
- Startup must have a working website on its own domain.
- BetaList does not accept app-store-only links or pages hosted on free subdomains.
- Landing page should be distinct, decent-looking, and not a generic template with little product information.
- Visitors should be able to sign up, get access, download, or log in.
- Screenshots or a screencast help explain the product.
- Regular review can take up to a few weeks.
- If selected, BetaList says you may receive an email within about a week.
- After acceptance, regular listings typically take about two months to be featured.
- Priority listing is reviewed within a few days and, if accepted, featured shortly after. It speeds review but does not guarantee acceptance.
- BetaList may edit the submission for grammar, clarity, and length, select or crop images, and categorize the product.

GrantPipe status:

- Conditional. Submit only if GrantPipe can credibly be framed as recently launched under BetaList's recency rule. If launch was weeks ago or longer, treat BetaList as higher risk or wait for a materially new launch.
- Use `https://grantpipe.com/` as the website.
- Use `https://app.grantpipe.com/signup` as the access path if the form asks.
- Needs no waitlist framing because signup is available.

## G2

Sources: [Create a G2 profile](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile), [G2 Product Information](https://documentation.g2.com/g2/docs/product-information), [G2 Badges](https://documentation.g2.com/docs/fr/g2-badges)

Requirements and notes:

- If a product is not on G2, submit a request form.
- G2 says its research team verifies a new profile in about 3-5 business days.
- Once the profile is live, claim it. G2 says claim approval is reviewed in about 1-3 business days.
- Product name should match the official website and avoid promotional terms.
- Profile details allow up to 500 words.
- Profile logo must be JPG, PNG, or GIF and at least 400 pixels.
- Grid logo must be SVG and under 5 MB.
- Banner image should be 1260x240 and under 5 MB. G2 recommends 2500x476 if rendering is off.
- Banner video links can use Vimeo, Vidyard, Wistia, or YouTube.
- Users Love Us requires 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average.
- Free G2 profiles can access and share Users Love Us.

GrantPipe status:

- Profile request copy is ready.
- Needs generated G2 logo/banner derivatives before upload.
- Badge is review-dependent and should not be claimed before the review threshold is met.
