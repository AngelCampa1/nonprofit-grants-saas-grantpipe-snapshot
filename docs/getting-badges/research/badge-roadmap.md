# Badge Roadmap

Verified on 2026-05-15.

## Immediate

SaaSHub:

- Submit GrantPipe.
- Verify with a `grantpipe.com` email.
- Once approved, use the listing as directory proof.

AlternativeTo:

- Submit GrantPipe as SaaS/web software.
- Seed the correct alternatives.
- Ask real users or supporters to like/review only after the listing is approved.

BetaList:

- Submit GrantPipe only if it can credibly meet BetaList's recency rule.
- Use the main website and direct signup path.
- Use the dashboard screenshot and explainer video source if media is requested.

G2:

- Request or claim the profile.
- Fill profile, logo, banner, category, and overview.
- Start review collection after profile is ready.

Product Hunt:

- Create a draft.
- Do not schedule until YouTube video, gallery images, launch date, and launch outreach are ready.

## Review-Dependent

G2 Users Love Us:

- Requires 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average.
- Free G2 profiles can access and share this badge.

G2 report badges:

- Require G2 report recognition.
- Downloading and sharing many report badges may require paid access.

Product Hunt validation:

- Depends on launch performance, comments, reviews, and ranking.
- Do not display a Product Hunt badge until the launch result exists.

## Suggested 30-Day Sequence

Week 1:

- Submit SaaSHub.
- Submit AlternativeTo.
- Submit BetaList if the recency framing is credible. Otherwise hold it for a materially new launch.
- Submit or claim G2.
- Generate Product Hunt and G2 image derivatives.

Week 2:

- Complete G2 profile after approval.
- Upload Product Hunt video to YouTube.
- Create Product Hunt draft.
- Build launch outreach list.

Week 3:

- Start G2 review asks with real users.
- Final Product Hunt launch review.

Week 4:

- Launch Product Hunt only if the draft and outreach list are ready.
- Follow up on G2 reviews once after 5 business days.
- Add approved directory URLs to marketing proof inventory.
