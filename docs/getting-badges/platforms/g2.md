# G2 Profile and Badge Guide

Sources: [Create a G2 profile](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile), [G2 Product Information](https://documentation.g2.com/g2/docs/product-information), and [G2 Badges](https://documentation.g2.com/docs/fr/g2-badges). Verified on 2026-05-15.

## Platform Facts

- If GrantPipe is not on G2, submit the profile request first.
- G2 says its research team verifies a new product or service in about 3-5 business days before placing it in the right category.
- After the profile is live, claim it. G2 says claim approval is reviewed in about 1-3 business days.
- Product names should match the official website and avoid promotional terms.
- Profile details allow up to 500 words.
- Profile logo must be JPG, PNG, or GIF and at least 400 pixels.
- Grid logo must be SVG and under 5 MB.
- Profile banner should be 1260x240 and under 5 MB. If rendering is off, G2 recommends 2500x476.
- Banner video links can use Vimeo, Vidyard, Wistia, or YouTube.
- Users Love Us requires 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average.
- Free G2 profiles can access and share Users Love Us. Downloading and sharing many report badges requires upgraded access.

## Profile Overview

Paste into "What is GrantPipe?":

```text
GrantPipe is a compliance-first grant management system.

It brings donor records, active grants, restricted funds, compliance deadlines, evidence, reporting, and fund accounting into one workspace. The product is built for Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance leads at mid-sized nonprofits with roughly $500K-$10M annual budgets.

GrantPipe helps teams replace disconnected donor CRMs, grant spreadsheets, reporting calendars, shared folders, and accounting side files with one connected operating record. Staff can track the donor relationship, grant award, fund restriction, reporting deadline, evidence, and financial context together instead of rebuilding that view during reporting and compliance follow-up.

Core capabilities include donor CRM, grant pipeline tracking, Grants.gov federal opportunity search, manual and imported non-federal opportunity tracking, restricted-fund views, compliance calendars, evidence bundles, activity history, reporting workflows, and fund accounting.

GrantPipe is a fit for nonprofits that manage several active grants and want staff-led setup without a custom Salesforce build or a consultant-heavy implementation.
```

## Review Collection Plan

Target:

```text
20 reviews with a 4.0+ average for Users Love Us.
```

Review ask criteria:

- Ask only real users who have used GrantPipe.
- Do not ask for a positive review.
- Ask for an honest review.
- Send one follow-up after 5 business days.
- Track status in a spreadsheet: invited, clicked, started, submitted, approved, not approved.

## Review Before Submit

- Profile copy is under 500 words.
- No fabricated customers or review counts.
- Category explanation includes at least 10 comparable products if G2 asks for a new category.
- Badge copy clearly says Users Love Us is review-dependent.

## Not Applicable

- On-premise deployment: not applicable.
- Mobile-only product: not applicable.
- Open source license: not applicable.
- Public customer logos: not applicable until real approved logos exist.
- Review badge: not applicable until the review threshold is met.
