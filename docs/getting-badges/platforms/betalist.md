# BetaList Submission Guide

Sources: [BetaList submission guidelines](https://betalist.com/criteria), [submission terms](https://betalist.com/terms/submissions), [support FAQ](https://betalist.com/support), and [landing page guide](https://guides.betalist.com/designing-your-landing-page). Verified on 2026-05-15.

## Platform Requirements

BetaList is for early adopters discovering upcoming and recently launched internet startups. Submit GrantPipe only if it can credibly be framed as recently launched under BetaList's recency rule. If the product launched weeks ago or longer, treat BetaList as higher risk or wait for a materially new launch.

BetaList wants:

- A technology startup.
- A product that is unreleased, in private beta, or recently launched.
- A working website on your own domain.
- A distinct landing page that explains the product.
- A way for visitors to sign up, get access, download, or log in.
- A clear value proposition.
- Product screenshots or a screencast if available.

BetaList does not want:

- Blogs, newsletters, courses, books, consultancies, or agencies.
- App-store-only links.
- Free hosting subdomains such as `vercel.app`, `netlify.app`, or `herokuapp.com`.
- Generic templates or pages with little product information.
- A page where the only action is "follow us on X."

Review notes:

- Regular review can take up to a few weeks.
- If selected, BetaList says you may receive an email within about a week.
- After acceptance, regular listings typically take about two months to be featured.
- Priority review is optional. It is reviewed within a few days and, if accepted, featured shortly after. It does not guarantee acceptance.
- BetaList may edit copy, crop or select images, categorize the product, and change link treatment.

## Review Before Submit

- Use the real product website, not the app signup URL, as the main website.
- Use the signup URL only when the form asks where visitors can get access.
- Do not describe GrantPipe as unreleased.
- Do not claim public traction, customer counts, or social proof.
- Make sure the live page shows screenshots and a clear signup path before submitting.
