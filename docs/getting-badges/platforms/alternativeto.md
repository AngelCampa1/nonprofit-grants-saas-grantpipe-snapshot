Full description:

```text
GrantPipe is a web-based, compliance-first grant management system. It is for nonprofits that need donor CRM and grant tracking. It keeps restricted-fund views, deadlines, evidence, and reports together. It also keeps fund accounting in the same place.

It is designed for teams that have outgrown spreadsheet grant trackers but do not want a consultant-heavy CRM implementation. GrantPipe keeps each grant connected to the donor, fund, deadline, source documents, and reporting work around it, so Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance leads can work from the same record.

GrantPipe includes Grants.gov federal opportunity search, manual and imported tracking for non-federal opportunities, compliance calendars, evidence bundles, reporting workflows, and restricted-fund context. It fits mid-sized nonprofits with roughly $500K-$10M annual budgets, especially organizations managing multiple active grants alongside donor relationships.
```

## Recommended Framing

Position GrantPipe as an alternative to donor-only CRMs plus spreadsheet-based grant tracking. AlternativeTo visitors are often replacing a tool they already know, so the useful claim is:

```text
For nonprofits where donor work, grant compliance, restricted funds, and reporting have started living in separate systems.
```

## Review Before Submit

- Do not mark GrantPipe as open source.
- Do not present it as a grant-writing copilot.
- Do not say it replaces every accounting system.
- Add alternatives that buyers actually compare, not unrelated startup tools.
