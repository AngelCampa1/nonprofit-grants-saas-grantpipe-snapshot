Pricing FAQ

Common questions about plans, billing, and getting started. Don't see yours? Email me directly.

What counts as an "active grant"?

Anything currently in your pipeline—prospect, drafting, submitted, awarded, or in reporting. Once a grant is fully closed out (final report accepted), it moves to your archive and stops counting against your plan limit.

Why charge by grants instead of users?

Because nonprofit teams shouldn't have to ration logins. The whole org—program staff, finance, ED, board treasurer, even your auditor—should be able to see one record. The work scales with grants, not seats.

How does the 1-month free trial work?

No credit card. You get the Growth plan for 30 days. If you want to keep going, add billing and pick a plan; if not, export your data and walk away. We'll never auto-charge you.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes—up or down, monthly. We pro-rate the difference. If you upgrade because you landed a big grant, the new plan kicks in immediately.

Do you offer discounts for small nonprofits?

The founding-customer cohort gets locked-in pricing for life. If your annual budget is under $500k, talk to me—we can almost always make Starter work.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export everything—donors, grants, restrictions, reports—as CSV, PDF, or JSON, anytime. After cancellation we hold your data for 60 days in case you change your mind, then delete it.

Is GrantPipe SOC 2 / HIPAA / etc.?

SOC 2 Type II is in progress. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted in US regions, with daily backups. For HIPAA or other special requirements, talk to me about Enterprise.

Who built this?

I'm Angel Campa—a solo engineer. I built GrantPipe because I kept watching nonprofit teams stitch programs together with spreadsheets and dread. Founding customers get direct access to me and a real say in the roadmap.
