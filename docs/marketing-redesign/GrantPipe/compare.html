GrantPipe vs spreadsheets

The default tooling. Why it stops working.

Spreadsheets

Free, fast, fragile.

  • One person owns the file. When they're out, the org is blind.
  • Restricted funds tracked in a side tab no auditor will accept.
  • "What's our grant position?" takes a Monday morning to answer.
  • Board reports are screenshots. Audit prep is panic.
  • No deadline alerts. You miss one grant a year, average.
GrantPipe

One record. Same answer for everyone.

  • Imports your existing tracker. You don't lose the work you've done.
  • Restricted funds are first-class. ASC 958 native, not a side sheet.
  • Real-time pipeline + restricted position. Your treasurer can self-serve.
  • Board exports and audit packs are one click.
  • Deadline reminders + federal-grants search built in.

GrantPipe vs Salesforce NPSP

The enterprise option. Why it's a heavy lift for most nonprofits.

Salesforce NPSP + Grants Management

Powerful, but it needs a person.

  • 3–9 month implementation. Often a consultant on retainer.
  • Restricted-fund accounting is a custom build, not a feature.
  • Per-user licensing punishes inviting your auditor or board.
  • Configuration drift: your setup ages out as Salesforce changes.
  • 10 nonprofit grants discount, but you still need an admin.
GrantPipe

Built for the team running the work.

  • Up and running same day. No admin, no consultants.
  • Fund accounting is the core, not an add-on package.
  • Unlimited users on every plan. Auditor and treasurer included.
  • Opinionated workflows—no config rabbit holes.
  • Pricing scales with grants, not seats.

Where GrantPipe fits

And where it doesn't. I'd rather you pick the right tool.

01

Pick GrantPipe if…

You run grants seriously (5+ active), have restricted-fund obligations, and don't have a Salesforce admin. The team is 2–20 people who want one record everyone trusts.

02

Stick with spreadsheets if…

You're running 1–3 grants and they're all unrestricted. You don't have an audit. The current setup genuinely works. It's fine—come back when it stops.

03

Pick Salesforce if…

You already have a Salesforce admin, you live in the broader Salesforce ecosystem, and individual giving (not grants) is your dominant revenue. NPSP earns its keep there.
