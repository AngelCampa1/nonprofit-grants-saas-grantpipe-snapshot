## Pattern Diagnosis

Do more of:

- Concrete compliance mechanics: SF-425, SEFA, 2 CFR 200, allowable costs, period of performance, questioned costs, match, subrecipient monitoring, time-and-effort.
- Direct finance and Executive Director utility: one-page schedules, printouts, monthly checks, what to reconcile before a board meeting.
- List posts that name the artifact and the order of work. The audit binder post and federal program officer request post both outperformed ordinary product framing.
- Donor plus grant unification framed through reconciliation work. Posts about the CRM, GL, spreadsheets, and board packet mismatch reached beyond the follower base.
- Short "quick check" posts, especially when they point to a concrete artifact or date instead of a vague problem.
- Builder-honest product decisions when they explain data shape, not when they simply announce the product.

Do less of:

- Generic product overview posts unless paired with a paid campaign. Organic evidence for the boosted May 12 overview was only 36 impressions.
- Repeated scene openers. "Picture this" is useful but became too visible as a pattern.
- Unsourced aggregate claims about what most nonprofits do. If there is no named source, write from a product or workflow perspective instead.
- Broad vertical statistics unless the source is named and the writer can verify the exact number.
- Hashtag blocks. The better June style should usually use zero hashtags.
- Multiple CTAs in one day cluster. Links should be deliberate because the export shows reach and comments can happen without constant CTAs.

## Audience And Voice

Primary audience: Executive Directors, Development Directors, grants managers, and finance or operations leads at US nonprofits with roughly $500K to $10M annual budgets.

Voice: rigorous, humane, earned. Write from the builder perspective. Angel is a Principal SDET building the product, not a former nonprofit operator. The page can explain workflow failure modes, evidence trails, product choices, and public pricing. It cannot claim nonprofit operator experience.

## Cadence And Time Slots

Run 15 posts every day, including weekends, in Eastern Time:

06:45, 07:30, 08:15, 09:00, 09:45, 10:30, 11:15, 12:00, 12:45, 13:30, 14:30, 15:30, 16:30, 18:30, 20:30.

Slot policy:

- Put the strongest checklist, definition, or artifact post at 09:45.
- Put one source-heavy compliance or federal post between 10:30 and 12:45.
- Put one short "quick check" at 06:45 or 20:30.
- Put build-in-public posts at 16:30 or 18:30, no more than once per day.
- Put lead-magnet posts in mid-morning or mid-afternoon. Do not stack two lead magnets back to back.

## CTA Policy

- One CTA max per post.
- No more than 3 CTA posts per day.
- Default to no CTA on educational posts.
- Lead-magnet CTA: one asset only, with a plain description and URL.
- Trial CTA: only on product-fit posts or build-in-public posts. Use "Start a one-month free trial. No credit card." only when the post earns it.
- Pricing CTA: use the pricing source of truth from `docs/social/linkedin/WRITER-BRIEF.md`. Mention the limited offer only when pricing or trial framing makes it relevant. Do not name the internal promo codes in public post copy.

## Claim Safety Policy

- No fake testimonials, user counts, customer logos, "trusted by" claims, or social proof.
- Do not cite trial-user counts in public posts unless explicitly allowed later.
- Do not write "most nonprofits" or "most teams" unless a named source supports it.
- Use exact source names for public stats and regulations.
- Use hypothetical scenes only when clearly marked with "Picture this:" or second-person framing. Do not write scenes as if they happened.
- Avoid unsupported claims about proprietary private foundation, corporate, association, or state/local grant databases. The safe product claim is: Grants.gov federal opportunity search is included, and non-federal opportunities can be manually tracked or imported.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes.

## Measurement Rules

Review the export after June 7 and after June 14.

- Separate organic from sponsored rows before judging performance.
- De-duplicate rows by activity link when LinkedIn exports Total, Organic, and Sponsored versions of the same post.
- Track median organic impressions, top organic impressions, comments, clicks, and engagement rate.
- Treat comments as the best signal for topic depth. Treat clicks as the best signal for CTA fit.
- If median organic impressions fall below 40 for three straight days, keep the 15/day objective but shift more posts to short utility checks and fewer long product posts.
- If a pillar earns comments on two separate days, increase that pillar by 2 posts the following day by reducing P6 or P7 first.
