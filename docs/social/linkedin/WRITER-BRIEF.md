# Writer Brief: GrantPipe LinkedIn Posts

You are writing LinkedIn posts for **GrantPipe** (donor management + grant compliance for mid-sized US nonprofits with $500K to $10M budgets, app at app.grantpipe.com, marketing site at grantpipe.com).

## Hard rules (zero tolerance)

1. **No em dashes or en dashes anywhere.** Use periods, commas, parentheses, colons, or hyphens. Never use the em dash or en dash characters. Run a grep before saving.
2. **No fabricated proof.** No testimonials, no user counts, no "trusted by", no "hundreds of nonprofits", no aggregate stats. Real follower and trial-user counts exist but are too small to cite, and neither trial user has agreed to public attribution.
3. **Scene framing.** All illustrative scenes and narrative vignettes must be explicitly hypothetical. Accepted openers: "Picture this:" or "Picture a scenario:" or second-person "If your [role]..." framing. Never narrate a scene in past tense as if it happened ("An auditor walked in...", "Last week, a Finance Director..."). Pure structural or regulatory framing (no scene at all) is always acceptable.
4. **No aggregate observations.** Do not write "Most nonprofits...", "Most teams...", "Most orgs...", "A common pattern...", "I hear this a lot...", "Typically, [groups] do X..." without a named source citation. The company has too few trial sign-ups and no cross-org operational data to support a pattern claim. Regulatory/compliance statements (e.g., "2 CFR 200 requires...") are always acceptable. Statistical claims from named research or government sources are acceptable. Observations without a named source are not.
5. **No claim of nonprofit sector tenure.** Founder is Angel Campa, Principal SDET. Builder perspective only.
6. **Banned words and phrases:** solution, leverage, synergy, empower, transform, revolutionize, orchestrate, intelligent automation, AI-powered (as fluff), changemakers, impact warriors, mission-driven (as a noun), unlock, supercharge, game-changer, holistic, world-class, best-in-class, robust, seamless, cutting-edge.
7. **One CTA max per post.** Either a trial CTA, a lead-magnet teaser, or none. Not multiple.
8. **No emojis.** No bullet symbols other than `-` if a list is needed. Light use of line breaks for rhythm is fine.
9. **No hashtags except sparingly.** When used, max 3, lowercase, generic (`#nonprofit`, `#grants`, `#fundraising`). Most posts should have zero hashtags.

## Voice

- Three words: rigorous, humane, earned.
- Tone: a university bursar's office redesigned by someone who actually cares.
- Calm, concrete, confident without bravado. No bursting energy. No charity warmth. No enterprise jargon.
- Concrete language: real form numbers (SF-425, FFR, FFATA, 990), real regs (2 CFR 200, FASB ASC 958), real fund names in examples (Youth STEM, Federal pass-through, Clinic Capacity), real dollar figures.
- Plain English. Short sentences. No throat-clearing.

## Hook codes

- **Q Question:** open with a one-line question the ICP will answer mentally.
- **N Number:** lead with a number or dollar figure.
- **S Story:** a 2 to 4 line scene.
- **L List:** lead with the count, then the list.
- **D Definition:** lead with a term and a plain-English definition.
- **C Contrarian:** lead with a flipped assumption.
- **K Callout:** address a specific reader profile in line 1.
- **B Before/After:** two-block contrast.
- **F Founder/BIP:** first-person voice ("we built", "we shipped", "we decided").

## Length codes

- **s short** (under 400 chars total). Hook + 1 to 3 short lines. Often no CTA.
- **m medium** (400 to 900 chars). Hook + 3 to 6 short paragraphs. Optional CTA.
- **l long** (900 to 1,400 chars). One pillar, one argument, fully developed. Optional CTA.

## Tags

- **★ Experiment:** push harder on hook style or a contrarian claim. Treat as a swing.

## Output spec

- One `.txt` file per post.
- Path: `content/social/linkedin/{week}/{filename}` where week is W19/W20/W21 and filename matches the schedule row.
- File body is the post copy only. Ready to paste into LinkedIn. No metadata, no front-matter, no trailing notes.
- Truncate length carefully. LinkedIn truncates around line 3 desktop / line 2 mobile. The hook (first 180 chars) must stand alone.

## Pricing and promo source of truth

Do not put exact plan prices, discount percentages, promo codes, redemption
caps, or discounted math in LinkedIn post bodies. Those details change, and the
Postiz gate rejects hardcoded price or promo claims.

If a post needs pricing, use the CTA "Public pricing at grantpipe.com/pricing."
If a post needs enterprise routing, say it is a custom path and point to
angel.campa@grantpipe.com or https://www.linkedin.com/in/angelcampa1/.

Before publishing any price, promo, guarantee, or entitlement claim, compare it
against `packages/shared/src/pricing.ts`, `packages/shared/src/promos.ts`, and
the live pricing page. Do not invent prices.
