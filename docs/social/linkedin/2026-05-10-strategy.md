# GrantPipe LinkedIn Strategy

_Effective 2026-05-11. Owner: Angel Campa. Re-evaluate 2026-06-08._

## Audience

As of 2026-05-10 the page is newly launched: a small follower base, a short posting history, and minimal engagement so far.

Decision makers and operators at US-based grant-funded nonprofits with $500K to $10M annual budgets. The page is talking to three roles in one feed:

- **Executive Director** (final buyer). Cares about audit posture, board confidence, and not buying another vendor that becomes a project.
- **Development Director** (champion). Cares about grant deadlines, donor context, and reporting evidence.
- **Finance and Operations lead** (technical influencer). Cares about restricted balances, releases, spend-down, audit evidence, and how the system handles FASB ASC 958.

Auditors and program officers read the page too. We do not write to them, but we do not write anything that would embarrass us in front of them.

**Anti-personas (do not write to):** sub-$250K grassroots orgs, large enterprise orgs already running Blackbaud or NetSuite with admin teams, fundraising-only teams without grant compliance load, buyers who want a consultant-led implementation.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)

- **Three words: rigorous, humane, earned.** Tone of a university bursar's office redesigned by someone who actually cares. Confidence without bravado.
- **Builder perspective.** Angel is a Principal SDET, not a former nonprofit operator. We do not claim sector tenure. We can write about workflow failure modes, evidence chains, and product decisions because we built and tested them.
- **No fabricated proof.** No testimonials, no user counts, no "trusted by hundreds" lines, no aggregate stats. Trial users exist today; neither has agreed to public attribution.
- **No em dashes.** Use periods, commas, parentheses, or colons. Run a grep before publishing.
- **Banned phrases:** solution, leverage, synergy, empower, transform, revolutionize, orchestrate, intelligent automation, AI-powered (as a fluff modifier), changemakers, impact warriors, mission-driven (as a noun), unlock, supercharge, game-changer.
- **Concrete language:** real fund names (Youth STEM, Federal pass-through, Clinic Capacity), real dollar figures, real form numbers (SF-425, FFR), real regulations (2 CFR 200, FASB ASC 958).
- **One CTA per post, max.** Trial signup, lead magnet, blog post, or none. Not all three.

## Cadence

15 posts per weekday, 10 posts per weekend day. Three weeks. Total = 285 posts.

**Weekday slots (15, all ET):** 06:30, 07:30, 08:00, 08:45, 09:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 14:30, 15:30, 16:30, 17:30, 19:00, 20:30.

**Weekend slots (10, all ET):** 08:00, 09:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 15:00, 17:00, 19:00, 20:30.

Slot rationale: spread across the day to test response curves on a young page. Concentrate "big rock" posts (long-form, founder voice, lead-magnet teasers) in the hypothesized engagement bands (Tue/Thu 09:30 to 12:30, Wed 11:30 to 13:30) and use the off-peak slots for short hooks, definitions, and experiments.

**Volume risk acknowledged.** 15/day is far above LinkedIn norms (1 to 2 per day for a company page). Reach throttling is plausible. Trigger: if median impressions per post in week 1 fall more than 40% versus the first 5 posts on the page, cut to 5/day weekday and 3/day weekend in week 2 and recover. Strategy is not the volume; the volume is a test.

## Length distribution

- ~30% short (under 400 chars): hook + 1 line + CTA or no CTA. Best for off-peak slots.
- ~50% medium (400 to 900 chars): hook + 3 to 6 short paragraphs + optional CTA.
- ~20% long (900 to 1,400 chars): the deep cut. One pillar, one argument. Reserve for prime slots.

## Image and media notes

We do not generate images in this run. Each post specifies in a comment whether an image asset would help (e.g., screenshot of a fund ledger row, a redacted SF-425, a glossary card). Text-only is fine. When the user wants to add visuals later, the SCHEDULE.md tag tells them which posts deserve one.

## Hook bank

Reusable templates. Pull from `HOOK-BANK.md`. Rotate so the same hook style does not appear twice in a 24-hour window.

## Engagement plan

- Reply to every comment within 60 minutes during business hours (09:00 to 17:00 ET). Outside that window, reply within 12 hours.
- Tag no one without consent. No DMs from posts.
- Repost is not a strategy. We do not repost competitors and we do not auto-repost our own.
- If a post catches a thread (5+ comments), follow up in 24 hours with a related post. Treat the thread as a research signal.

## Measurement and review

Weekly review every Monday 09:00 ET, starting 2026-05-19. Pull from LinkedIn analytics:

- Impressions per post (median, top decile)
- Engagement rate per post (reactions + comments + reposts / impressions)
- Click-through rate on lead-magnet and trial CTAs
- Follower delta
- Top 5 posts of the week, bottom 5 posts of the week

Decision rules at week 4:

- Top quartile by engagement rate: keep slot, keep pillar, keep hook style.
- Bottom quartile: retire slot, retire hook style, replace with the pattern from the top quartile.
- Pillar share: rebalance toward whichever pillar is over-indexing on engagement, but no single pillar exceeds 30% share.
- Volume: hold at 15/10 only if median impressions held within 30% of the first 5 baseline. Otherwise scale down.

## Risks called out

- **Reach throttling at 15/day.** Mitigation: weekly volume gate.
- **Originality drift across 285 posts.** Mitigation: hook-bank rotation, pillar mix, source-asset variety (115 lead magnets, 60+ comparisons, glossary, vertical pages).
- **Comment management load.** 60-minute reply target plus 285 posts is a real time commitment. If unsustainable, fold the engagement plan to 4 hours total (09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00 ET) and accept the reach trade-off.
- **No proof points.** Until trial users opt into attribution, every post must hold up on logic alone, not testimony.
