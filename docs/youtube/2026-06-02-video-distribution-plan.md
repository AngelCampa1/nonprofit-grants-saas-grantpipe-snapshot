## Core strategic move

Product videos and SEO videos are **not** separate buckets. The operations-pillar topics (track restricted funds, track grant spending, manage multiple grants, generate reports) are exactly where GrantPipe is the answer. Film those as **concept-then-demo** so one video serves both goals: it ranks on a search term *and* shows the product. The remaining product videos (onboarding, allocations, compliance deadlines, AI intake) are **short screen-records** for help + email only.

### Non-negotiable: when we show the product, we show the *real product*

Every "demo" or "screen-record" segment in section A is **the actual running GrantPipe app** — real screens, real navigation, real data flowing through the real UI. It is never a mockup, a Figma frame, a slide that looks like the app, a CSS-drawn placeholder, or a "coming soon" stand-in. If a feature isn't built or isn't demo-ready, we don't fake it — we cut that beat or hold the video until the feature is real. This is compliance software sold to risk-averse buyers; a staged screenshot that doesn't match what they get after signup destroys exactly the trust these videos exist to build. Use a real (seeded but realistic) demo org, not the literal logo-placeholder or lorem data.

## Distribution map

The 8 product videos are not discovered on their own. Each has a deliberate home:

### In-app Help section (permanent home)
All 8 product videos embed next to their matching docs page (see "Help page" column). This is where a paying or trialing user lands when stuck — the highest-intent context. Organize Help by domain so the videos slot into the structure rather than living in a generic "videos" dump.

### Email (sequenced to the user journey)
Onboarding/nurture sequence, one product video per email, ordered the way a new user actually works:

1. Welcome → **P1 (set up & import)**
2. First grant → **P2 (add grant + allocate)**
3. Day-to-day → **P4 (track spending)** then **P5 (manage multiple grants)**
4. Funds done right → **P3 (restricted funds)**
5. Stay compliant → **P6 (deadlines + evidence)**
6. Show your funders → **P7 (reports)**
7. Power feature → **P8 (AI intake)** as a "did you know" re-engagement email

Lead-magnet nurture emails (single audit, templates) keep linking the **SEO** videos, not the product ones.

### Social (LinkedIn — existing heavy cadence)
- **Feature spotlights:** P8 (AI intake) and P5 (manage multiple grants) are the strongest "did you know GrantPipe can…" hooks. P3/P4/P7 work as problem→solution posts.
- **Authority content:** the 7 SEO videos posted as teaching content, no hard pitch.
- Reuse: each product video cut into a 30–60s vertical clip for the LinkedIn feed, full version linked.

### YouTube
- Keep product videos **Public** (not Unlisted) so they *can* surface, but don't budget on organic pull — the concept-then-demo overlap (P3/P4/P5/P7) is the only product content expected to earn search traffic.
- The 7 SEO videos carry discovery and feed everything else.

## Sequencing

1. **P1, P2** first — onboarding videos unblock the email sequence and Help section immediately.
2. **S1 (fund accounting), S4 (uniform guidance)** — highest-volume authority, keep discovery growing.
3. **P3, P4, P5, P7** — the concept-then-demo four; each doubles as SEO + Help + email + social.
4. **P6, P8** — round out the product set.
5. **S2 (software comparison)** — film once the four overlap videos are live, so the comparison has supporting depth to link to.
6. **S3, S5, S6, S7** — fill remaining SEO slots.

## Conventions (unchanged from the existing strategy doc)

- Soft GrantPipe plug in the outro; teach first, never a hard "buy now."
- Lead magnets are **email-gated** — say "we'll send it to your inbox," never "no email."
- Use the real GrantPipe logo mark (`production/assets/grantpipe-mark.svg`).
- Founder is a **builder**, not a former grants officer — no fabricated sector experience.
