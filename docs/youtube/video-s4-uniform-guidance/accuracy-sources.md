## Errors to AVOID (common in online Uniform Guidance explainers)
- Quoting the OLD numbers (10% de minimis, $5k equipment, $25k subaward cap, $750k single audit). These are pre-Oct-1-2024 and must appear ONLY as "it used to be…".
- Calling the de minimis rate a flat 15% — it is a ceiling ("up to 15%").
- Saying the single audit is "per grant" — it is organization-wide.
- Saying the single audit triggers on money *received* or *awarded* — the trigger is federal funds **expended** in the fiscal year.
- Misstating the effective date as a single calendar date for everyone — awards key to Oct 1, 2024 issue date; the single audit keys to the recipient's fiscal-year start.
- Claiming Uniform Guidance applies to all nonprofit money — it governs **federal** awards only (and money passed through from federal sources).
