## Saturated vs. missing

- **Over-covered:** generic deadline/pipeline trackers; vendor "ditch your spreadsheet" pitches; pure-accounting QuickBooks expense tracking; unrelated Excel trackers.
- **The gap to own — compliance-aware tracking.** No video covers restricted vs. unrestricted funds, spend-against-budget per line, period-of-performance / reporting-deadline logic, or *why a spreadsheet breaks* (no audit trail, version chaos, no allocation across funds). The literal #1 has only ~760 views — rankings are weak and beatable.

## Length & title patterns

- **Sweet spot: 6–9 min.** (Our target: ~10 min, justified by the deeper compliance layer; keep pacing tight.)
- Titles: noun-first, platform-named ("Google Sheets for…"), benefit framing ("Effortless," "Made Easy"). "Free / Template" recurs and drives clicks.

## How we win

1. **Hook on the failure mode, not the build** — "Most grant trackers fail an audit — here's the column nonprofits forget."
2. **Actually ship the free template** (Sheets + Excel) and say so in title/first 15s. Most top videos show a sheet but bury/paywall the download. A real free download = SEO + trust win.
3. **Include the compliance layer competitors skip:** restricted vs unrestricted, budget-vs-actual per grant, reporting-due-date tracking, allocation across funds.
4. **Cut generalist Excel formula tutorials and Gantt/dashboard wizardry** — wrong, risk-averse, non-technical audience.
5. **End with the honest scale limit, not a hard pitch** — show *when* the spreadsheet breaks; position GrantPipe as the next step. Avoid the vendor "ditch your spreadsheet" tone.

**Free-template expectation: HIGH.** Deliver a genuinely free, instantly usable template (this becomes the lead magnet the video links to).
