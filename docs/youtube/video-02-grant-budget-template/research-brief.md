## Saturated vs. missing

- **Over-covered:** generic "what funders look for" explainers; tips-list videos with no live build; funder-portal-specific template walk-throughs (Horizon, RISCA, EPA, CDFA — all single-program, useless to a general searcher); short faceless AI explainers defining one term each.
- **The gap to own — a real template walkthrough that is also compliance-aware.** No single video does all three: (1) shows an actual reusable template being filled line by line, (2) ships it as a free download, AND (3) explains the federal 2 CFR 200 rigor — period of performance, allowable vs. unallowable costs, indirect cost rate / de minimis 15%, cost-share/match, and the budget narrative. The compliance terms exist only as scattered 3-minute shorts (The Nonprofit Digest) or buried inside hour-long govt webinars no busy ED will watch. Nobody packages it as one tight, downloadable, step-by-step template tutorial. The #1 result (Grant Chatter, 14K) has no live template and no deep compliance layer — beatable on substance.

## Length & title patterns

- **Sweet spot: 9–12 min.** The two strongest evergreen performers sit at 9:51 and 12:04; sub-3-min explainers rank but don't satisfy a "template walkthrough" intent. Hour-long webinars rank poorly per-view. Our target ~10–12 min is well justified by the added compliance + narrative layer — keep pacing tight.
- Titles: "How to Write/Create a Grant Budget" and "Grant Budget Template" dominate; "Step by Step," "Tutorial," and "Free Template" recur and drive clicks. Lead with the exact keyword + "(Step by Step)" as planned.

## How we win

1. **Lead with the live template fill, not theory** — title and first 15s promise "fill out a real grant budget template, line by line." Most top videos talk _about_ budgets; almost none build one on screen.
2. **Actually ship the free template (Excel + Sheets) and say so up front.** Only Vena (vendor) and Benjamin Dean (PDF tips) offer downloads; neither pairs a genuinely free, reusable grant budget template with a full walkthrough. This is the lead magnet the video links to.
3. **Own the compliance layer competitors fragment:** period of performance, allowable vs. unallowable costs, indirect cost rate / de minimis 15%, cost-share/match, and a budget narrative. Consolidate into one video what The Nonprofit Digest splits across ten 3-minute shorts.
4. **Anchor to federal rigor (2 CFR 200) but stay plain-spoken** — the only compliance-deep results are 27–60 min academic/govt lectures. A tight, self-evident 10-min version beats them on watch-through and intent match.
5. **Close on the limits of a spreadsheet template, not a hard pitch** — show where manual budget-vs-actual and cross-fund allocation break, then position GrantPipe as the next step. Avoid the vendor "ditch your spreadsheet" tone.

**Free-template expectation: HIGH.** "Template" is the head term and carries commercial intent ($7.94 CPC). Deliver a genuinely free, instantly usable grant budget template (Excel + Sheets) with the line items and a budget-narrative tab pre-built — this is the conversion engine.
