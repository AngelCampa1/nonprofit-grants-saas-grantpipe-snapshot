# GrantPipe — Hormozi Analytical Scaffolding

**Framework lock (verified):** Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice). Maximize the numerator, minimize the denominator. Grand Slam Offer = problem → solution list → trim & stack → price → guarantee → scarcity → urgency → bonus → name. Naming = M-A-G-I-C. Sources at the bottom.

A note that governs everything below: the buyer truth from the brief is the load-bearing insight. **Most $500K–$10M orgs are NOT subject to a single audit** (it triggers at $1M federal awards _expended_). So the Dream Outcome that the wallet is already open for is **fundraising + board/cash confidence**, and compliance is the **differentiator/moat**, not the lead. The current offer leaks the most value by leading with the moat instead of the pull. Hold that thought — it reappears as the single biggest constraint at the end.

## 1. The Value Equation — variable by variable

### NUMERATOR

### 1A. Dream Outcome

_What transformation is the buyer actually paying for?_

The real dream is **personal, not operational**: "I am never the person caught not knowing where a dollar went." The ED/DD buys the _feeling of being unimpeachable_ — calm when the auditor calls, fast when the board asks, in control when a deadline looms. Operationally that resolves to one sentence: **the donor-to-finance loop closes itself, no reconciliation, no consultant.**

**Current levers (real, shipping):**

- The ONE-schema moat — donor → grant → restricted fund → GL → report with zero cross-system reconciliation. This is the genuine dream and nobody else credibly sells it at this price.
- "No consultants required" — directly kills the Salesforce/Blackbaud scar tissue.
- Ask-Your-Ledger — "ask where a dollar went in English, get a grounded answer you confirm." This IS the board-meeting fear, solved.
- Auditor/funder read-only portal + restriction evidence packages — the "auditor calls" fear, solved.
- Unlimited users on every plan — removes the "who do I have to leave out" tax.

**Where it's LEAKING:**

- **Leads with the moat, not the pull.** The strongest dream for the _wallet that is already open_ is fundraising/board confidence; the site likely frames GrantPipe primarily as compliance software, which is the differentiator a buyer rationalizes _after_ they want it. The dream outcome is under-sold for the 80% of the market not facing a single audit.
- **The dream is described as features, not as a felt end-state.** "Restricted-fund rollforward" is a capability; "you'll never again rebuild your fund balances by hand the night before a board meeting" is the dream. Most surfaces probably state the former.
- **No quantified dream.** Hormozi wants the outcome _sized_. There's no "close your books in X days instead of Y," no "answer the board in 30 seconds." The dream is qualitative where it could be a number.
- **QuickBooks-read-only is positioned as a limitation, not a dream.** "We don't replace QuickBooks, we close the loop _around_ it" is a dream (keep your accountant happy, lose the reconciliation). Likely under-framed.

### 1B. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement

_Does the buyer believe it will work — for THEM?_

This is the buyer's #1 silent objection. They were _already burned once_. Risk-averse, non-technical-but-sophisticated. Perceived likelihood is the variable GrantPipe should fight hardest on, because the avatar's prior is "software like this fails me."

**Current levers (real):**

- 1-month free trial, no card → try-before-believe.
- 30-day money-back → de-risks the purchase.
- Founder-led guided setup on Audit-Ready/Enterprise → "a human makes sure it works."
- AI is **always human-confirmed** ("you check each result") → this is a _huge_ likelihood lever for a risk-averse buyer; it removes the "AI will hallucinate my audit" fear.
- Built on the real schema double-entry GL → "the numbers are exact" (matches the brand canon "trust through precision").

**Where it's LEAKING — this is the biggest leak in the whole equation:**

- **No proof, and proof is the likelihood multiplier.** Founder is a solo Principal SDET; the brief forbids fabricating testimonials/user counts (correctly). But that means perceived likelihood is currently carried _entirely by the trial and guarantee_ — there's no third-party belief transfer. This is the structural weak point.
- **The human-confirm story is a likelihood goldmine that's probably buried.** "Our AI never acts alone — you approve every extracted award term and every ledger answer" should be a headline trust pillar, not a footnote. For a burned, risk-averse auditor-fearing buyer, _human-in-the-loop_ is more persuasive than accuracy claims.
- **Setup likelihood is unproven for the self-serve tiers.** Guided setup exists only on Audit-Ready/Enterprise. The Starter/Growth buyer — the volume of the market — has no visible "you will get this working" assurance beyond the trial. That's where belief collapses.
- **No "show your work" mechanism.** GrantPipe's whole moat is _correctness_. Likelihood would jump if the product visibly proved its own math (e.g., "every fund balance traces to its journal entries, click to see the trail"). The product may do this; the _offer_ doesn't claim it.
- **The displacement event (Foundant sunsetting GrantHub Jan 31 2026) is unused as proof-of-motion.** "Orgs are migrating off sunset tools right now" is social proof of category movement that requires no testimonials.

### DENOMINATOR

### 1C. Time Delay

_How long until the buyer feels the dream?_

For this avatar the relevant clock is **time-to-first-confidence**, not time-to-full-migration. The first "oh, I can breathe" moment must come in the trial.

**Current levers (real):**

- AI Award Document Intake → upload an award letter, terms extract in minutes vs. manual transcription. Strong first-value lever.
- QuickBooks read-only sync → pulls real numbers in without manual GL rebuild.
- Grants.gov search built in → value before any data entry.
- Onboarding wizard + sample-data engine (from memory: goal-branched wizard, "First Light" aha banner) → engineered specifically to compress time-to-aha.

**Where it's LEAKING:**

- **The "empty CRM" cold start.** Donor management's value is proportional to data in the system; a fresh org sees an empty pipeline. Sample data helps the _demo_ but the _real_ dream needs _their_ data, which is a migration delay. The offer doesn't promise a time-boxed "we'll have your last 12 months loaded by day X."
- **No named, time-bound first win.** Hormozi wants "results in [interval]." There's no "your first restricted-fund rollforward in your first session" or "board-ready report in 10 minutes." The fast wins exist in the product but aren't _promised as a timeline_ in the offer.
- **Compliance value is inherently slow-clock** (you feel it at audit/board/deadline time, which may be months out). This _deepens_ the argument to lead with the fast-clock dream (fundraising/cash) and let compliance be the thing they're glad they have when the slow clock strikes.
- **Migration friction is the hidden time tax.** Importing from spreadsheets/Blackbaud/DonorPerfect is the real delay. If there's no white-glove import on self-serve tiers, time-to-value silently balloons for exactly the buyer who was already burned by long implementations.

### 1D. Effort & Sacrifice

_What work/inconvenience/cost must the buyer endure?_

The avatar is time-poor and non-technical. Every unit of effort is taxed double. This is where the "consultant is the competitor" principle lives — the dream is _removing_ the consultant's effort.

**Current levers (real):**

- "No consultants required" — the core effort-killer and the central differentiator vs. Blackbaud/Salesforce.
- ONE schema → no cross-system reconciliation = the single largest sacrifice GrantPipe removes (manual donor-to-finance tie-out is the avatar's recurring agony).
- AI extraction + Ask-Your-Ledger → removes manual transcription and report-building labor.
- Unlimited users → no "ration the seats" effort/cost.
- QuickBooks read-only → they don't have to rip out their accountant's system (low switching sacrifice).
- 990 export templates, evidence packages → removes manual document assembly.

**Where it's LEAKING:**

- **Setup effort falls on the buyer for self-serve tiers.** The biggest sacrifice — getting data in, configuring funds/restrictions — is exactly where guided setup is gated to the top tiers. The volume buyer carries the heaviest effort. This is a leak _and_ an upsell lever, depending on framing.
- **The effort-removal isn't dollarized.** Hormozi: quantify the sacrifice removed. "Replaces the $X/yr consultant + the 3 systems you reconcile by hand" makes the effort reduction _visible_. Currently it's claimed qualitatively.
- **Learning-curve sacrifice is real and unacknowledged.** A double-entry GL + restricted-fund accounting is genuinely sophisticated. The non-technical buyer fears "I won't understand it." The offer should explicitly absorb that sacrifice (templates, defaults, "we set up the standard fund structure for you"), not assume the brand's "self-evident UI" principle covers it.
- **Decision effort itself.** Four tiers + caps + annual/monthly + promo is cognitive load at the buy moment. The _offer_ (not just the pricing page) should make the choice feel obvious — "most $500K–$10M orgs start on Growth" — to remove selection sacrifice.

## 2. Why GrantPipe should charge PREMIUM — the divergent-thinking / price-to-value-discrepancy argument

**The trap to refuse:** GrantPipe sits visually next to Instrumentl ($299/$499/$899) and Bloomerang/DonorPerfect/Little Green Light at similar monthly numbers. The lazy move is to price _as a CRM_ — i.e., benchmark against single-category tools and shave a few dollars to look competitive. That is the convergent error. It anchors GrantPipe to the wrong category and caps its price at "another donor database."

**The divergent reframe (this is the whole argument):** GrantPipe is not in the CRM category. It is the **only product that collapses an entire stack** the avatar otherwise assembles from _multiple_ vendors plus _human labor_:

- A donor CRM (Bloomerang/DonorPerfect/Neon ≈ $100–500/mo)
- _plus_ a grant-management tool (Instrumentl/AmpliFund/Submittable ≈ $300–900/mo)
- _plus_ fund accounting (Aplos/MIP/Sage Intacct Nonprofit — Intacct/MIP run into the many-thousands/yr, quote-only)
- _plus_ the **reconciliation labor between all three**, which today is a person's time or a **consultant** (Blackbaud/Salesforce implementations run tens of thousands).

Priced against that **assembled alternative**, GrantPipe's Audit-Ready at $199/mo ($1,908/yr when billed annually) is a fraction of the stack it replaces, and it deletes the one thing none of those tools delete: **the reconciliation seam between fundraising and finance.** That seam is the value. Nobody else sells it.

**Why premium is the _correct_ strategic price, not just a defensible one:**

1. **Price-to-value discrepancy is enormous and uniquely ownable.** The value (one auditable source of truth from donor to GL) is high and the marginal substitute (stitch 3 tools + pay a consultant) is _much_ more expensive and effortful. Hormozi's rule: price into that gap, not against the cheapest single-category lookalike.
2. **The avatar is risk-averse, not price-sensitive.** Their fear is personal accountability, not budget. Cheap _signals risk_ to a burned buyer ("if it's $99 it'll fail me like the last cheap thing"). A premium price is a **trust signal** here — it says "this is the serious system, built for the moment the auditor calls." Underpricing actively lowers Perceived Likelihood.
3. **Premium funds the dream.** Founder-led guided setup, white-glove migration, and human-confirmed AI are _expensive to deliver_ and are exactly the levers that raise Perceived Likelihood and cut Effort. A race-to-the-bottom price makes those impossible, which collapses the value equation. Premium price → can afford the levers → higher value → justifies the price. Virtuous loop.
4. **Compliance is a fear-priced category.** People pay more to avoid a catastrophic downside than to gain an equivalent upside. The compliance moat lets GrantPipe price on _downside-avoidance_ (audit findings, clawbacks, board embarrassment), which carries a premium that pure fundraising tools can't access.
5. **Unlimited users is a premium-enabler, not a giveaway.** Per-seat competitors (Salesforce $60–100/user/mo) _look_ cheaper at one seat and balloon at ten. GrantPipe's flat premium price _beats them at the org level_ while reading as "simple and generous." Use it as a premium justifier, not a discount.

**Conclusion for later agents:** never benchmark the headline price against a single-category CRM. Always benchmark against **the assembled stack + the consultant + the labor**. The number to beat is "$X,000/yr in tools and reconciliation time," not "$299/mo Instrumentl." The promo (80% off first year to July 3, 2026) is a _founding-cohort entry ramp_, not the real price — protect the list price as the anchor.

## 3. Grand Slam Offer — construction checklist (scaffold for later agents to fill)

This is the to-do scaffold. Each step lists what it is, GrantPipe-specific seeds already on hand, and the `[ ]` work the later agents must complete.

### Step 1 — Problem (enumerate every problem in the avatar's path)

_Hormozi: list every problem the prospect faces before, during, and after the dream. Each becomes a solution + a potential bonus._

- [ ] Write the exhaustive problem list for the ED/DD avatar across the donor-to-finance loop. Seeds: "I reconcile fundraising to finance by hand"; "I rebuild fund balances before every board meeting"; "I can't answer where a restricted dollar went without digging"; "auditor asks for evidence and I assemble it manually"; "a grant deadline / spend-down slips and I find out late"; "my last system needed a consultant and still failed me"; "I'm rationing seats"; "I dread the 990"; "QuickBooks holds the money but not the donor/grant context."
- [ ] Tag each problem by avatar (ED/DD vs. Finance/Ops Director vs. multi-client consultant).
- [ ] Mark which are **fast-clock** (fundraising/board/cash — lead with these) vs. **slow-clock** (audit/compliance — differentiate with these).

### Step 2 — Solution list (turn each problem into the solution GrantPipe delivers)

_Hormozi: invert every problem into a solution statement._

- [ ] Map each Step-1 problem to its shipping GrantPipe capability (one schema, Ask-Your-Ledger, restriction lifecycle + rollforward, compliance calendar + alerts, AI Award Intake w/ human confirm, auditor portal, evidence packages, QBO read-only sync, 990 templates, unlimited users). Stay inside verified capabilities — do not invent.

### Step 3 — Trim & Stack

_Hormozi: cut low-value/high-cost solutions; keep high-value ones; stack them so the perceived value dwarfs the price._

- [ ] **Trim:** flag anything that adds cognitive load without moving the dream (e.g., over-exposing every tier feature at the buy moment).
- [ ] **Stack:** assemble the kept solutions into one stacked offer where the summed perceived value visibly exceeds the price. The stack's spine should be the **fast-clock dream first** (fundraising/board confidence), then the compliance moat as the "and you're covered when it counts" capstone.
- [ ] Produce the stacked value-vs-price visual scaffold (replaces-this-stack math from §2).

### Step 4 — Price

_Set the premium anchor; the §2 argument is the justification._

- [ ] Confirm live truth: Starter $329 ($269 annual), Growth $539 ($449), Audit-Ready $1,079 ($899), Enterprise custom. Caps: 10/30/100/unlimited active grants. Unlimited users all tiers.
- [ ] Anchor list price against the **assembled-stack cost**, never the single-category CRM.
- [ ] Position promo (80% off first year, ends July 3 2026) as founding-cohort ramp; keep list price as the visible anchor with renewal clarity.
- [ ] Pick the "most orgs start here" default tier to kill selection effort (likely Growth).

### Step 5 — Guarantee (stack and name them; this is GrantPipe's highest-leverage missing piece)

_Hormozi guarantee types: unconditional, conditional, anti-guarantee, implied._

- [ ] **Unconditional** — already have 30-day money-back; name it.
- [ ] **Conditional / outcome** — draft a results guarantee that's true and deliverable for a solo founder (e.g., a setup/first-value or accuracy-traceability guarantee tied to the human-confirmed, double-entry correctness). Must be honest — no fabricated outcomes.
- [ ] **Implied** — "AI never acts alone — you confirm every result" is an _implied accuracy guarantee_; surface it as a trust pillar.
- [ ] **Anti-guarantee** — consider a "this isn't for you if…" qualifier (e.g., "if you want software you never have to look at, this isn't it — every number here is one you can stand behind").
- [ ] Stack and **name** the guarantee bundle.

### Step 6 — Scarcity (limit quantity, honestly)

- [ ] Use the genuine, non-deceptive scarcity: **founding cohort** (finite spots at the founding price) and **founder-led guided setup capacity** (a solo founder can only onboard so many guided setups at once — real and true). Do not manufacture fake limits.

### Step 7 — Urgency (limit time, honestly)

- [ ] Anchor on the real deadline: **founding-cohort promo ends July 3, 2026** — price reverts to full after. (Engine already enforces this; promos.ts.)
- [ ] Optional honest urgency: the **Foundant/GrantHub sunset (Jan 31, 2026)** as a "migrate now" moment for displaced orgs.

### Step 8 — Bonuses (each its own mini-offer with a named value tag)

_Hormozi: bonuses solve adjacent problems; each gets a name and a dollar value._

- [ ] Build bonuses from adjacent problems, drawn ONLY from real capabilities/assets. Candidate seeds: the founder-led guided setup (value-tag it), restriction-evidence-package templates, 990 export templates, compliance-calendar asset, a "migrate off your spreadsheet/legacy tool" import assist, the existing lead-magnet library (grant-compliance checklist, etc.). Each needs a name + honest value tag.

### Step 9 — Name (M-A-G-I-C)

_Magnetic reason-why · Avatar · Goal · Interval · Container word._

- [ ] Draft 3–5 names. Scaffold per slot:

## 4. The single biggest constraint on the offer today

**Perceived Likelihood of Achievement — specifically, the proof vacuum for a burned, risk-averse buyer — is the binding constraint.**

Everything else in the equation is already strong: the Dream Outcome is real and uniquely ownable (the one-schema moat), Time Delay has genuine fast-value levers (AI intake, native accounting records, Grants.gov), and Effort & Sacrifice is the core differentiator ("no consultants, no reconciliation"). The numerator term that's capped is **belief**. The avatar's prior is "software like this has failed me before," the founder is solo and (correctly) cannot fabricate testimonials/user counts, and guided-setup assurance is gated to the top tiers — so the _volume_ buyer (Starter/Growth, the 80% not facing a single audit) is asked to believe on the strength of a trial and a refund alone.

Because Value is multiplicative, a weak Perceived Likelihood throttles the entire equation no matter how strong the dream is. **Raising honest, non-fabricated proof of likelihood is the highest-leverage move available**, and the good news is the levers already exist and are just under-deployed:

- Lead the trust story with **human-confirmed AI** ("it never acts alone — you approve every result").
- Make the product **prove its own correctness** (traceable fund balances → journal entries), turning the moat into visible belief.
- Add an honest **outcome/setup guarantee** and extend setup assurance below the top tiers.
- Use **category-motion proof** (Foundant sunset; migration moment) instead of testimonials.
- Reframe the entire offer to **lead with the fast-clock dream** (fundraising/board confidence the wallet is already open for) and let compliance be the moat — which also shortens perceived Time Delay and sidesteps the "I'm not subject to a single audit so why am I here" bounce.

If later agents fix one thing, fix belief. The offer doesn't need a bigger dream — it needs the buyer to believe the dream will happen _for them_.
