## 1. The core problem (restated)

The Executive Director and the Finance Director are not afraid of price. They are afraid of **being the one who can't answer the question** — when the auditor calls, when the board asks where a dollar went, when a deadline slips. Today that answer lives in three systems that never agree (a donor CRM, QuickBooks, and a pile of spreadsheets), and stitching them together is a weekend of manual reconciliation — or a consultant on retainer. The fear is personal accountability with a name on it.

**The promise the offer must deliver:** one record from donor → grant → restricted fund → ledger → report, so the answer is already there. No reconciliation. No consultant. You check every AI result, so the numbers are ones you can stand behind in front of your board.
