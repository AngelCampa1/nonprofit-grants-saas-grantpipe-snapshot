# GrantPipe Guarantee Suite — "The Stand-Behind-It Stack"

**Strategic frame:** For a risk-averse, already-burned buyer, **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement is the binding constraint** (per the Hormozi engine). The whole field has _zero_ published guarantees — Instrumentl, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Neon, Aplos, Submittable, AmpliFund, Sage, Blackbaud, Salesforce, Candid. Not one. So a _named, stacked_ guarantee suite is free, durable differentiation no rival can copy this quarter.

**The honesty line that governs everything:** GrantPipe controls its software, its setup help, and the math on confirmed data. It does **not** control the customer's bookkeeping, their underlying records, or an auditor's judgment. **Every guarantee below promises only what GrantPipe controls.** No guarantee promises a passed audit, a won grant, or an avoided clawback. That restraint is what makes the suite defensible for a solo founder — and, paradoxically, what makes a burned buyer believe it.

## Guarantee 0 — The Free Look _(implied / risk-reversal entry)_

**Terms (final voice):**

> Try the whole thing free for one month. No credit card. See your own grants live before you decide.

**What it actually is:** the existing 1-month free trial, no card — renamed so it reads as a _guarantee of low commitment_, not a feature. A visible "you risk nothing to look" gate.

**Avatar fear it kills:**

- **The ED who got burned by a year-long Salesforce rollout** ("To really set up well an outside consultant is needed which is very expensive"). The Free Look says: _you find out it works before money or a card ever enters the picture._ It directly answers "what if it doesn't stick?" — the silent #1 objection of a buyer with scar tissue.

**Why it's honest & safe:** it costs the founder nothing but compute; there's no refund mechanics to honor because nothing was paid.

## Guarantee 1 — The First-Month, No-Risk Guarantee _(unconditional)_

**Terms (final voice):**

> Pay for your first month and still not sure? Tell us within 30 days and we refund every dollar. No forms. No "why." No catch.

**Exact mechanics:**

- **Window:** 30 days from the first paid charge (the charge that follows the free trial).
- **Condition:** none. Any reason or no reason.
- **Remedy:** 100% refund of the first paid period. Annual plans refunded in full within the 30-day window.
- **What it does _not_ cover:** it's a money-back promise, not a usage credit — once refunded, the account closes (data export still available under Guarantee 4).

**Avatar fear it kills:**

- **All three avatars, "cheap signals risk" reversal.** A real paid product ($49-$199/mo) on a solo-founder product still needs a no-questions refund so the low-friction price does not read as low-trust. This converts the price from a worry into a confidence signal: _we charge real money and we'll hand it all back._
- It's the **only money-back guarantee in the entire category** — lean on that fact.

**Why it's honest & safe:** GrantPipe fully controls refunds. A 30-day unconditional window is standard, cheap to honor, and the trial already filters out most mismatches before a dollar is charged.

## Guarantee 2 — The Two-Week Setup Guarantee _(conditional / outcome — the highest-leverage piece)_

This is the guarantee that attacks the real adoption cliff: _the year-long implementation trauma._ It promises a **process outcome GrantPipe controls** (data in, first report out), never an audit outcome it doesn't.

**Terms (final voice):**

> Start a guided setup and within 14 days you'll have your real data in GrantPipe and your first compliance report in hand — or the founder keeps working with you, free, until you do.

**Exact mechanics:**

- **Who qualifies:** customers who book a guided setup (included on Audit-Ready and Enterprise; offered as the named onboarding path elsewhere). The clock starts at the scheduled setup session.
- **What "done" means — three controllable checkpoints:**
  1. Your existing donor/grant/fund data is imported and reconciled to a preview you approve.
  2. At least one restricted fund is linked to its grant and its restriction terms.
  3. One compliance or board-ready report generates from your live data.
- **Customer-side conditions (kept fair and explicit):** you provide your data export and attend the scheduled session. The clock pauses while we're waiting on records only you can send. _(This is the honest carve-out — GrantPipe can't import data it never receives.)_
- **Remedy:** if the three checkpoints aren't met within 14 active days for reasons on GrantPipe's side, the founder continues hands-on setup at no charge until they are. No extra fee, no upsell.

**Avatar fear it kills:**

- **The ED's "it took us a year to set up" trauma** — answered with a 14-day, founder-backed ceiling.
- **The Finance Director's "I'll be alone with it"** — a human is on the hook until the first report exists.
- **The volume buyer's belief gap** — extends real setup assurance _below_ the top tiers as the named onboarding path, closing the leak where "guided setup is gated to Audit-Ready/Enterprise" left Starter/Growth buyers believing on a trial alone.

**Why it's honest & safe:** every checkpoint is something GrantPipe controls or co-controls. The "you send your data / you show up" carve-out is the load-bearing honesty that keeps a solo founder from being liable for a customer who never uploads a file. The remedy is the founder's _time_, which is finite — see Scarcity.

> **Scarcity tie-in (honest):** a solo founder can only run so many guided setups at once. "Founder-led setup slots are limited each month" is _literally true_ and a clean, non-fabricated scarcity lever — not a fake countdown.

## Guarantee 3 — The Confirmed-Number Guarantee _(conditional — the AI/correctness pillar)_

This is the trust wedge against every "AI-powered" incumbent that never addresses _"will it hallucinate my numbers in front of my board?"_ The promise is narrow on purpose: **correctness of math on data the human confirmed**, not correctness of the human's underlying records.

**Terms (final voice):**

> GrantPipe's AI never acts alone — you confirm every award term it reads and every answer it gives. And every number GrantPipe calculates from the data you confirmed is one you can trace back to its source and check. If our math is ever wrong on confirmed data, we fix it free and refund that month.

**Exact mechanics — what's covered vs. not (this precision IS the guarantee):**

**Covered (GrantPipe controls):**

- **Human-in-the-loop, always.** AI Award Document Intake pre-fills terms; _you approve each one._ Ask-Your-Ledger answers from your real ledger; _you confirm._ The AI never writes to your books unattended. (Implied accuracy guarantee, surfaced as a pillar.)
- **Traceability.** Every figure GrantPipe computes — fund balances, rollforwards, budget-vs-actual, indirect cost, restriction releases — links to the journal entries / source records it came from. You can always click to the trail. ("Show your work" turns the moat into visible belief.)
- **Arithmetic correctness.** Given inputs you confirmed, the calculation is right: the rollforward sums, the indirect rate applies the rule you set, the release reduces the right fund.

**Explicitly NOT covered (and we say so plainly):**

- Whether your _underlying bookkeeping_ is right. Garbage in is still garbage — confirmed garbage.
- Whether an _auditor agrees_ with a judgment call. We don't promise audit outcomes.
- Whether _your data entry_ (the part you typed or confirmed) reflects reality. You own the inputs; we own the math on them.

- **Remedy:** if GrantPipe miscalculates a figure from data you confirmed — a genuine arithmetic or rule-application error on our side — we (1) fix the calculation at no charge and (2) refund the month in which the error occurred.

**Avatar fear it kills:**

- **The ED's "I'll be the one who can't answer where a dollar went / the number was wrong in front of the board."** The traceability + arithmetic promise means the board number is one she can defend line by line.
- **The Finance Director's "an AI will introduce an error I get blamed for at audit."** Human-confirm + "we own the math, you own the inputs" draws the liability line exactly where a CPA-literate buyer wants it.
- **The universal AI fear.** Converts "AI might lie to me" into "the AI can't act without me, and the arithmetic carries a warranty." That's a _reason to choose_ GrantPipe over "AI-powered" rivals that stay silent on the fear.

**Why it's honest & safe:** the promise is scoped to deterministic arithmetic on confirmed inputs — something a Principal SDET can actually stand behind and test. It never promises the customer's records are good or that an audit passes. The clean covered/not-covered boundary is what makes it both _believable_ (it's specific) and _defensible_ (it's bounded).

## Guarantee 4 — The Your-Data-Always Guarantee _(implied / anti-lock-in)_

A quiet but potent one for buyers whose last vendor _trapped_ them (Blackbaud lock-in is a named horror story).

**Terms (final voice):**

> Your data is yours. Export every donor, grant, fund, and ledger entry anytime, in open formats you can take anywhere. Leave whenever you want and walk out with everything.

**Exact mechanics:**

- Self-serve export of all core entities (donors, grants, funds, GL entries, documents) in CSV/JSON, available on every plan, at any time — including during the trial and after a refund request.
- No "export fee," no support-ticket gate, no waiting period.

**Avatar fear it kills:**

- **The Blackbaud/Salesforce escapee's "I'll be trapped again."** The fear isn't just that it fails — it's that leaving costs another migration project. This says the exit is free and one click.
- It also _strengthens Guarantee 1_: "get a full refund **and** take all your data with you" removes the last hesitation.

**Why it's honest & safe:** export already exists as product capability; naming it as a guarantee costs nothing and removes a real objection. It's a standing promise, so the "remedy" is simply that it's always on.

## The anti-guarantee (qualifier — use as a sidebar, not a guarantee row)

Hormozi's anti-guarantee filters the wrong buyer and raises belief for the right one. Honest framing, founder voice:

**Sidebar copy (final voice):**

> This isn't software you set and never look at. Every number here is one you can stand behind — because you confirmed it. If you want a black box that promises to think for you, that's not us. If you want to be the person who always has the answer, you're in the right place.

**Why it works:** it reframes the human-confirm step from a _limitation_ ("ugh, I have to check things") into the _entire value proposition_ ("you're never blindsided, because nothing happens without you"). It speaks straight to the avatar's actual emotional goal — **confidence and control, not delight** — and repels the buyer who wanted magic they don't have to understand.
