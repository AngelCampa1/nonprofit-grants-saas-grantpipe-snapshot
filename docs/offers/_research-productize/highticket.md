## The Core Design Decision: Resolve the Double-Sold-Setup Problem First

The red-team flagged this as a blocker. The current state: `hasGuidedOnboarding: true` on Audit-Ready and Enterprise [code] makes a "founder-led setup" a tier inclusion — but the value ladder also proposes selling it as a separate $2,500 SKU. Selling the same scarce founder time as both a bundled inclusion AND a paid add-on is double-dipping. It also obscures value: an invisible perk is worth nothing to a buyer deciding whether Audit-Ready is worth $159/mo billed annually.

**Resolution: the "gifted-on-annual" model, with a visible price tag.**

The First-Close Intensive is priced at a real number ($3,500 — see rationale below). It is gifted free on Audit-Ready annual and Enterprise annual. It is NOT included on monthly billing at any tier. It is available as a paid add-on for Growth annual buyers who want it. It is not sold to Starter. This model:

- Makes the annual Audit-Ready plan visibly more valuable (the buyer sees what they are NOT paying for)
- Creates a real upgrade lever from Growth to Audit-Ready ("you can add the session for $3,500 on Growth annual, or upgrade to Audit-Ready annual and it is included")
- Puts one honest scarcity cap on a single SKU (not split across tiers and a paid service simultaneously)
- Is producible by one person: four sessions per month [planning estimate — real ceiling, not a marketing number]

## The Offer: The First-Close Intensive

### Name

**The First-Close Intensive** _(internal working name; final name must clear the copy gate)_

**Rationale for the name:** "First-Close" is specific — this is about closing the loop on the first real grant, the first real fund, the first real answer from your data. "Intensive" signals: this is not a tour of the software. It is work. The buyer comes in, brings real documents, and leaves with something done. "Intensive" also justifies premium pricing in the buyer's mind without invoking "consulting" (which the burned avatar distrusts).

**Alternate name options to test:** "The Founder's Setup Session" (simpler, lower-barrier), "The First Grant Intensive" (narrower, stronger if the primary ICP is grant-heavy). The existing offer copy uses "Founder Setup" — either is honest. The name that wins is the one that makes the deliverable list feel obvious.

---

### Exact Format

This is a **done-with-you** engagement, not done-for-you. The distinction is load-bearing: done-for-you requires the founder to do the work after the session ends, which creates unbounded liability. Done-with-you means the buyer is in the room, making decisions in real time, and the founder is the guide. What gets done is determined by what the buyer brings and approves. The founder never holds credentials or takes unsupervised action on the buyer's data.

**Structure: one primary 90-minute live session + one follow-up call 7 days later**

Session 1 — The Setup Call (90 minutes, live, screen-share)

Format: video call, screen share on the buyer's GrantPipe account, founder driving with the buyer navigating approvals.

What happens:

- 10 min: review what the buyer brought (one award letter, one fund or grant already in their records, or a bank statement showing a restricted gift). Confirm the session scope.
- 25 min: AI Award Document Intake ✅ — run the award letter through intake together; founder explains each field as it pre-fills; buyer confirms or corrects every extracted term. Buyer leaves knowing how to do this independently.
- 20 min: Fund setup and restriction lifecycle ✅ — create the fund, set the restriction terms, link the grant to the fund. Buyer sets up the restriction; founder explains what net-asset class means and why the choice matters for audit evidence.
- 15 min: Compliance calendar + spend-down tracker ✅ — enter the deadlines from the award letter; confirm alert timing; set spend-down tracking. Buyer sees the first automated email preview before the call ends.
- 10 min: Ask-Your-Ledger demo on their live data ✅ — buyer asks one real question about the grant or fund they just set up; founder walks through drill-down to journal entries. This is the aha moment: the answer exists in their system now, not in a spreadsheet.
- 10 min: Scope Session 2 — what to bring, what to expect, open questions.

Session 2 — The Close Call (30 minutes, 7 days later)

Format: video call, screen share.

What happens:

- Review what the buyer did in the 7 days since Session 1 (entered more grants, imported donations, reviewed native accounting records if applicable).
- Answer one or two questions that came up after Session 1. The buyer almost always surfaces a question after they start working alone.
- Walk through the compliance calendar and confirm it matches the buyer's fiscal year.
- Optional: run one board-ready financial output ✅ if the data is in the system.
- End with the buyer confident enough to run the next grant independently.

Total founder time per engagement: approximately 2.5 hours (90-min + 30-min + 20 min prep/follow-up). [planning estimate — real number, not a round-number marketing figure]

### Concrete Deliverables (✅ only)

The buyer leaves with all of the following or the guarantee triggers:

1. **One live grant in the system** ✅ — created from a real award document, AI intake run and confirmed by the buyer, budget lines entered.
2. **Compliance calendar populated** ✅ — at least one reporting deadline and one spend-down date on the calendar, automated alert scheduled.
3. **Restricted fund linked to the grant** ✅ — fund created with correct net-asset class, restriction terms documented, restriction lifecycle started.
4. **First Ask-Your-Ledger answer** ✅ — buyer has asked at least one question about their real data and seen the drill-down to journal entries. This is not optional: it is the moment the moat becomes tangible.
5. **"What We Set Up" follow-up document** — emailed within 24 hours of Session 1. A plain-text summary of exactly what was configured, what choices the buyer made, and three things to do before Session 2. One page. Written by the founder from memory immediately after the session. No template generation required — this is a handwritten note, not an automated output.

### Price

**$3,500 one-time** [planning estimate — proposed; not yet in `pricing.ts`; do not publish until formalized in code]

**Why $3,500, not $2,500:**

The value ladder and the grand-slam doc both propose $2,500. The red-team's analysis is correct that $2,500 is keepable and defensible — but it is anchored wrong. The anchor for a done-with-you founder session is not "what a consultant charges for data migration" — it is "what a fractional controller charges for the onboarding and audit-prep equivalent."

A fractional CFO charges $5,000–$7,500/mo [planning estimate, k38 Consulting benchmark] and the first month is heavily front-loaded with onboarding work. A pre-audit engagement consultant charges $3,000–$8,000 for one evidence-assembly sprint [planning estimate]. The First-Close Intensive delivers: one grant live, one fund structured correctly for ASC 958, one compliance system activated, one aha-moment from the single-schema moat. That is substantially the same output as the first deliverable of a $5,000/mo fractional engagement.

$3,500 sits:

- Below the floor of one fractional CFO month ($5,000) [planning estimate]
- Above generic "implementation fee" psychology ($2,500 reads as an implementation tax, not a premium expertise engagement)
- High enough that the buyer who pays it treats the session seriously and comes prepared
- Defensible as "you're paying for the person who built the system to sit with you for two calls and leave you with something done"

The honest solo-founder capacity argument also supports $3,500: four sessions per month at $3,500 = $14,000/mo from this SKU alone [planning estimate], which is the right contribution margin for the time it consumes. At $2,500 and four sessions it is $10,000/mo — a meaningful difference for a solo founder deciding whether to protect this time.

**Gifted-on-annual framing (draft copy):** "The First-Close Intensive is a $3,500 engagement. It is included free with Audit-Ready annual." The buyer sees what they are not paying. That is the value anchor — not a vague "guided setup included."

### Monthly Capacity and Honest Scarcity

**Real cap: 4 sessions per month.** [planning estimate — defensible based on 2.5 hours per engagement × 4 = 10 hours of focused founder time, which is a realistic upper bound alongside active product development]

This is the only honest scarcity GrantPipe has, and it is real. A solo founder building a product and running setup calls cannot sustain more than four per month without the product stopping. That constraint is not a marketing tactic — it is a structural fact.

**How to surface scarcity without lying:**

- Show the "next available" date for a session slot, wired to real calendar availability from the Cal.com booking link (`FOUNDER_BOOKING_URLS.onboardingCall` [code]).
- Show a slot counter ONLY when ≤ 3 genuine open slots remain in the current month AND the count is pulled from real booking data, not hand-typed.
- When full for the current month: show the next available month with a waitlist option. Never block the buyer from the plan itself — the plan is always purchasable; the session is the scarce element.
- Do not advertise a hard number in the static copy. "A limited number of setup sessions are available each month" is accurate and honest without requiring a live count.

**The scarcity copy that works (draft copy):** "One person runs every setup session. That means four slots a month. When they fill, you join the waitlist — the plan and the free trial stay open the whole time."

### How It Bundles With or Feeds Into the Annual Subscription

**The single model: gifted on Audit-Ready annual, paid add-on for Growth annual, not available on Starter or monthly billing.**

| Scenario                      | What the buyer pays                 | Notes                                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Audit-Ready annual            | $1,908/yr [code] — session included | Show "$3,500 First-Close Intensive, included free" explicitly at checkout        |
| Enterprise annual             | Custom — session included           | Same framing                                                                     |
| Growth annual (wants session) | $948/yr [code] + $3,500 one-time    | Session is an add-on at checkout, not an automatic inclusion                     |
| Growth annual (no session)    | $948/yr [code]                      | Normal checkout                                                                  |
| Any monthly billing           | Session not available               | Keep the upgrade CTA: "Annual plans include [or add] the First-Close Intensive"  |
| Starter (any billing)         | Session not available               | Starter does not have `hasGuidedOnboarding: true` [code] — do not offer or imply |

**The ascension mechanics this creates:**

A Growth annual buyer who adds the $3,500 session pays $948 + $3,500 = $4,448 total. Audit-Ready annual is $1,908. This means the standalone session math no longer supports an Audit-Ready upsell without revisiting the setup SKU. Do not publish this bundle until the setup offer is repriced or re-scoped.

**Solving the double-sold problem permanently:** Once the First-Close Intensive is a named, priced, gifted-on-annual SKU, it can never be both a "bundled inclusion" and "also available separately for the same price" — because the prices are explicit. The gift is visible. The tier comparison makes the value obvious.

### The Guarantee Attached to This Offer

**The First-Close Guarantee (draft copy):**

"Book your session. Come with one real award letter. In that call, we get your first grant live. Its deadlines go on the calendar. Its fund is linked. If we do not finish all three before the call ends, we schedule a second session free, and we keep going until they are done."

**Terms (internal — these govern what the guarantee covers):**

- Buyer must be on a paid Audit-Ready or Enterprise plan (or have added the session to a Growth annual plan).
- Buyer attends the session and brings at least one real award document (PDF, email, or Word file).
- The three covered outputs are: grant created in the system, at least one compliance deadline on the calendar, the grant's restricted fund linked. These are founder-controlled outputs — they depend on the buyer providing a real document and making decisions in the session, not on external factors.
- Remedy: a second 60-minute session scheduled within 14 days at no charge. NOT a refund of the session fee (for the gifted version, "remedy" is the second call; for the paid add-on version, an additional 60-minute session is the equivalent of the original value).
- The guarantee does NOT cover: audit outcomes, passing any regulatory review, correctness of the buyer's underlying records, or anything outside the three defined deliverables.
- The quiet correctness line from the existing offer copy [docs/offers/founder-setup-offer.md] remains separate and is not part of this guarantee.

**Why this guarantee works for a solo founder:**

- The remedy is bounded: a second 60-minute call, not "free access forever" or "work until done."
- The trigger is verifiable: either the three deliverables are in the system at the end of Session 1 or they are not. No judgment call.
- The buyer controls the main variable (bringing a real document). Showing up without a document does not trigger the guarantee — that is clearly stated in the terms.
- It is uncontested: no competitor in this category publishes any session guarantee. Naming this guarantee at all is white space.

### Premium Positioning (No Discounting)

This SKU does not go on sale. It is not part of the founding-member promo. The founding-member 80%-off price applies to the subscription cost, not to the session. This is a deliberate choice:

- The session's value comes from founder time, which is fixed and not scalable. Discounting it implies the time is worth less than the stated price, which undermines the belief the session is designed to build.
- The buyer who is price-sensitive at $3,500 for two calls with the founder is not the buyer the session is designed for. The session is for the buyer who wants to be live and confident in one sitting — that buyer is not shopping on price.
- If a buyer says "I'd book it if it were cheaper," the right response is: "Start with the Audit-Ready annual plan — the session is included." That is not a discount; it is a routing conversation.

The anchor to use when asked about the price (draft copy): "One hour with a nonprofit fractional controller costs $150–$200. Two calls totaling two and a half hours — plus you leave with grants live, not with a list of recommendations — is the better comparison. We are not doing recommendations; we are doing the work with you."
