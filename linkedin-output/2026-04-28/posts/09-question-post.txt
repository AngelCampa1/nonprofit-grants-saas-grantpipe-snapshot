Board treasurers carry personal fiduciary exposure for an organization they see for two hours a month.

The BoardSource Leading with Intent 2021 survey found 75% of board members rate their own financial literacy as basic or below. That is not a character flaw — it is structural. Volunteer boards are composed for governance breadth, not accounting depth. The treasurer is liable for restricted fund misuse they "knew or should have known" about, while reviewing a Statement of Activities that reports net assets with donor restrictions as a single aggregate line.

FASB ASC 958 does not require grant-level detail on the face of the statements. A nonprofit managing 10 restricted grants can satisfy the standard with one number. The treasurer either trusts that number or asks for a supporting schedule, which usually arrives Friday evening before a Tuesday meeting via spreadsheet.

GAO-24-106173 reported that 17% of federal award spending from 2017 to 2021 was linked to severe and persistent single audit findings. Median audit costs for $1M to $10M budgets run $10,000 to $45,000, with single audits adding $5,000 to $20,000 on top.

Question for board treasurers and finance committee chairs: what is the verification step you wish you had between Friday's packet and Tuesday's vote — and how often does that gap actually get closed before you sign Form 990?
