DonorPerfect publishes a 93% retention rate and a 13-year average customer tenure. Those numbers are real. They reflect a mature donor CRM that teams build operations around and stay on.

The honest comparison with GrantPipe is not "which one is better" — it is which question the Executive Director most often needs answered. If the question is "who gave, when, how much, what campaign did they respond to," DonorPerfect's reporting is fast and the workflow is well-designed for fundraising. If the question is "what is the current balance of the State Housing Grant fund," DonorPerfect holds a fund designation label but not a fund balance. Someone in finance calculates the answer from a separate source. That calculation may or may not be current.

The Nonprofit Finance Fund 2025 survey found 36% of nonprofits ended FY2024 with an operating deficit, and organizations managing restricted grants without integrated compliance tracking spent an average of 11 additional hours per month assembling manual reports. Eleven hours a month is more than a full work week per quarter spent on data assembly that should be a system output.

The decision framework is simpler than feature comparisons. Draw a circle around what the ED needs every week. If the circle contains restricted fund balances and grant compliance status, the unified system removes the coordination cost. If it does not, DonorPerfect covers it.
