Myth: software marketed as "grant tracking" handles post-award compliance.

Reality: most of it covers the pre-award pipeline only. Pre-award is grant discovery, application stages, deadline reminders, award decisions. Post-award is restricted fund balances by budget category, expenditures linked to grant lines, and SF-425-ready compliance reports pulled from real posted transactions.

The two are different problems with different data models. A tool that tracks "applied → awarded → reporting" without ever touching the GL is a calendar with a logo. It cannot answer the only question that matters at audit: did the dollars spent against this grant match the approved budget categories.

Three capabilities separate compliance tools from pipeline trackers — restricted fund balance tracking by budget category, expenditure documentation linked to individual grant budget lines, and compliance report generation from actual expenditure data. If a tool cannot do those three, it tracks grants informally.

Foundant GrantHub Pro was sunset January 31, 2026, and never covered restricted fund tracking in the first place. Buyers searching for a GrantHub replacement should be very clear about which problem they are actually solving.

The mistake most buyers make is searching for "grant tracking software" when what they actually need is post-award compliance.
