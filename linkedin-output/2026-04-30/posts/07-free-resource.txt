Federal grant closeout is not a single event. It starts 90 days before the period of performance ends and continues for 3 to 7 years after the grant formally closes.

The 90-day mark is for budget-versus-actual review, no-cost extension decisions, and subrecipient closeout planning. The 30-day mark is for equipment review (capitalization threshold $10,000 under 2 CFR 200.313), unspent funds calculation, and the final obligation lock. The final week is for expenditure lock and reconciliation to the dollar against the accounting system.

Post-period, the final SF-425 is due within 120 calendar days for most agencies, 90 days for some HUD programs. The cumulative federal expenditures on that report must match the GL exactly. A discrepancy is an audit finding.

Record retention under 2 CFR 200.334 is 3 years from the date of final expenditure report submission — not from the period end date. USDA and HUD programs commonly require 7 years. Records involved in pending audits, litigation, or claims must be retained until the matter resolves.
