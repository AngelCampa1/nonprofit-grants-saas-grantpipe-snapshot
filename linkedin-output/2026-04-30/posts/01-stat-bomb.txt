The nonprofit CRM market reached $846 million in 2025, and 48% of nonprofits are actively considering switching platforms in the next 12 months — up from 10% the year before.

Blackbaud holds 40-50% of U.S. mid-to-large deployments and processes nearly $31 billion in annual charitable donations. Salesforce serves about 55,000 organizations through Power of Us. Bonterra absorbed Network for Good, EveryAction, Social Solutions, CyberGrants, and added OneCause in October 2025 — 14,000 more clients in one transaction.

The top five vendors hold only 40-45% of global revenue. The other 55-60% is spread across dozens of focused tools.

The blind spot in the consolidation story is the gap between donor management and grant compliance. Most CRMs were built around individual giving. Grant management — restricted funds, post-award compliance, funder reports — is treated as a secondary feature or shipped to a separate tool.

For mid-sized nonprofits running both, that gap is operational drag, not a feature checklist. The vendor you sign with today may be a different company in two years. Prioritize data portability and contract flexibility over brand loyalty.
