Bloomerang Standard runs $125 per month for up to 1,000 records and Pro runs $249 per month for up to 5,000. Capterra puts it at 4.66 out of 5 across more than 1,286 reviews — second highest in the nonprofit CRM category.

The product earns those numbers honestly on the donor side. The retention dashboard, lapse-risk indicators, and donor timeline are the most developed in the under-$300 tier. Development staff coming off spreadsheets find it intuitive on day one.

The gap is grants. Bloomerang has no grant lifecycle module — not a thin one, a complete absence. No restricted fund tracking, no budget-versus-actual against grant line items, no compliance report generation. An organization that wins a $500,000 federal grant cannot model where the restricted dollars go inside Bloomerang.

The result for grant-receiving nonprofits is a parallel system: Bloomerang for donors, a spreadsheet or standalone tracker for grants. Duplicate entry, manual reconciliation, and compliance records that drift away from CRM records by month six.

The honest evaluation question is whether the organization is donor-funded or grant-funded. Donor-funded under $5M with no restricted grant exposure — Bloomerang is a strong choice. Grant-receiving with three or more active awards — the parallel-system tax becomes the actual cost.

Per-record pricing also keeps climbing as the database grows.
