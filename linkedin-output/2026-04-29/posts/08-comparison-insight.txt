Little Green Light at $45 to $200 per month versus GrantPipe at $329 to $1,079. The right answer depends on one variable: do you actively manage federal or foundation grants, or just donors.

Little Green Light is a strong tool for organizations under $500K in budget that are donor-funded and need clean gift entry, acknowledgments, and basic reporting. Any staff member can learn it without training. The ceiling shows up around $500K to $1M in budget when reporting needs go beyond what an Excel export can handle and when grant management enters the picture.

GrantPipe assumes grants from day one. Restricted-fund tracking, deadline calendars, budget-versus-actual against grant budgets, and donor CRM all in one system, priced flat per organization rather than per contact. The tradeoff is more surface area than a $250K all-donor nonprofit needs.

The wrong reason to pick either tool is current pricing. Replatforming in 18 months costs more than the price difference in any direction.

If your organization is past $500K in budget and your grant load is growing, the cheap tool stops being cheap when you outgrow it.
