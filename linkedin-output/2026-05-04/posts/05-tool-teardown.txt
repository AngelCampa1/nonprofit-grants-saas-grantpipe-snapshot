Network for Good was acquired by Bonterra in 2022, alongside EveryAction, CommunityForce, and Social Solutions.

Before the acquisition, pricing was published in tiers. After the acquisition, pricing moved to a custom contract model requiring a demo. Reviews written before 2022 describe a different product structure than what current prospects encounter in a sales conversation.

What the platform does: fundraising pages, peer-to-peer campaigns, donor email tools. What it has never done: grant prospect tracking, restricted fund management, grant budget-versus-expenditure tracking, or compliance report generation. These were not features removed at acquisition — they were never part of the product.

Transaction fees may apply on processed donations in addition to the subscription cost. The specific fee structure is part of the negotiated contract, not disclosed publicly.

For nonprofits whose primary activity is campaign-based fundraising with no grant compliance requirements, the product is coherent. For organizations receiving restricted foundation or government grants, grant compliance still lives elsewhere — usually in a spreadsheet.

The evaluation question is not whether the platform works for fundraising. It is whether your organization's workflow requires a system that handles restricted grant compliance alongside donor management, or two separate systems with manual reconciliation between them.
