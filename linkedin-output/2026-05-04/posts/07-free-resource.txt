Grant compliance failures happen after the award, not during the application.

The obligations begin the moment the grant agreement is signed. Spending restrictions, reporting cadences, documentation standards, and time tracking requirements are all active before the first dollar is spent. Most mid-sized nonprofits discover the scope of those obligations when the first report is due, not when the award letter arrives.

The Nonprofit Grant Compliance Checklist covers the post-award sequence: reading the award letter before any spending begins, setting up restricted fund tracking in the accounting system, calendaring every reporting deadline immediately, implementing time documentation for grant-funded staff, and running budget-vs-actual comparisons monthly. It also covers the warning signs that the current system is approaching its limits — spreadsheets typically break at three to five active grants with different fiscal years and staff splitting time across programs.

The threshold at which a dedicated system replaces the spreadsheet is around $500,000 in grant revenue or three active grants managed simultaneously.
