Program income Step 3 under 2 CFR 200.307: establish the tracking ledger before any income-generating activity begins.

Program income is gross income earned by the grantee that is directly generated by activities supported by the federal award. Patient fees at a federally-funded clinic. Admission to a grant-funded training program. Rental of a federally-funded facility. The revenue is federal money earned under the award's authority — not unrestricted income, not a donation.

The ledger tracks the award number, income source description, date earned, amount, and cumulative total by period. Each of those fields is there because the Federal Financial Report SF-425 asks for cumulative program income earned and expended on Lines 10 and 10a — and auditors testing Single Audit compliance cross-reference those lines against the accounting records.

Program income deposited into the general operating account without a separate tracking mechanism becomes unrecoverable within 30 days. The accrual basis applies: record when earned, not when collected. A patient visit on September 30 generates program income on September 30 regardless of when the insurance payment arrives.

Under 2 CFR 200.307(e)(1), deductive treatment is the default when the award is silent on disposition. Plan the election at award time — changing it mid-award requires written federal agency prior approval.
