Foundations cite inconsistent reporting compliance as the most common non-programmatic reason for declining grant renewals, according to a 2023 Candid funder survey.

The Federal Audit Clearinghouse data shows approximately 60% of Single Audit submissions arrive after the 30-day submission window. Late reporting is the top corrective action trigger in HHS Office of Inspector General findings. Development staff at mid-sized nonprofits spend an estimated 15–20% of their time on grant compliance administration, including deadline tracking, per NTEN benchmarking.

A grant deadline missed because it lived in an Outlook calendar that was not updated when a grant was amended is a compliance finding. A report submitted late because the finance contact did not know the drawdown window was closing is a compliance finding.
