Bloomerang starts at $125/month. Little Green Light starts at $39/month. Neither manages grants.

Little Green Light is the budget choice for small nonprofits that need a basic donor database — contacts, gifts, pledges, and basic reports. Setup runs 1-3 days. Reporting depth is shallow.

Bloomerang is the mid-tier upgrade. The retention dashboard surfaces at-risk donors, which most nonprofit CRMs ignore entirely. Pricing scales with record count to $249/month. Setup runs 1-2 weeks.

Only 33.3% of nonprofits rate their CRM as effective per the 2025 Fifty & Fifty Nonprofit Peer Report. 46.5% call it neutral. 20.1% call it ineffective.

The shared limitation: neither platform tracks restricted fund expenditures against grant budgets, produces audit-ready compliance reports, or handles the post-award grant lifecycle. Organizations receiving grants run grant management in spreadsheets alongside the CRM and reconcile manually each month.

Choose Little Green Light if budget dominates and the work is donor-only. Choose Bloomerang if retention analytics drive strategy. Choose neither if grants represent more than 20% of revenue — the spreadsheet workaround compounds quickly.
