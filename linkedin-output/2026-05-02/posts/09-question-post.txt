A question for executive directors at $500K-$10M nonprofits: how long does board prep currently take, and how many systems does the staff touch to assemble the packet?

The pattern is consistent. Revenue year-to-date comes from QuickBooks. Pipeline status comes from a CRM or a spreadsheet. Retention metrics come from a different report. Restricted fund balances come from QuickBooks classes if they exist or a spreadsheet if they do not. Compliance deadlines live in Outlook or one staff member's head.

When each number reconciles differently, assembling a packet that holds up under board scrutiny takes a full week. The ED often does it personally because no one else has context across every system.

Mid-sized nonprofits spend an average of 20 to 30 percent of staff time on compliance data entry across at least 12 different reporting systems per the Center for an Urban Future January 2023 research.

Organizations that compress board prep from a week to an afternoon do it the same way: one system holding donor, grant, restricted fund, and compliance data, with reports generated from live data rather than assembled the night before.
