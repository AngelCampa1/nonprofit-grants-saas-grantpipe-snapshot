NTEN's 2024 Security Survey found 46% of nonprofits reported a data breach or unauthorized access incident in the past three years. Improper access controls were the leading contributing factor.

Salesforce NPSP implementations average $15,000-$50,000 in consulting fees just for permission and security model configuration. The platforms are flexible, but configuring them correctly requires expertise most nonprofit operations staff do not have.

The result: organizations leave default permissions in place because locking it down requires a consultant.

GrantPipe uses three roles — Admin, Editor, Viewer — that cover every legitimate access pattern for nonprofit operations. Role assignment is two fields and takes effect immediately. No support ticket, no setup project.

Board members get Viewer access for dashboards. Program staff get Editor access to update grant milestones. The audit trail logs every create, edit, and delete by user and timestamp.

Staff turnover at nonprofits runs 20-30% annually. Simpler permission systems handle the recurring deprovisioning work more reliably.
