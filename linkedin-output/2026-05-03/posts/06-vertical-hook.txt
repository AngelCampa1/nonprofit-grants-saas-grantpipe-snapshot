A mid-sized homeless shelter manages 8-12 simultaneous funding streams: HUD ESG, HUD Continuum of Care, CDBG sub-allocations, state housing authority grants, county homelessness funds, United Way, and three or four foundation awards.

Each has distinct allowable expense categories. ESG alone has five activity buckets — street outreach, shelter operations, rapid re-housing, prevention, and HMIS.

When a case manager's salary touches multiple grants, the time must be allocated contemporaneously. Month-end reconstructions from memory create the documentation gaps that auditors flag.

HUD's Continuum of Care Program awarded approximately $3.16 billion in FY2023 to more than 7,000 projects. ESG distributed $375 million. The compliance load is real.

Commingling federal ESG with state housing funds is an audit finding that can trigger repayment demands and loss of future HUD funding. The reconciliation needs to be continuous, not deadline-driven.
