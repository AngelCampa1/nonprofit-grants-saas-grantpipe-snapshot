The de minimis indirect cost rate is 15% of MTDC, raised from 10% by the 2024 Uniform Guidance revision under 2 CFR 200.414(f).

Eligible nonprofits — those that have never received a federally negotiated indirect cost rate — can claim it on federal awards without going through NICRA negotiation.

Two facts that get organizations into trouble.

First, the base is Modified Total Direct Costs, not total direct costs. MTDC excludes equipment, capital expenditures, participant support, and sub-award amounts above $50,000 per subcontract. Applying 15% to the full budget is a recurring audit finding.

Second, once a NICRA is executed, there is no return to de minimis — even after the NICRA period closes.

Urban Institute analysis of Form 990 data found roughly 25% of eligible nonprofits claim no indirect cost recovery at all. That is real money left unrecovered.
