Most development directors at mid-sized nonprofits manage 10-30 active grants. Each grant has its own reporting schedule, format, and renewal timeline. That is 60-100 deadline events per year for an 8-funder portfolio.

Missed deadlines do not happen because someone forgot. They happen because the grants manager remembered with three days to go and could not assemble narrative, financial reconciliation, and ED approval in time.

Lead time rules that work for a team of 1-3 people: 3 weeks for an interim narrative report, 2 weeks for an interim financial report, 4 weeks for a final narrative, 6 weeks for a new application, 4 weeks before grant end for a no-cost extension.

Request financial data from the bookkeeper at least 10 business days before any financial report is due. That accounts for workload, coding questions, and one round of corrections.
