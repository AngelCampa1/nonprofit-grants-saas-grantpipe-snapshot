A $50,000 grant with $10,000 in compliance overhead is a $40,000 net grant.

The Nonprofit Finance Fund coined "net grant" to quantify what most grants managers already know: compliance costs eat roughly 20% of every award.

The median nonprofit spends 6-8 hours per grant per year on reporting alone, with cumulative effort reaching 30+ hours over a grant's life.

Outsourced compliance consultants charge $132-$159/hour. For a small nonprofit, 10-20 hours of monthly help runs $18,000-$36,000 a year — sums that rival individual awards.

42% of non-government funders cap indirect costs at 10% or less while actual overhead runs 25-35%. That gap is why 36% of nonprofits ended FY2024 in deficit.

GrantPipe automates the reporting workflows that consume those hours so more of each grant reaches the mission.
