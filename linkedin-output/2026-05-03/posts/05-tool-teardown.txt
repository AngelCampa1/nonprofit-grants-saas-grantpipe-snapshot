Neon CRM handles donors, members, and event registration in one platform — a fit for organizations running fundraising galas, membership drives, and giving days simultaneously.

What it does not include: grant lifecycle management or restricted fund compliance. Organizations receiving federal or foundation grants with restricted funds typically run a second system or spreadsheet alongside Neon to cover the gap.

Pricing is custom. Every quote requires a demo first, and the modular NeonOne add-ons (Fundraise, Giving Days, Websites) push total cost higher than the base CRM line item suggests.

The Omatic 2025 Nonprofit Integration Report found nearly half of nonprofits are considering a CRM switch in the next 12 months — driven by feature gaps and growth, not cost.

If the compliance burden is restricted grants rather than membership renewal, a unified donor-plus-grant platform avoids the second-system problem entirely.
