The SF-SAC has two certifications, and missing either one keeps the submission in draft.

Step seven of the Single Audit Data Collection Form workflow at fac.gov: the auditee certifies the submission is accurate and complete, then the auditor separately certifies the audit findings.

Neither party can submit unilaterally. A form sitting at one signature is not filed, even if every field is complete and the reporting package uploaded cleanly.

Deadline pressure compounds this. The SF-SAC is due 30 days after the auditor's report or 9 months after fiscal year-end, whichever is earlier. Late filings affect low-risk auditee status under 2 CFR 200.520 in subsequent years.

Confirm both certifications cleared before treating an SF-SAC as submitted. Download the FAC confirmation PDF and retain it in the audit binder.
