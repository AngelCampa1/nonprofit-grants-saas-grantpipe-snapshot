The 2024 Uniform Guidance revision raised the federal single audit threshold to $1,000,000 in federal expenditures.

That change pulled smaller cost-reimbursement awardees out of single-audit scope, but it did not change how revenue gets recognized.

FASB ASU 2018-08 still governs the two tests for grant revenue under ASC 958-605: contribution vs exchange, then conditional vs unconditional.

Cost-reimbursement federal awards are typically conditional contributions. Revenue is recognized only as allowable costs are incurred, not when the award letter arrives.

Finance teams that book the full award upfront overstate revenue and end up restating it during audit fieldwork.

If your grant ledger does not match your expenditure schedule line for line, the conditional accounting is breaking somewhere.

GrantPipe attaches a ledger to each award so revenue recognition tracks expenditures automatically.
