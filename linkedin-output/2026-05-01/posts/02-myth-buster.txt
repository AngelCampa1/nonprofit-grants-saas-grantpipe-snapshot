Program service revenue is not unrelated business income.

This is where mid-sized nonprofits over-file Form 990-T and pay UBIT they do not owe.

Program service revenue is income earned from activities substantially related to the exempt purpose. A health center charging sliding-scale fees is program service revenue. A literacy nonprofit selling workbooks tied to its curriculum is program service revenue.

Earning it does not jeopardize tax-exempt status. The risk only appears when an activity stops being substantially related and starts looking commercial in scope and substance.

Excessive unrelated commercial activity raises private benefit and inurement concerns, which is a separate analysis from whether the revenue itself is taxable.

The practical test: review activities annually against the exempt purpose stated in your determination letter. Document the connection. Keep that memo with the 990 workpapers.
