A grant that projects $40,000 unspent with two months remaining is not a financial planning problem. It is a compliance problem that requires action now.

A spend-down tracker answers one question per active award: given what has been spent and when the period ends, are we on pace to use the full award correctly?

The structure is straightforward. One worksheet per grant. Budget vs actual by allowable cost category. Monthly burn rate. Projected end-of-award balance based on current pace. A decision framework for low burn rate situations — accelerate eligible expenditures, request a no-cost extension, or rebudget within funder rules.

The point is not the spreadsheet. The point is catching underspend in month 9 instead of month 11 of a 12-month award, when the options are still real.

The Grant Spend-Down Tracker is a free download. PDF with the template structure and the decision framework.
