GrantPipe brand. Third person. Authoritative, direct, practitioner-grade.

## Rules for every post

1. The first line is the hook. It must be concrete: a specific number, a regulation name, a named tool, a dollar amount, or a specific scenario. No abstract openers.
2. The body contains 4-8 lines. At least one line must include a specific: regulation cite (e.g., 2 CFR 200.430), dollar amount, percentage, named tool, or named process step.
3. End with 1-2 lines that are either a question for engagement, a link/free resource mention, or a soft product mention (only for CTA posts).
4. Final line: 3-5 lowercase hashtags with no space after #. Examples: #grantcompliance #nonprofitfinance #restrictedFunds #grants
5. Max 280 words per post.
6. No exclamation marks. Confidence does not need exclamation marks.
7. Do not start sentences with "But" or "And".
8. No bullet points inside the post body. Use prose lines only.
9. Posts should read like something a knowledgeable practitioner would say to a peer, not a vendor blog post.

Generate all posts in sequence separated by `---`. Number each post 1 through 10. Use this exact format:
