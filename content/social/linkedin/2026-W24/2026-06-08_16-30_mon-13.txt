We model grants and funds as separate records in GrantPipe.

That choice is slower to build, but it matches the work.

One grant can fund several restricted funds.

One fund can support work tied to several awards.

A donor restriction can outlive a reporting deadline.

An award can close while the financial statement still needs the right restriction history.

For grant-funded nonprofits, flattening that into one "grant" object makes the early screen simpler and the later audit trail worse.
