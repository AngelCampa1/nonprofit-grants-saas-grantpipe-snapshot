FFATA reporting is easy to miss because it sits beside, not inside, the usual monitoring routine.

If you are the prime recipient and obligate a federal subaward of $25,000 or more, the report is due through SAM.gov by the end of the month after obligation.

The record needs the FAIN, subrecipient UEI, obligation amount, place of performance, and other required fields.

That is separate from 2 CFR 200.332 monitoring.

Same subaward. Different obligation.
