Grants.gov is for finding and applying to federal opportunities.

Post-award reporting happens somewhere else.

SF-425s, drawdowns, performance reports, and closeout steps live in agency systems such as PMS, GrantSolutions, IDIS, DRGR, or JustGrants, depending on the award.

That distinction matters when you build a compliance calendar.

"Submitted in Grants.gov" is not the end of the operating record.
