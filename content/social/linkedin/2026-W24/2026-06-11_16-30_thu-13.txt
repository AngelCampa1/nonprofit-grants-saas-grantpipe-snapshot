We modeled grants and funds as separate records in GrantPipe.

A grant is an agreement: funder, award amount, period, reports, amendments.

A fund is a pool of restricted dollars: balance, activity, releases, net asset classification.

They overlap, but they are not the same thing.

That choice made the product harder to build. It also prevents a bad shortcut: treating one grant as one fund forever.

Real restricted fund work is many-to-many. The data model has to admit that.
