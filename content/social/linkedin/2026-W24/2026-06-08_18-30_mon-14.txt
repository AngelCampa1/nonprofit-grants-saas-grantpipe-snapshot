Grants.gov search is only the front door.

For federal awards, the harder operating work starts after the opportunity becomes an award:

- UEI and SAM.gov details
- period of performance
- ALN or FAIN
- reporting cadence
- indirect cost basis
- pass-through or direct award status
- closeout deadline

GrantPipe includes Grants.gov federal opportunity search.

The post-award record is where the compliance work has to hold together.
