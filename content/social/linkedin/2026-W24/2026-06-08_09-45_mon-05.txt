Six things that belong in the grant audit binder before fieldwork starts:

1. Notice of award and amendments
2. Grant ledger report
3. SF-425 or funder financial reports already submitted
4. Budget vs. actual summary
5. Time and effort records for grant-funded staff
6. Procurement records for major purchases

That list is not glamorous. It is the difference between "here is the support" and "we think that file is in a folder somewhere."

The binder is not a performance.

It is a map from every claim in the grant file to the document that supports it.
