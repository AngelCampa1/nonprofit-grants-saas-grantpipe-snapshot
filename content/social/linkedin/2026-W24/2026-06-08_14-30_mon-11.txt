Donor-to-grant reconciliation is boring until the numbers disagree in public.

The template should answer:

- What did Development enter?
- What did Finance record?
- What does the award document say?
- Is the amount pledged, awarded, received, or released?
- Who changed the record after review?

GrantPipe has a Donor-to-Grant Reconciliation Template for teams cleaning up CRM, fund, and grant records before board or audit season.
