Cost reimbursement creates a cash timing problem.

The organization spends first, documents the expense, submits the request, then waits for payment.

Advance drawdowns are different. Under 2 CFR 200.305, federal cash advances are tied to immediate need, and excess cash creates interest obligations.

That means the grant file should track more than spend-down.

It should also track request dates, payment dates, and aging reimbursement status.
