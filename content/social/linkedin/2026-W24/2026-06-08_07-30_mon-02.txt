The donor CRM and the grant ledger can describe the same funder in different languages.

Development sees Foundation X as a relationship, proposal, award letter, and pledge.

Finance sees a receipt, restriction, fund code, release schedule, and reporting obligation.

Neither view is wrong.

The risk starts when they do not meet.

A monthly reconciliation should match:

- funder name
- award amount
- cash received to date
- restriction classification
- grant status
- source document

The source document matters most. If the grant agreement says restricted, the CRM tag should not say unrestricted general support.
