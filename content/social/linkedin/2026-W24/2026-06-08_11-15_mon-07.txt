Release from restriction is not new revenue.

Under FASB ASC 958, a release moves donor-restricted net assets into without-donor-restrictions when the purpose or time restriction has been met.

That distinction matters in board reporting.

A $100,000 grant can be real, restricted, and unavailable for general operations at the same time.

The schedule should show what was received, what remains restricted, what was released, and what condition triggered the release.
