Seven things to have ready when a federal program officer asks for status.

1. Award number and ALN.
2. Period of performance dates.
3. Approved budget by line.
4. Spend-down by line.
5. Last SF-425 submitted.
6. Open prior approvals or amendments.
7. Next performance report due date.

That list is not the audit binder. It is the conversation file.

If it takes half a day to assemble, the grant record is not current enough for normal management.
