SF-425 line 10e deserves more respect than it gets.

That line is the federal share of expenditures. On a final SF-425, it is cumulative from the start of the award through the end of the period of performance.

Before submission, compare it to the grant-specific general ledger.

If the form and the ledger do not match, do not explain it later.

Find the variance before the report goes out.

That is the cleaner audit trail.
