Allowable is not enough.

For a federal grant cost to survive 2 CFR 200 review, it also has to be allocable, reasonable, consistently treated, and adequately documented.

The allocable part is where messy work hides.

If rent, payroll, software, or supplies benefit multiple programs, the split needs a documented basis.

Convenience is not a cost allocation method.

Time worked, square footage, headcount, or another reasonable basis is much easier to defend.
