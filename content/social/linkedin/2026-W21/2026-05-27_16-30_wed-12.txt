We decided early that grants and funds would be separate entities in the data model, with a many-to-many table between them.

Most simpler grant tools collapse the two. One grant equals one fund. It feels right at first.

It breaks the moment you take a $200K federal pass-through that funds parts of three programs, or a multi-year foundation grant where year one is unrestricted operating and year two is purpose-restricted.

FASB ASC 958 cares about the restriction, not the funding source. Sage Intacct and Blackbaud model it the same way. The audit binder needs both views.

So the model has grants. It has funds. It has grant_fund_allocations between them. The UI hides the join when you do not need it and shows it when you do.

That is the kind of decision that pays off in year three, not week one. We made it in week one anyway.
