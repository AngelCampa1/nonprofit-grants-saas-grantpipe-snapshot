Picture this: an auditor walks in. They ask which board meeting approved the Clinic Capacity grant rebudget.

The Executive Director opens her email. Then a Google Drive folder. Then a different Google Drive folder.

The minutes are in there somewhere. They were approved. The trail is just not where she can point to it in 60 seconds.

That gap, between "we did the right thing" and "I can show you we did the right thing," is what audit week actually feels like.
