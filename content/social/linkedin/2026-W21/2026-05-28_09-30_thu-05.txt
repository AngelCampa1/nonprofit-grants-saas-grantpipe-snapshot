Six things that belong on the May development calendar at a $2M nonprofit, in priority order.

1. Mid-year touch on every donor in the top 25 portfolio. Not an ask. A call.

2. Lapsed donor list pulled from this date last year. Anyone who gave May 2025 and has not given since gets a personal note from the Development Director by month-end.

3. Recurring giving audit. How many monthly donors did you start the year with, how many do you have today, and what is the failure rate on card declines.

4. Q3 grant pipeline review with Finance. What is in submission, what is in cultivation, what is at risk of slipping past your fiscal year close.

5. Board prospect introductions. Each board member, one name, by June 15. Not a list of fifty. One that they will actually make.

6. Annual report writing kicks off. Not the design. The narrative. Stories collected from program staff while the year is still warm.

The work is unglamorous. It is also the work that decides whether the November appeal performs or limps.
