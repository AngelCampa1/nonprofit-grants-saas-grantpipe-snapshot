$1,000,000. The single audit threshold under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

If your organization expends $1,000,000 or more in federal awards during your fiscal year, a single audit is required. Different scope and fee than a standard financial statement audit.

Two things to know about this number:

It is expended, not received. If you drew down $400,000 in cash but accrued and spent $800,000 on federal grant activities during the year, you are over.

It is total federal awards, including pass-through. State agencies and large nonprofits pass federal money through to subrecipients, and that money still counts. The pass-through entity should label funds with the Assistance Listing Number (formerly CFDA), but track it on your end too.

If you are within shouting distance of the threshold, talk to your auditor before fiscal year-end, not after.
