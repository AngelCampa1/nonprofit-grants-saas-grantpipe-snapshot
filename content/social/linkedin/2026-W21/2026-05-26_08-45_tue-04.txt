For the Finance Director who has been asked, again, to "just put restricted funds in the donor CRM."

It will not hold. Here is why.

Donor CRMs track gifts and donors. Restricted funds are a balance-sheet concept. The dollars sit in net assets with donor restrictions until released against the purpose or time stipulation the donor specified.

Your CRM does not have a chart of accounts. It does not know what a release from restriction is. It cannot produce the schedule of activity by net asset class your auditor will ask for.

You can fake it with custom fields and pivot tables. The cost is reconciliation work every month and a fragile audit trail every year.

Restricted funds belong in a system that models FASB ASC 958 directly. If your software does not have those concepts as first-class entities, you are configuring around them.
