For every Finance Director who has been told "just configure the CRM for grants", here is what that sentence usually hides.

The donor CRM tracks gifts. A grant is not a gift. It is a contract.

A gift has a date and an amount. A grant has a period of performance, a budget by line item, allowable cost rules, a reporting cadence, a drawdown schedule, match requirements, a sub-recipient list, and a closeout package.

You can stuff some of that into custom fields. The data goes in. It does not come back out as the report your auditor wants.

The CRM gives you a contact record. It does not give you the schedule of expenditures of federal awards, or the variance report by fund, or the spend-down by purpose restriction.

Configuring the CRM for grants is how you end up with three systems pretending to be one.

This is the gap GrantPipe was built for. The trial runs on your real data.
