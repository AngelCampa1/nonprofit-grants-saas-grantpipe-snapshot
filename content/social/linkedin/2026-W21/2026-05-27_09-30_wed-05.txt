For every Development Director who has rebuilt the same grant report three times this year because Finance and Development store the data differently.

You are not the problem. The shape of the tooling is the problem.

Here is what this often looks like: Donors live in Bloomerang or Salesforce NPSP. Grants live in a Google Sheet, sometimes two. Restricted fund balances live in QuickBooks classes. The grant narrative lives in a shared drive. The board report combines all of it, manually, the night before the meeting.

Each system is fine. None of them was built to talk to the others. The hand-off between Development and Finance happens in someone's inbox.

The visible cost is the hours you spend reconciling. The invisible cost is what gets dropped. The grant report that shipped with last quarter's spend-down number. The donor whose restricted gift got coded to the wrong fund. The board memo where the numbers tied at 11pm but not the next morning.

A unified system is not glamour. It is one less reconciliation per week. It is the thing that makes audit prep a checklist instead of a project.
