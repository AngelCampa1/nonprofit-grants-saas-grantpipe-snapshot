For every Development Director who walks into June with a stewardship calendar that hasn't been updated since March.

The mid-year donor check-in is one of the highest-yield calls you make all year. Not an ask. A thank-you, an update, and one specific question about how the year is going for them.

Three names a week. Fifteen minutes each. Notes back into the file before you close the call.

By August your top 25 portfolio is current, your year-end asks have real context, and you are not building the cultivation plan from memory in October.

The work is small. The compounding is not.
