For Finance Directors looking at SAM.gov for the first time.

The UEI registration is free. It used to be DUNS, then it changed in April 2022. Existing DUNS holders were transitioned automatically; everyone else gets a fresh UEI.

The renewal cycle is annual. Miss it and your active federal awards can be paused mid-drawdown. The renewal email comes from a noreply address that ends up in spam more often than it should.

Two things that catch new registrants:

1. The Entity Administrator role is tied to a specific person. When that person leaves, the next Admin has to be appointed through the role-management workflow inside SAM.gov. Not through email.

2. Banking information for federal payment goes through a separate system (PAM, plus the agency's payment portal). Updating one does not update the other.

The registration takes a couple of hours. The maintenance is what costs orgs an award.
