If your Development Director left tomorrow, who would know which grant reports are due in June?

This is the Sunday question worth asking before the calendar fills up.

Three things to check tonight, before Monday gets busy:

1. The list of reports due in the next 30 days. Funder, form, due date, owner.
2. The folder where evidence lives. Receipts, time logs, board minutes, signed amendments.
3. The person who could finish a report if the primary owner were out for two weeks.

If any of those three lives in one head, write it down before bedtime. June is two days away.
