The cheapest federal grant to manage is the one you read carefully before accepting.

Pre-award is where the budget gets fixed in place. Once the NOFO becomes a Notice of Award, the line items, the period of performance, the indirect cost treatment, and the match requirement are all locked. Rebudgeting more than ten percent typically requires prior approval. The ten percent rule is in 2 CFR 200.308.

That means the time to ask "can we charge a fractional accountant to this award" or "do we need to negotiate a NICRA versus our 15 percent de minimis" is before signature, not after the first quarterly FFR shows the variance.

Pre-award costs themselves require prior written approval under 2 CFR 200.458 and only apply 90 days before start.

Federal grant compliance is less about having a bigger finance team. It is about having a pre-award checklist.
