For every Executive Director heading into June board prep.

The five reports your board will ask for, in the order they actually ask:

1. Cash position and runway. Always first.
2. Year-to-date revenue vs budget, broken into unrestricted and restricted.
3. Grant pipeline (committed, pending, prospect) with renewal dates.
4. Functional expense ratio, because someone read a Charity Navigator article.
5. Restricted fund balances by purpose, with spend-down status.

The fifth one is where teams fall behind. Not because it is hard, but because it lives in a different system than the first four.

If you find yourself rebuilding restricted fund balances from QuickBooks classes the night before the board meeting, you are not behind. The tool is. That work should be a query, not a project.
