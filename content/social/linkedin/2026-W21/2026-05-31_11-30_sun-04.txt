Six things that should be in your audit binder before the fiscal year ends. Not the audit itself. The binder.

1. Award letters and fully signed amendments for every active grant.
2. The current indirect cost rate agreement (NICRA), or a note that you are using the 15% de minimis under 2 CFR 200.414(f).
3. Time-and-effort certifications for staff charged to federal awards.
4. Procurement files for purchases over the small purchase threshold, with three quotes or a sole-source memo.
5. Board minutes approving any rebudgeting over 10% that required prior approval.
6. Reconciliation between your general ledger and the most recent SF-425 filed for each federal award.

This list comes from the Audit Prep Week-by-Week Checklist, which walks the same six items week by week.
