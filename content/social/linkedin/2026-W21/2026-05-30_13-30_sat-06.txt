We decided early that GrantPipe would not have a setup fee.

Not because setup is free. It is not. We absorb the cost.

The reason is simpler. Implementation quotes from Salesforce and Blackbaud partners routinely run into five and six figures. That number is not a fee. It is a barrier.

A nonprofit with a $2M budget cannot underwrite a $40K bet on whether the software fits. So they do not buy. They keep the spreadsheet.

A setup fee on our side would replicate the same problem at a smaller scale.

So instead: month-to-month, public pricing, no setup fee, real data in the trial. If it does not fit, you leave.

The cost shows up on our P&L. The benefit is that nobody has to write a check before they know.
