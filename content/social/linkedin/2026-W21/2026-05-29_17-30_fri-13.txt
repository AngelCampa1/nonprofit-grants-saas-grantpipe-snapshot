Five things a unified donor and grant workspace should do without an export.

1. Look up a donor and see every gift, every grant they made or referred, and every restricted fund they touch.

2. Open a grant record and see live spend-down, next reporting deadline, assigned staff, and attached documents.

3. Run the activity feed on a fund and read the chronological story of how dollars came in and where they went.

4. Filter the donor list by giving capacity and last touch date without leaving the page.

5. Hand a read-only login to your auditor that shows grants, funds, documents, and GL mapping, without exposing donor names or board notes.

Most tools cover one or two. The ones that cover all five feel correct on a Tuesday afternoon when the work is real.
