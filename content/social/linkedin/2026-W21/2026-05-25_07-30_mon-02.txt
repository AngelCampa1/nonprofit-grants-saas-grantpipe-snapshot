$1,000,000.

That is the federal expenditure threshold that triggers a single audit under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F. Total federal awards spent in your fiscal year, not received. Pass-through dollars count.

The math people miss: it is cumulative across all federal sources. A $400K HRSA grant plus a $380K state pass-through that originated federally puts you over the line.

If you are close to the threshold this year, three things to nail down before fiscal close:

1. A clean schedule of expenditures of federal awards (SEFA) by ALN.
2. Sub-recipient vs vendor classification documented for every payment.
3. An auditor selected and engaged by month nine. Capacity gets tight in Q4.

The single audit is not punishment. It is the price of federal money. Plan for it like you plan for the 990.
