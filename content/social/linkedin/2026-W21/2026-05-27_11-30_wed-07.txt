The 2 CFR 200 Audit Prep Checklist covers items commonly missed in the second week of audit prep.

What is in it:

- The seven document categories auditors will request in the opening meeting (and the format they expect each in).
- Sub-recipient monitoring evidence, including the annual risk assessment most pass-through grantees skip.
- Time-and-effort certification samples, with the language that satisfies the "after the fact" requirement.
- Cost transfer documentation, the most common 2 CFR 200 finding for orgs new to federal funding.
- Procurement evidence aligned to 2 CFR 200.318, including the conflict of interest disclosure trail.
- The closeout package contents, with the 90-day clock starting from the period of performance end date.

It is six pages. No email gate.
