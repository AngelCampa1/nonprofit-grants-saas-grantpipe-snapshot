The week before the board meeting has a shape.

Monday: pull YTD financials. Notice the variance on the Clinic Capacity grant. Email the program lead.

Tuesday: rebuild the donor pipeline slide. The CRM export does not match the gift entry log.

Wednesday: write the narrative. Get stuck on which restricted funds were released last quarter. Ask Finance.

Thursday: chase the answer. Cancel a one-on-one to do it.

Friday: print packets. Notice the printer is out of toner.

The work is the work. But four of those five days were spent reconciling, not reporting. That is the part that breaks people.
