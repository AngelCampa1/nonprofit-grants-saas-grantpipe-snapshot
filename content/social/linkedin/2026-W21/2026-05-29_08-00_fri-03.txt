Four signs your donor CRM is not the right home for grant compliance.

1. There is no field for period of performance. Just a single "close date" that does not distinguish between the grant ending and the report being due.

2. Restricted fund balances are calculated by exporting to a spreadsheet. The CRM does not know which dollars are spent and which are still owed to a purpose.

3. Reporting cadence lives in a calendar, not in the record. A quarterly FFR is not the same shape as a "follow up" task.

4. The auditor cannot get a read-only view. Every user pays a per-seat license to look.

A donor CRM is built for the gift cycle. Compliance is a different job, with different fields and a different audience.

Two systems with one clean handoff. Or one workspace that holds both. What does not work is asking the donor CRM to do the second job by force of configuration.
