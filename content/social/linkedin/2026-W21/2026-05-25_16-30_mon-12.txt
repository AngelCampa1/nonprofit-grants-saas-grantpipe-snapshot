Why we publish pricing on the marketing site.

Nonprofit software often hides it. The reasoning, when stated, goes: "every org is different, so we tailor pricing to your needs." The reasoning, when honest, is that opaque pricing lets the salesperson read the room and quote what the buyer can stand to pay.

Mid-sized nonprofits cannot run a procurement on a quote that arrives after a 45-minute call. They need to compare. They need a number to put in a board packet. They need to know if a product is even in their bracket before spending two hours of their week on a demo.

Public pricing is not a marketing trick. It is the minimum that respects the buyer's time.
