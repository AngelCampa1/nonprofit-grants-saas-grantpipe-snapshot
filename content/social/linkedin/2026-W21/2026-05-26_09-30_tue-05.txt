Six things that should be in your audit binder before May 31.

1. Current chart of accounts with net asset classifications. Without donor restrictions, with donor restrictions, board-designated. Date-stamped.

2. Schedule of restricted activity for the year. Beginning balance, additions, releases from restriction, ending balance, by fund.

3. Grant agreements for every active and recently closed award. Signed, with all amendments and no-cost extensions attached.

4. Drawdown documentation for federal awards. Date, amount, expense detail, period of performance check. SF-425 filings if applicable.

5. Time-and-effort certifications for staff allocated across multiple awards. Signed quarterly. Tied to actual payroll allocation, not budgeted percentages.

6. Policies and procedures binder. Procurement (2 CFR 200.318), conflict of interest, allowable cost determination, cash management, sub-recipient monitoring. Reviewed within the last 12 months, with a sign-off page.

Six items. If any of them require more than one folder to assemble, the binder is doing two jobs: storing evidence and reconciling systems.

It only needs to do the first.
