Saturday list for Development Directors who are tired.

Things that look like progress but are not:

1. Reorganizing the donor pipeline in the CRM.
2. Renaming the campaigns from last year.
3. Cleaning up duplicate contacts.
4. Drafting a new gift acceptance policy.
5. Building a new dashboard.
6. Reviewing the org chart for the team you do not have.

Things that are actually progress:

1. Calling three lapsed donors and asking what changed.
2. Writing a one-page case for support that you would actually mail.
3. Sitting with the Finance Director for an hour and reconciling one grant.
4. Asking your board chair which two prospects she would introduce you to next month.
5. Sending a thank-you letter that mentions the program by name.
6. Closing the laptop at 4pm because Saturday is supposed to be Saturday.

The first list feels productive because it makes neat outputs. The second list feels uncomfortable because it requires conversations.

Most of the work that moves the org forward lives in the second list.
