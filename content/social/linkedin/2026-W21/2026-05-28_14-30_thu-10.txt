If your Development Director left tomorrow, who would know which grant reports are due in June?

Real question. Not rhetorical.

At a $1M to $5M nonprofit, the grant function can come down to one person, one inbox, and a spreadsheet that lives on their laptop. Maybe a calendar invite she set up two years ago. Maybe a handoff document that has not been updated since the last hire.

The reporting calendar is not a personal artifact. It belongs to the organization. So does the funder relationship history, the narrative templates, the budget vs actual context for every active award.

When that knowledge sits in one head, the org is one resignation away from missing a Performance Progress Report and triggering a corrective action plan with a federal program officer who has a long memory.

Make the calendar institutional. Make it visible. Make it survive.
