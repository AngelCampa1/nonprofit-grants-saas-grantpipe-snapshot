We publish pricing because not publishing it is a choice that favors the vendor.

Opaque pricing means the number you see depends on how you answered the qualification questions on the demo call. It means mid-sized nonprofits, the ones who cannot afford a procurement mistake, spend two hours in a discovery call before they know whether the product is in their bracket.

GrantPipe pricing is on the marketing site. Each tier lists the price and what is included. Buyers can check the number before they book a call.

A Finance Director can send that link to their Executive Director without scheduling a call. An Executive Director can put a number in a board packet before anyone talks to us. That is the minimum the buyer deserves.
