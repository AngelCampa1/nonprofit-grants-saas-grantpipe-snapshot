The week-prep ritual is the cheapest piece of fundraising infrastructure you own.

A useful weekly ritual: 20 to 40 minutes on Sunday night or Monday morning to set up a clean week.

The shape of it, roughly:

Look at the calendar four weeks out, not just one. Every report deadline, every site visit, every board meeting, every funder check-in.

Look at the major gift portfolio. Who needs a touch this week. Not a solicitation. A note, a thank-you, a quiet update on the program they fund.

Look at the grant pipeline. Three columns. Submitted and waiting. In drafting. On the radar but not started. Move one card forward in each column this week.

Look at restricted fund balances. Anything over 90 days old without spending activity. Anything close to a spend-down deadline. Anything that needs a release entry.

Look at the team. One person you owe something to. One person who owes you something. Close one of those loops.

It is not glamorous. It is the work that makes the rest of the work possible.

Whatever system you use to do this, the point is doing it. The system is downstream of the habit.
