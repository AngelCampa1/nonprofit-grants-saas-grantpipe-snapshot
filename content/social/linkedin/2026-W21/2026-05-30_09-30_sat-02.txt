A Saturday ritual worth borrowing: an hour with coffee, the donor list sorted by last gift date, and a notepad.

No CRM. No campaign tool. Just names, dates, and questions.

Three letters, by hand. Not thank-you letters. Check-in letters.

"How is the new building working out for you?" "Did your daughter graduate?" "I saw the article about your foundation's grant to the food bank. Wanted to say congratulations."

No ask. No event invitation. No reminder.

The segment is donors who gave between $1,000 and $25,000 last year. Not the top tier. Not the lapsed. The middle.

The system is paper, a pen, and an hour. The rigor is the discipline of doing it every Saturday, year after year.

Some things software does not improve.
