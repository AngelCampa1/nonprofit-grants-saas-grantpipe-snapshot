$1,000,000. That is the federal expenditure threshold that triggers a single audit under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

Federal awards in your fiscal year, aggregated, not per grant. Pass-through funds count. Loans count under specific conditions.

If you are at $620K this year and you just signed a new HRSA award for $250K, you are over. The audit is not optional, and the auditor needs to be procured under your written procurement policy, which itself has to comply with 2 CFR 200.318.

Orgs often first encounter this on the engagement call with their independent auditor.

The orgs that find out in May, when the second federal award comes in, have time to plan for it.
