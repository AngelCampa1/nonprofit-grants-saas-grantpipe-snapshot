Six things to put in your audit binder this week, before fiscal year-end gets loud.

1. Signed grant agreements for every active award. Originals or executed PDFs, not summaries.

2. The full schedule of expenditures of federal awards (SEFA), broken out by ALN. Sub-recipient pass-throughs flagged separately. Auditors will ask.

3. Time and effort certifications for every staff member who charges any percentage of their time to a federal award. Quarterly minimum, signed.

4. Procurement documentation for any single purchase over $10,000 made with federal dollars. 2 CFR 200.318 wants the trail. Bids, sole-source justifications, conflict-of-interest disclosures.

5. Bank reconciliations for the last twelve months, signed off by someone other than the person who wrote the checks. Segregation of duties is the most common finding at small orgs.

6. The board minutes that approved every grant agreement, every budget amendment over 10 percent, and every restricted fund release.

This is not glamorous work. It is the work that makes the audit boring, which is exactly what you want it to be.
