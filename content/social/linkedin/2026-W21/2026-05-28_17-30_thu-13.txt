Picture audit week at a community health org. Two pots of coffee and a printer that runs out of toner by Wednesday.

The auditor asks for the trial balance, the schedule of federal awards, the list of restricted funds with year-end balances, and three sample transactions per major program. Each one becomes a half-day archaeological dig because the supporting documents live in different folders, journal entries point to attachments that were renamed, and the bank reconciliations are in a binder that walked off someone's desk in February.

The audit itself is not the hard part. The retrieval is.
