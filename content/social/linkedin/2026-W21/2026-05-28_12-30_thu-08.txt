For the Executive Director carrying both the audit prep and the Q3 board packet this month.

The audit binder, the SF-425, the variance narrative, and the restricted fund schedule do not need to be assembled from scratch every quarter. They need to be the byproduct of how you record the underlying activity.

If your books are clean, the binder is a print job. If the binder is hard, the books are telling you something.

Closing the books is the work. The reports are the receipt.
