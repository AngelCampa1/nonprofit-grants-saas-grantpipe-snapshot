Most "grant tracking" advice assumes you already know which grants you have, what they require, and when each report is due.

If you are running a $2M to $8M nonprofit with a Development team of two and a Finance team of one, you do not have that picture sitting in any one place.

We made a free download for that exact problem.

The Award Setup Worksheet. One page per grant. Funder, award amount, period of performance, indirect cost rate (negotiated or de minimis 15%), reporting cadence, restricted purpose, and the three or four compliance flags most often missed in the first 30 days after signing.

Print it. Fill it out for each active award. You will find at least one date you did not have on a calendar.
