Six things that should be in your audit binder before May 31, in case you want a Saturday project.

1. The signed engagement letter from your auditor.
2. The trial balance as of fiscal year end, tied to the general ledger.
3. The schedule of net assets with donor restrictions, broken out by fund.
4. The schedule of expenditures of federal awards (SEFA), if you are over the $1M single audit threshold.
5. Bank reconciliations for the last three months of the fiscal year, signed and dated.
6. Board minutes for the full fiscal year, including votes on budget approvals and grant acceptance.

If any of those six are not ready, the binder is not ready.

The good news: assembling these does not require a system. It requires an afternoon and a checklist.
