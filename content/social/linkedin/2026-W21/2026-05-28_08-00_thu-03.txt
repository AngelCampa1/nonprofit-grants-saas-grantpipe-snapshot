Quick check: if a federal program officer emailed today asking for the cumulative drawdown total on your active pass-through award, how long would it take you to send a defensible number?

If the answer involves opening QuickBooks, the donor CRM, two spreadsheets, and a shared drive, the number is not defensible. It is a guess assembled from four sources that disagree at the edges.

Drawdown timing matters under 2 CFR 200. You have to draw funds close to when you spend them, document the basis, and reconcile against actual cash needs.

If the source of truth is "wherever the most recent spreadsheet lives", the audit finding writes itself.
