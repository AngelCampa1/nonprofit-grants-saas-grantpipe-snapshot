Three free resources in our library are aimed at Development Directors on small teams.

One. Donor Stewardship Plan Template. A 12-month rhythm for thank-you, mid-year touch, year-end ask, and post-gift report. Not generic. Tiered by gift size.

Two. Donor-to-Grant Reconciliation Template. The two-tab spreadsheet that lets Finance and Development agree on what was raised, what was restricted, and what got spent in the period.

Three. Audit Prep Week-by-Week Checklist. Twelve weeks out from fieldwork, what should already be done, week by week, by role.

Each is a single PDF. Each was written for the org with one person doing two of these jobs and no budget for a consultant.
