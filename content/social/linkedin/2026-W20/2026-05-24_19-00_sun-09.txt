Quick check before the week starts: do you know which of your federal awards are inside their last 90 days of period of performance?

That is the closeout window. Final SF-425 due 90 days after the period ends. Equipment inventory reconciled. Sub-recipient final invoices in. Cost transfers documented and posted before the books close on the award.

If the answer is "I would have to look", look tonight. The closeout package is not a thing you build the week the funder asks for it. It is a thing you assemble across the award, and then sign at the end.

Sunday is the cheapest day to find out one is sneaking up on you.
