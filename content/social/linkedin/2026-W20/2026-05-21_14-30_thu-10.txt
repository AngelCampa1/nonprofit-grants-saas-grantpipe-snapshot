Could you produce, in the next ten minutes, the list of every grant with a report due in the next 60 days, the responsible staff member, and the data source each report depends on?

If your team cannot, it is often not because the data is missing. It lives in three places.

Grant agreements in a shared drive organized by funder. Deadlines in someone's Outlook calendar. Data sources in the Finance Director's head and the program team's quarterly spreadsheets.

The list exists. It is just not assembled.

A real audit binder is the assembled version. So is a real reporting calendar. The system that holds the work is whatever lets one person produce that list at 9:32 on a Tuesday, in the ten minutes before the board call starts.
