A fair board question for any Finance Director: "We have $1.2M in restricted net assets on the balance sheet. What does that actually mean?"

Restricted net assets are dollars received but not free to spend. Donors or grant makers tied them to a purpose, time, or both. Under FASB ASC 958, those dollars are reported separately from net assets without donor restrictions.

Two flavors. Purpose-restricted: the Clinic Capacity grant pays for equipment, not general operations. Time-restricted: a $300K pledge, $100K per year for three years.

When the org spends on the right purpose in the right window, dollars release from restriction. The release belongs on the schedule of restricted activity.

The board's real question is behind the question. They want to know how much of that $1.2M is theirs to use this year. The answer is on the spend-down schedule.
