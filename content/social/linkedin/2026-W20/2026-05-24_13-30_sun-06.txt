3. The number of places one grant typically lives at a small nonprofit.

QuickBooks (the accounting truth). The donor CRM (the relationship truth). A shared Google Sheet (the working truth that finance and development both edit).

None of those three are wrong. The problem is that none of them are complete on their own, and reconciling them is a person's job, not a process. When that person is out for a week, the org loses its grip on its own awards.

The fix is not better discipline. It is a single record per award where finance and development both write, and where the audit trail is automatic.

Worth thinking about before Monday.
