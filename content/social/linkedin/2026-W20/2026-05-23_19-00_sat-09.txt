Period of performance: the start and end dates printed on the federal award notice, between which costs must be incurred to be allowable.

Not the dates the work happens. Not the dates you bill. The dates the obligation is live.

Two things catch organizations off guard.

One. Pre-award costs (anything incurred before the start date) are unallowable unless the agency gives prior written approval, per 2 CFR 200.458. The conversation you had with the program officer in March does not count if the start date is April 1.

Two. The period of performance is not the same as the project period. On multi-year awards you usually get one project period covering the whole thing and annual budget periods inside it.

When the period ends, you have ninety days to submit the closeout package and final FFR. After that, unspent funds get deobligated.

Read the dates. Put them on a calendar.
