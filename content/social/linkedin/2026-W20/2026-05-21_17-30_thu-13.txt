Audit-prep week is often harder than gala week for mid-sized Development offices. The auditor's prepared-by-client list is the reason.

Twenty-three line items. Schedule of restricted activity, SEFA, board minutes, in-kind log, donor restriction documentation, grant agreements, payroll allocations, signed time-and-effort certifications.

The week before the auditor arrives is spent reconstructing parts of that list from email threads and shared drive folders. Not because the work was not done. Because the evidence was never assembled in one place at the moment the work happened.

Audit prep is not a project. It is a byproduct of how you do the work in March, July, October. If the byproduct is not piling up week by week, audit week is the catch-up.
