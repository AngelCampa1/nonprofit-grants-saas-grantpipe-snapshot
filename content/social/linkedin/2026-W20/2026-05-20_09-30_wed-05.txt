If you are the Development Director who inherited a grant portfolio with no documentation, this is for you.

You opened the shared drive. There are folders with funder names. Some have award letters. Some have proposals from three cycles ago. Some have nothing but a budget last edited by someone who left in 2023.

Here is the order I would rebuild in.

First, identify what is active. An award is active if the period of performance has not ended and there is unspent money or unfiled reports. Pull bank statements and ask Finance which funders sent money in the last 18 months.

Second, get the signed agreement for each active grant, not the proposal. The proposal told the funder what you would do. The agreement tells you what you owe them. If you cannot find one, email the program officer.

Third, build a one-page summary per grant. Funder, award amount, period of performance, reporting cadence, allowable costs, match requirement.

Fourth, check the next six months of deadlines. SF-425, FFR, performance progress reports. Anything due in 30 days gets a Monday block today.

You do not need new software for this. You need three days and the discipline to write down what you find.
