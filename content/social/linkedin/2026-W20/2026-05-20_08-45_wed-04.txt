Four federal grant gotchas that are not in the NOFO.

One. Pre-award costs require explicit prior approval in writing. Spending the day after notification but before the start date is not automatic.

Two. Rebudgeting more than 10% between cost categories needs prior approval on most awards. The 10% applies to the total direct cost budget, not the line item.

Three. Period of performance ends at midnight on the end date in your federal time zone. Costs incurred after that date are not allowable, even if the invoice arrives later.

Four. The 90-day closeout clock starts on the period-of-performance end date. Final FFR, final performance report, equipment inventory, and refund of unused drawdowns all live in that window.

None of these are obscure. They are just not where program staff look. They live in 2 CFR 200 Subparts D and E.
