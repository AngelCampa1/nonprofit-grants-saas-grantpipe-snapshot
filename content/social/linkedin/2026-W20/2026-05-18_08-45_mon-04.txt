The Cost Allocation Plan Worksheet, free to download, walks through how to split shared costs across funding sources without tripping 2 CFR 200.

What is in it:

- A worked example of allocating a Program Director's salary across three awards using time-and-effort data.
- The five allocation bases auditors will accept (square footage, FTE, direct labor hours, usage logs, total direct costs) and when each one applies.
- A reasonable/allocable/allowable cross-check column, so every allocated cost has a defensible answer to all three tests.
- Sample language for the methodology narrative auditors expect on file.

Most cost allocation findings come from one missing thing: a written plan that says how the split is done and why. The worksheet is the plan in template form.
