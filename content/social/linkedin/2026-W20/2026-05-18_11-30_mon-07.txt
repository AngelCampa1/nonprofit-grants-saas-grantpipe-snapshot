The Donor-to-Grant Reconciliation Template covers one report. Teams rebuild it when donor and grant records live apart.

What it does:

A side-by-side reconciliation between what the development team recorded as a grant gift in the donor system, and what the finance team recorded as restricted revenue in the general ledger. With variance columns, a notes column for explanations, and a sign-off line for both sides.

It is the conversation that keeps the audit cleaner: where finance and development agree on the same number for the same award, every quarter, before anyone has to ask.
