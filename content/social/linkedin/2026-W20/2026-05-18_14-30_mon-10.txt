Picture the scenario: a program officer calls on a Tuesday afternoon because the Performance Progress Report is overdue. The Grants Manager who owned the reporting calendar left in February. Two weeks of scramble follow, ending in a corrective action plan on file with the funder.

The fix is boring. Reporting cadences belong on the award record, not on a person's calendar. When someone leaves, the deadlines do not leave with them.

That is half of what compliance software actually does. The other half is making the deadlines visible without anyone having to ask.
