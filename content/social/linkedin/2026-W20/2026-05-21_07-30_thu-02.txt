For every Development Director hiring their first grants manager this summer.

Three things to put in the job description that most postings skip:

1. Comfort with a period of performance. Not just deadlines. The shape of a multi-year award where the budget rolls but the dollars do not.

2. Reading a NOFO without flinching. Eligibility, match, indirect cost, reporting cadence, allowable costs. If those words are unfamiliar, training has to be in the plan.

3. Coordination with Finance. Most grant work fails at the Development-to-Finance hand-off, not at the proposal stage. The right hire likes that hand-off. They do not avoid it.

Salary band matters too. A grants manager who can run a federal pass-through portfolio is not a $52K hire in a metro market. Pricing the role correctly is the first compliance decision.
