Five things a federal program officer can ask for, with little notice, that you should be able to produce within 24 hours.

1. Current period budget vs actual, by line item, with variance explanation for anything over 10%.

2. The supporting payroll allocation for any staff charged to the award, with the time-and-effort certifications behind it.

3. A copy of the procurement file for any single purchase over $10,000, including the vendor selection memo.

4. The Performance Progress Report metrics for the current quarter, even if the report itself is not yet due.

5. The current FFR figures, drawn down through the most recent date, with the unobligated balance reconciled to the GL.

If any of these takes more than a day to produce, the gap is the system, not the team. The data is somewhere. The retrieval time is what changes.
