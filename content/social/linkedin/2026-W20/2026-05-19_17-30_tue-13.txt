Four signs the restricted-fund tracking in your donor CRM is going to fail an audit.

1. Fund balance is calculated by summing donations and subtracting a manually entered "spent" number. No link to actual expenditures.

2. Releases from restriction are recorded as a note on the gift, not as a journal entry against the fund.

3. No schedule of restricted activity ties the fund balance back to the GL at period end.

4. The "purpose" field is free-text, so the same restriction shows up as "Youth STEM," "Youth STEM Program," and "STEM youth grant" across three years of gifts.

The donor CRM was not designed for fund accounting. FASB ASC 958 expects net assets with donor restrictions to be tracked and reported with balance-sheet rigor.
