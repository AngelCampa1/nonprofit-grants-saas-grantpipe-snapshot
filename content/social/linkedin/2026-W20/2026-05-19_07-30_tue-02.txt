A practical audit prep resource: the 2 CFR 200 Audit Prep Checklist.

It walks through the seven binder sections that most 2 CFR 200 audit engagement letters call for. Internal controls narrative, procurement file, time-and-effort certifications, sub-recipient monitoring, equipment inventory, drawdown reconciliation, and the closeout package.

Each section lists the documents to pull, the regulation citation, and a sentence on what auditors are actually checking. So when your engagement letter lands in July, the prep is already mapped.
