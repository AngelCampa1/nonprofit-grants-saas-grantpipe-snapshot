Picture a Saturday morning at a $3M nonprofit. The Executive Director opens the gala folder for next month and finds three versions of the seating chart. The development associate emailed one Wednesday. The board chair sent edits Thursday. A volunteer printed Friday's draft and left it on the desk.

She does not panic. She just picks one, deletes the other two, and renames the file with today's date.

The work is not the gala. The work is choosing which version is the one that counts, and making sure everyone else knows which one that is.

This is a significant part of the job at $3M nonprofits. Not always strategy. Not always vision. Picking the canonical copy.

A good system is a system that lets you do that in five seconds instead of forty minutes.
