We decided early not to charge a setup fee. Here is the thinking.

A setup fee on a $329-a-month product is a way of saying we know onboarding is hard, please pay us for the friction. That is the wrong incentive. If onboarding is hard, fix onboarding. Do not bill for it.

So we built CSV import for donors and grants on day one, scoped V1 to eight modules so the configuration surface stays small, and made the trial run on real data without a kickoff call. The trial is a month, no card. If it does not load and feel useful inside that month, no setup fee was going to save us.

The other thing a setup fee does is filter out the orgs we want most. A $500K-budget shop that just got off Salesforce is not paying a four-figure setup fee to try the next thing. They have already paid that bill.
