For Executive Directors thinking about board onboarding this summer.

A new board member should not be handed a 60-page binder on day one. They should be handed three things: the current strategic plan, the most recent audited financials, and a one-page snapshot of the grant portfolio.

The grant snapshot often gets skipped. It should show: total active awards, total restricted net assets, the top five funders by dollar, the next four reporting deadlines, and the program areas those grants fund.

That is the page that turns a board member from a fundraiser into an informed steward. Without it, every board meeting starts with re-explaining the funding base.

If pulling that snapshot together takes more than thirty minutes, the underlying records are not in one place. That is a fixable problem.
