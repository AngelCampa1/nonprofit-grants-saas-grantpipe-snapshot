Five figures and up. Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud implementation quotes for a $3M nonprofit frequently land in that range, before any annual license fee.

The pitch is "configure the CRM to track grants too." The reality is custom objects, custom fields, custom validation rules, a partner statement of work, and a year of admin time someone has to own.

What you actually need is a place where a $250,000 federal pass-through award has its own record, with the period of performance, the indirect cost rate, the FFR cadence, and a document trail. Plus a place where the program officer's contact information sits next to it.

A donor CRM was built for major gifts and annual fund moves management. Asking it to also be a grant management system is a five-figure ask, every year, in time and consulting fees.

That hand-off problem is what we set out to remove.
