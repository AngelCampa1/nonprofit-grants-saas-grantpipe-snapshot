Six things that should already be in your audit binder by May 31.

1. Signed grant agreements for every active award, with amendments attached.
2. The current approved budget for each award, plus the most recent budget-vs-actual.
3. Time-and-effort certifications for staff charged to federal grants, signed and dated.
4. Procurement documentation for any vendor purchase over your micro-purchase threshold.
5. The most recent SF-425 or FFR for federal awards, with reconciliation back to the GL.
6. Board minutes approving any rebudgeting over 10 percent or scope changes.

If any of those are not at hand, the auditor is not the problem. The week before the audit is the problem.
