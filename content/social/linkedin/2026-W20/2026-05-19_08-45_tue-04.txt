If you have inherited a multi-year pledge that nobody scheduled.

Here is what FASB ASC 958 expects, in plain English.

A multi-year unconditional pledge gets recorded at present value the year it is promised, not the year the cash arrives. The discount unwinds as contribution revenue across the pledge period.

A $300,000 five-year unrestricted pledge does not show up as $60,000 of revenue per year. It shows up as roughly $280,000 of revenue in year one (present value) plus the unwind across years two through five.

Most donor CRMs cannot model this. The next four years of statements rest on the data you put in this week.
