Six things that belong on a development calendar before Memorial Day weekend, if your fiscal year ends June 30.

1. Year-end appeal letter copy. Drafted, edited, and at the printer or in the email queue. June 1 is too late.

2. Major donor stewardship calls scheduled. Not the ask. The thank-you and the update on what their last gift funded. Twenty calls beats a glossy report.

3. Board prospect list refreshed. Who is rotating off, who is being recruited, and which of them can introduce three names by August.

4. Recurring giving prompt tested. Most monthly programs are quietly leaking lapsed cards in May. Fix the dunning copy now, not in November.

5. Annual report outline locked. The narrative arc, the program photos, the financial summary structure. Writing happens in July if outlining happens in May.

6. Audit prep date on the calendar. The auditor's fieldwork week, the prep week before it, and the management response window after. All three blocked, all three communicated to the board treasurer.

The pattern: every June workload that feels like a fire drill was an April or May calendar entry that did not get made.
