$1,000,000. That is the federal expenditure threshold that triggers a single audit under the Uniform Guidance.

Cross it in any single fiscal year, across all federal awards (direct and pass-through combined), and your audit shifts scope. New schedules, new fieldwork, new findings categories.

Three things small orgs miss until they are inside it.

The threshold is expenditures, not awards received. A multi-year grant of $300K this year and $500K next crosses in year two.

Pass-through dollars count. State dollars sourced from HHS are federal expenditures on your books for single audit purposes.

The cost is not just the auditor's fee. Internal control narratives, sub-recipient monitoring, time-and-effort, procurement files. None of that gets built two weeks before fieldwork.

If federal expenditures are nearing $600K and rising, start single-audit-ready bookkeeping now.
