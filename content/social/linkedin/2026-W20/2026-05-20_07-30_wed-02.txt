$1,000,000 in federal awards expended in a fiscal year. That is the single audit threshold under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

It is not a per-grant figure. It is the combined total across every direct federal award and every federal pass-through award you draw on during the year.

Three things this catches small orgs off guard with.

One. Pass-through awards count. A state agency hands you ARPA dollars, those dollars are still federal for the threshold test.

Two. The clock is your fiscal year, not the grant period. A grant that crosses two fiscal years can push you under the threshold one year and over it the next.

Three. Expended, not awarded. A $1.2M award you spent $400K of in the year does not trigger the audit. A $400K award you fully spent does count its full $400K.

If you are within $100K of the line, build the binder anyway. Easier to have it and not need it than the reverse.
