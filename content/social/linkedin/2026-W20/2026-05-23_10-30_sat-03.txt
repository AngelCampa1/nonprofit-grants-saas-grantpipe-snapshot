Number you should know before the next federal award lands: $1,000,000.

That is the single audit threshold under 2 CFR 200.501. Once your org expends $1M or more in federal awards in a fiscal year, you are required to commission a Single Audit (Uniform Guidance audit) within nine months of year end.

Not a regular financial audit. A different scope, a different auditor qualification, a different price tag.

We put together a free worksheet that walks through how the threshold gets calculated, what counts as "expended," and what to do when a pass-through award pushes you over the line mid-year.
