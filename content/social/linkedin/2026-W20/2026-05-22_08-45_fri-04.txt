Period of performance: the start and end dates inside which a grantor will reimburse allowable costs.

Spend a dollar one day before the start date and it is a pre-award cost, allowable only with prior written approval.

Spend a dollar one day after the end date and it is unallowable, full stop, with no recourse.

The dates are not suggestions. They are reimbursement boundaries. Put them on the calendar the day the award letter arrives, not the week before closeout.
