Before: an Executive Director asks "where did the Clinic Capacity grant come from?" and gets "I think it was the foundation we met at that event in March."

After: the grant record shows the funder, the program officer, the introduction source (a board member), the proposal date, and every email since.

The hand-off between Development and Finance is where most grants lose their story. Development knows the relationship history. Finance knows the spend status. Neither knows the whole record.

We built one record per grant that holds both halves. Funder details, period of performance, drawdown schedule, document trail, contact history. One row. Two teams reading the same source.
