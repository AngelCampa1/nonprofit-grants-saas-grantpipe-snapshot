$25,000.

That is the federal sub-award threshold that triggers FFATA reporting under the Transparency Act. First-tier sub-awards at or above $25K go into FSRS by the end of the month after the obligation.

People miss it because the prime award is huge, the sub-award feels small, and the reporting tool lives outside Grants.gov.

Three things to know:

1. The clock starts on the obligation date, not the cash transfer.
2. Executive compensation reporting kicks in if 80% of revenue and at least $25M came from federal funds last year.
3. A missed FFATA report shows up in audit findings as a compliance deficiency, not a financial one. Different remediation, same headache.

Put it on the calendar the day the sub-award is signed. The 30-day window goes faster than you expect.
