Most "grant tracking" inside a donor CRM is a custom field on the gift record. That is not grant tracking. That is a label on a transaction.

A grant has its own life cycle. Application, award, period of performance, draw schedule, reporting cadence, modifications, closeout. Some of that lives years before the cash arrives. Some lives years after.

A donor CRM models the gift, not the obligation. So the workflow ends up split: the development side tracks the relationship in the CRM, and the finance side tracks the dollars in QuickBooks. Both teams maintain a parallel spreadsheet for what neither system carries.

That spreadsheet is the audit trail. The audit trail is not in either system of record. That is the gap.

Two systems of record means no system of record.
