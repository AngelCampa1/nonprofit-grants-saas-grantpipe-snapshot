For the Executive Director reading the SAM.gov renewal notice for the first time.

UEI registration is required for any organization receiving federal funds, direct or pass-through. The number itself is free. The renewal is annual.

Three things that quietly cause problems.

One. Renewal is not automatic. If you let it lapse, you cannot draw down funds, even on grants already awarded. Some federal program officers will not even read your performance report until your registration is active again.

Two. The Entity Administrator role is a person, not a position. When that person leaves, transferring the role takes documentation. Plan for this before they give notice, not after.

Three. The financial information you submit on renewal must match what your audited financial statements show. Auditors check this in single audit fieldwork.

Put the renewal date in two places. The Executive Director's calendar and the Finance Director's calendar. Not just one.
