We decided early that GrantPipe would not have a sales team.

Not because sales teams are bad. Because the moment we hire one, the product has to support a different kind of conversation than the one the user actually needs.

Sales-led B2B SaaS optimizes for the buyer. The buyer is rarely the operator. In nonprofit software, the buyer is often a board member or an Executive Director, and the operator is the Development Director or the Finance Manager who never sat in the demo.

That is how we end up with products that look great on a slide and feel terrible on a Tuesday morning.

Public pricing. Free trial on real data. Month-to-month billing. No setup fee. The product has to earn the next month every month.

That is the constraint we wanted. Sales-led companies have other constraints, and they are also valid. They are just not the ones I wanted to optimize for.
