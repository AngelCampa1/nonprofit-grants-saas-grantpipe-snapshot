If your Development Director took next week off, who on staff could pull the spend-down detail on the Youth STEM grant by Tuesday morning?

Not the budget. The actual spend, by line, with receipts attached.

This is the question that decides whether your ops are durable or just functional. One person knowing where the documents live is not a system. It is a single point of failure in a polite outfit.

Sunday is a good night to write down the answer. Tomorrow is a good day to fix it if the answer is no one.
