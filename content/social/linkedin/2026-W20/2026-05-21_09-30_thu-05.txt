Six items most Development Directors should add to their May calendar before June starts.

1. Mid-year donor check-in calls. Top 25 donors by lifetime giving. No ask. Just an update on what their last gift made possible. Twenty minutes each. Block four hours total over two weeks.

2. Lapsed donor list pull. Anyone who gave in calendar 2024 but not yet in 2025. Sort by previous gift size. Write three personal notes a day for two weeks.

3. Q2 grant report inventory. Pull every report due between June 15 and July 31. Confirm who owns each one. Confirm the data each one needs. Teams can discover a report needs program data the program team has not started collecting yet.

4. Audit prep kickoff with Finance. If the fiscal year ends June 30, the audit binder needs to be assembled now, not in August. Schedule the meeting.

5. Board prospect refresh. Board fundraising committee should review the major gift pipeline before the June meeting. Ten names, current status, next action.

6. Year-end campaign architecture. November and December planning starts in May for a calm fall. Theme, segmentation, channel mix, mailhouse calendar.

This is the unsexy half of the job. It is also the half that determines whether September feels manageable.
