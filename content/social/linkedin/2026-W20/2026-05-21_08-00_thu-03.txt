How fast could you produce time-and-effort certifications for the last quarter, by award, signed?

Federal awards under 2 CFR 200 require personnel costs to be supported by records that reflect actual work, not budget estimates. Semi-annual at minimum for single-award staff. Monthly with after-the-fact reconciliation for split-funded staff.

What auditors look for: the certification is signed by someone with firsthand knowledge of the work, dated within a reasonable window of the period it covers.

If those certifications live in an email thread or a shared drive folder named "T&E 2025 final final", the audit finding is already drafted. It just has not been written down yet.

The fix is dull. A cadence, a template, a place where every signed certification ties to the payroll allocation it supports.
