The donor CRM and the general ledger answer different questions.

The CRM says: who funded us, when did the relationship change, what did we promise?

The GL says: what cash arrived, where was it coded, what restriction class did Finance use?

The risk shows up when those answers drift.

A June 1 check worth doing:

Pick five active funders.

For each one, compare CRM amount, grant record amount, GL receipt, restriction code, and report deadline.

If the same funder has five different names, fix the naming before you build another report.
