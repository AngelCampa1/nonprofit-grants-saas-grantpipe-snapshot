Seven tabs I would want in a grant audit binder before a reviewer asks.

1. Award record

Notice of award, grant agreement, budget, amendments, no-cost extensions, and approval dates.

2. Financial trail

Ledger detail by grant, invoice or receipt, proof of payment, budget line, and coding.

3. Payroll support

Position description, payroll register, time and effort record, and effort certification for staff charged to the award.

4. Program evidence

Attendance records, participant eligibility files, output data, submitted performance reports, and source records behind totals.

5. Procurement file

Quotes, RFP, vendor selection notes, contract, conflict disclosure, and purchase approval.

6. Reporting history

SF-425 or funder financial reports, program reports, portal confirmations, and any explanation for revised numbers.

7. Correspondence

Program officer emails, approvals, amendment requests, and guidance that changed the work.

The goal is not a pretty binder. The goal is for a person who did not manage the grant to trace the story without asking five staff members to remember what happened.
