Questioned costs can be a trail problem before they become a repayment problem.

When an auditor questions a cost, the agency has not made a final decision yet. The cost is being challenged because something about it needs support.

Start with four checks:

1. Was the cost allowed by the award and 2 CFR 200 Subpart E?
2. Did the award actually benefit from the cost?
3. Would the amount look reasonable to a careful reviewer?
4. Is the support complete enough to prove the first three answers?

The best time to answer those questions is when the cost is posted.

The second best time is before fieldwork.

The worst time is after the draft finding lands.
