Before submitting an SF-425, reconcile three stories.

1. The grant ledger

This is the spending story. It should show cumulative expenditures from the beginning of the period of performance through the reporting date.

2. The draw history

This is the cash story. It should show cumulative federal cash received through PMS, ASAP, or the agency payment portal.

3. The award record

This is the permission story. It should show authorized federal funds, budget changes, match requirements, program income treatment, and period dates.

The SF-425 sits where those stories meet.

The federal expenditure field should come from cumulative federal expenditures, not current-quarter activity.

The unliquidated obligations field should include actual commitments, not future plans.

The program income field should not be blank if program income exists.

The report is short. The support behind it is where the discipline lives.
