SEFA prep starts with identifiers, not totals.

For each federal award, capture:

- federal agency
- pass-through entity, if any
- Assistance Listing Number
- program name
- cluster, if applicable
- expenditures
- amount passed to subrecipients

2 CFR 200.510(b) tells you what belongs on the schedule.

The cleaner the identifiers are at award setup, the less archaeology happens during single audit prep.
