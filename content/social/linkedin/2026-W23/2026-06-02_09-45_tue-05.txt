A restricted fund release schedule should answer one question cleanly:

What restriction has actually been satisfied?

For a purpose-restricted grant, the release is tied to qualifying expenses. The program did the work, the cost was allowable, and the documentation supports it.

For a time-restricted gift, the release is tied to the date or period the donor specified.

For a conditional contribution under FASB ASU 2018-08, revenue recognition may wait until the barrier is substantially met.

The release schedule should not be a monthly guess.

It should tie each release to:

- source restriction
- expense or time trigger
- amount released
- journal entry
- reviewer
- source document

That lets Finance explain why restricted net assets changed without rebuilding the story from grant folders, donor notes, and old emails.
