12 weeks out from your audit. What should be done by week 10?

We put together an Audit Prep Week-by-Week Checklist that maps the work backward from fieldwork day one.

What is in it:

- Week 12: confirm scope, request the PBC list, schedule kickoff
- Week 10: reconcile every restricted fund balance to the GL
- Week 8: pull award documents, modifications, and ICR agreements into the binder
- Week 6: time and effort certifications for the audit period
- Week 4: sub-recipient monitoring evidence and match documentation
- Week 2: dry run of the audit confirmations and the management representation letter

Each week has the deliverable, the owner, and what counts as "done."
