To the Executive Director who has been quoted five-figure implementation fees for a Salesforce NPSP implementation, plus annual license costs on top, plus a part-time admin to keep it running:

The license price is the smallest number on the invoice.

Real cost lines on a typical NPSP rollout for a $3M nonprofit:

- Implementation partner: tens of thousands in implementation fees
- Licenses: per-user license fees on top, plus add-ons
- Admin time: 10 to 15 hours a week, indefinitely
- Re-implementation in year 3 when the original consultant moves on

What you bought was a platform. What you needed was a product. Those are not the same thing.

If your team spends more time configuring the system than using it, the system is the project. The grants and the donors are the side quest.
