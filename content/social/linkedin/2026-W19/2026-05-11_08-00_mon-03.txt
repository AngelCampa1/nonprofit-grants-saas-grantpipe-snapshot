Your donor CRM was not built for grant compliance. That is fine. The problem is when people pretend it was.

Donor CRMs track gifts. They are good at it. They model a constituent, a gift record, an appeal, a campaign.

Grants need different fields. Period of performance start and end. Allowable cost categories. Indirect cost rate. Match commitment. Drawdown schedule. Sub-recipient flags. SF-425 due dates.

You can bolt custom fields onto a donor CRM until the form has 80 fields and nobody fills any of them. Or you can run grants in a tool shaped like grants and let the donor CRM be a donor CRM.
