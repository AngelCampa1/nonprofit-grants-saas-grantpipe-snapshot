Picture this: a board treasurer asks: "How much of the Clinic Capacity grant is still restricted?"

The Finance Director pulls the balance sheet. $312,000 in net assets with donor restrictions. But that is every restricted fund combined. Not the answer.

The right answer requires the schedule by fund, the spend-down to date on Clinic Capacity, the releases recorded this quarter, and any pending reimbursements not yet drawn.

If that takes longer than three minutes to produce, the system is the bottleneck.
