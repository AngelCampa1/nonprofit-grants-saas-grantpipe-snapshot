An auditor asking which donors funded a specific program last year forces a reconciliation between the donor CRM and the GL. If your org cannot answer it cleanly, it is often because donations and grant-funded program spend live in different systems.

The CRM tracks the relationship. The accounting system tracks the dollar. The grant lives in both and fully in neither. That gap is where audit prep weeks come from.

A unified system does not eliminate the work. It makes the question answerable in one place, by one person, on one screen.
