Six items that belong in your audit binder before fieldwork starts. None of these are surprising. All of them get pulled together at the last minute anyway.

1. The signed award letter or contract for every active grant, with any amendments attached in date order.

2. The current period of performance dates, the indirect cost rate in effect (NICRA letter or de minimis 15% election), and the approved budget by category.

3. SF-425 / FFR submissions for the audit period, with the supporting general ledger detail tying each cash-drawn figure to the bank deposit.

4. Time-and-effort certifications for any staff allocated to federal awards, signed at least semi-annually, or after-the-fact certifications if you use a personnel activity reporting system.

5. Procurement file for any single purchase above the simplified acquisition threshold ($250K under current 2 CFR 200), with the three-quote documentation or sole-source justification.

6. Sub-recipient monitoring file: the risk assessment, the executed sub-award agreement, the most recent single audit report from the sub, and the documented review of their financial reports.

The work is not in pulling these together once. The work is in keeping them attached to the award record as they happen, so "audit binder" is a filter on a system, not a binder you assemble in May.
