If you have a federal pass-through award coming through a state agency this year, the Auditor Evidence Checklist is worth a look.

It walks through what a Single Audit reviewer typically asks for: award letter, budget narrative, drawdown log, time and effort certifications, sub-recipient monitoring documentation, and the FFR filings for each quarter of the period of performance.

It is structured the way an auditor builds their own workpaper, so you can see what they are looking for before they ask. That tends to shorten the back-and-forth during fieldwork.
