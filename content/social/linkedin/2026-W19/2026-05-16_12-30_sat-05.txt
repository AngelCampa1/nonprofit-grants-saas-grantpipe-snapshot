Five things that catch small nonprofits off guard on their first federal pass-through award.

1. The pass-through entity (the state or county) is your funder for compliance purposes, but the federal rules still apply. You owe 2 CFR 200 even though your check came from the state.

2. Your UEI on SAM.gov has to be active and matching the legal name on the subaward. Mismatch on a single character delays drawdowns.

3. Period of performance starts on the federal award date the prime received, not the date your subaward letter is signed. Pre-award costs are not automatically allowable just because they happened after you signed.

4. The 15% de minimis indirect rate is available if you have never had a negotiated rate. If you ever negotiated one, even for a closed grant, you cannot fall back to de minimis without re-negotiating.

5. FFATA reporting at $30,000 obligated lives with the prime, but the prime will ask you for the data. Have your DUNS-era history, board comp, and primary place of performance ready.

None of this is in the award letter. It is in the federal regulations the award letter incorporates by reference. Saturday is a fine day to read them.
