Picture this: three different versions of the same grant budget at a Development office. One in QuickBooks, one in a Google Sheet, one in the donor CRM. None of them are wrong. They are all incomplete.

The QuickBooks version has the GL detail but no funder reporting categories.

The Sheet has the funder categories but the actuals are a month stale.

The CRM has the award amount and the contact but no spend data at all.

Three systems, one truth split into thirds. The hand-off between Development and Finance happens by copying numbers between tabs. Every quarter. By a person whose actual job is something else.

Single source of truth is not a slogan. It is the moment one number stops needing reconciliation.
