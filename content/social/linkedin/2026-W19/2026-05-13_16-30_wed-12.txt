For every Finance Director who has been asked to "just configure the CRM" for grant compliance: this is for you.

The ask sounds reasonable. The CRM is already paid for. It has custom fields. It has reports. Why not?

Because grant compliance is not a custom field problem. It is a ledger problem. Restricted funds need release schedules. Awards need period-of-performance windows. Drawdowns need cash basis and accrual basis at the same time. FFR Box 12 needs an indirect cost rate that travels with the award.

You can put fields on the donor record for all of this. You will spend the next two years explaining to auditors why the labels do not match FASB categories.

The donor record is the wrong home for this. Not because the CRM is bad. Because the shape of the data is different.
