We decided early that grants and funds would be separate entities in our data model.

A grant is the award. The funder, the budget, the period of performance, the reporting cadence.

A fund is the bucket of restricted dollars on the balance sheet. The release schedule, the purpose constraint, the time constraint.

One grant can feed multiple funds. One fund can be fed by multiple grants. A many-to-many relationship via grant fund allocations.

This is not clever. It is how Sage Intacct and Blackbaud Financial Edge model it, because it is what FASB ASC 958 actually describes. Donor-restricted dollars are a constraint on net assets, not a property of a contract.

A lot of CRMs collapse the two into one record because it is easier to ship. Then the audit comes and the data shape will not bend.

We picked the harder shape because the easier shape is what the user works around for ten years.
