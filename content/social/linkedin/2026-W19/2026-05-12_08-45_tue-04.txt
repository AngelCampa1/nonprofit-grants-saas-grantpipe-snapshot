For every Finance Director who has been told "just put the restricted fund tracking in QuickBooks classes":

Classes work for two restricted funds. Maybe four. Past that, you are stitching reports together by hand every month, and the schedule of activity by net asset class is a manual rebuild.

FASB ASC 958 does not care about your software. It expects two clear categories on the statement of activities: net assets with donor restrictions, net assets without donor restrictions. Plus a roll-forward of releases.

Classes give you the dimensions. They do not give you the schedule. They do not link the donor or grant to the restriction language. They do not flag when a time-restriction expires.

If you are doing this in classes and a spreadsheet, the spreadsheet is the system. QuickBooks is the bookkeeper of the system. Worth naming honestly.
