Four signs your donor CRM is not the right home for grant compliance:

1. You cannot store the period of performance start and end dates as required, validated fields. They live in a notes field or a custom text box.

2. The system has no concept of a reporting deadline that is not the same as a renewal date. Grants have both. Donor cycles usually do not.

3. Restricted fund balance is not a calculated value. It is a number someone types in.

4. There is no audit-only read role. The auditor either gets full access or gets a PDF export.

A donor CRM is built around the donor relationship. A grant is a contract with a reporting cadence, an evidence trail, and a closeout package. The two share a contact record. They do not share a workflow.

If you bought a CRM and bolted grants on, you are not wrong to have done it. You are just at the size where the bolt is the bottleneck.
