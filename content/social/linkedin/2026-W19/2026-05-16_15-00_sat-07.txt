If you have inherited a grant tracker spreadsheet, the four-item weekend check below tells you what shape it is in.

One: the period of performance dates on every active award. Not the calendar year. The actual start and end on the agreement. About a third of teams have at least one wrong.

Two: the reporting cadence. Quarterly FFR, annual progress narrative, monthly drawdown request. Write them on a calendar. Real dates, not "Q2."

Three: which dollars are restricted and to what. Purpose, time, or both. Everything else is general operating, and your CFO needs the same picture you have.

Four: who the program officer is and when you last spoke. If it has been more than six months on an active grant, that is a phone call, not an email.

Spreadsheets are not the enemy. Untracked spreadsheets are.
