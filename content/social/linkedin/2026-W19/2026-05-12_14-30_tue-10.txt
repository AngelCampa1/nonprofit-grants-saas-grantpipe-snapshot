Reconciling a donor CRM to the general ledger can surface three patterns: payments booked to the wrong fund, pledges entered twice, restricted gifts coded as unrestricted.

None of these are unusual. All three surface only when someone compares the two systems row by row.

The quiet cost of running parallel systems is not the integration tax. It is the recurring two-week reconciliation that shows up before every audit, every board meeting, and every 990. The work that produces no new value, only confidence that the existing values are correct.

If you are doing that reconciliation more than once a year, the system count is too high.
