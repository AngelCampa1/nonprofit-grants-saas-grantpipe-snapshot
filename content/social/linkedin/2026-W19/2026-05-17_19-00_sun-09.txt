If your Development Director left tomorrow, who would know which grant reports are due in June?

Not in theory. In practice. Whose laptop has the funder portal logins. Whose inbox has the program officer's last email. Whose head holds the unwritten rule that this funder wants narrative before financials.

At a $500K to $10M nonprofit, the grant function often comes down to one person. Sometimes two. The deadline calendar lives where the work happens, which is wherever that one person works.

This is not a software problem first. It is a documentation problem. But software can make the documentation a side effect of the work instead of a separate task that never gets done.

Sunday is a good day to write down the next 90 days of grant deadlines, the funder, the report type, and the portal URL. One page. Shared drive. Done.

If the person who knows this is reading: the page is for the version of you that gets the flu in week two of June.
