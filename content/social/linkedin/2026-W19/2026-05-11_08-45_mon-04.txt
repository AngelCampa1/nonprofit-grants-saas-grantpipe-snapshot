If your SAM.gov UEI expires the week an award notice lands, drawdowns freeze. The renewal calendar is the highest-impact compliance task most Development offices forget.

SAM.gov registration is annual. It is free. It also takes 7 to 10 business days to process if anything trips the entity validation step, and longer if your bank info or notarized letter does not match.

Two rules that save grief:

1. Renew 60 days before expiration. Not 14.
2. The UEI lives at the entity level, not the grant level. One nonprofit, one UEI, even across 12 federal awards.

If your renewal date is "I think sometime this summer," log into SAM.gov today and check. The award letter does not wait.
