Integration tax: the recurring cost of keeping two systems pretending to be one.

It looks like a Zapier line item, a part-time consultant, a staff person who exports a CSV every Friday, and the recurring Slack thread asking why two numbers do not agree.

It compounds. Every new field on the donor CRM needs a matching field on the accounting side. Every change to the chart of accounts needs a sync rule. When something breaks, no one owns the fix because the bug crosses two products.

The case for one record per grant in one system is not aesthetic. It is the recovery of the hours your team currently spends translating between tools.

Worth measuring before the next renewal cycle: hours per month spent reconciling donor records to financial records. That number is the integration tax.
