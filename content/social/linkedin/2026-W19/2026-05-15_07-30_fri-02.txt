Picture this: an auditor sits down at the conference table on a Tuesday morning. They ask for the time-and-effort certifications for the three staff members allocated to the federal pass-through award.

The Finance Director knows they exist. They were signed. They are in someone's email, or on the shared drive, or in the binder from 2024.

Forty minutes pass. The auditor asks if they can come back after lunch.

This is not a software problem. It is a filing problem. But software is where the fix lives now.

Time-and-effort certifications belong attached to the award record, dated, signed, and pulled in one click. Not because the auditor wants speed. Because the trust you build in the first hour shapes the rest of the week.
