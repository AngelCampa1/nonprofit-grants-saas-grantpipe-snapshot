Audit-ready is not a feature. It is a workflow.

The vendors who put "audit-ready" on the marketing page rarely define it. So here is a working definition.

Audit-ready means: every dollar of restricted spending has a date, an approver, a receipt, and a fund tag. The tag matches the grant award letter. The approver matches the policy. The receipt is in the same record as the journal entry.

If pulling that evidence for one transaction takes more than two minutes, you are not audit-ready. You are audit-recoverable. There is a difference.

The 2 CFR 200 retention rule is three years from the closeout report. That is three years of evidence you have to be able to retrieve on demand. Not regenerate. Retrieve.
