$1,000,000. That is the threshold of federal expenditures in a fiscal year that triggers a single audit under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

The threshold counts all federal funds spent in the year, including pass-through dollars from a state agency or another nonprofit. Many mid-sized orgs cross it without realizing because the pass-through portion does not feel federal day-to-day.

Three things that change once you cross:

Your audit scope expands. The single audit covers compliance testing on each major program, not just the financial statements.

Your timeline tightens. The single audit report is due to the Federal Audit Clearinghouse within 30 days of receiving the auditor's report or 9 months after the fiscal year end, whichever is earlier.

Your sub-recipient monitoring obligations grow. If you pass federal funds through to other organizations, you now have documented monitoring requirements on each.

If you are within 50K of the threshold this year, plan as if you will cross it. The audit firm pricing and the internal prep both deserve a head start.
