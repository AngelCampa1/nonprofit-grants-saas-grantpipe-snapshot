ALN: Assistance Listing Number. The federal program identifier you used to call CFDA.

The number itself did not change when the name did in 2018. 14.218 is still CDBG Entitlement Grants. 93.243 is still SAMHSA's Projects for Assistance in Transition from Homelessness.

If your award letter says CFDA, the funder is using old paperwork. The number is still valid. Match it on assistancelistings.gov to confirm program details before you cite it in a board memo.
