If you are negotiating your first NICRA, read this before the call.

NICRA is the Negotiated Indirect Cost Rate Agreement. It is the percentage of direct costs you can charge to indirect under federal awards, negotiated with your cognizant agency.

A few things worth knowing before you start.

The 15% de minimis rate is available without negotiation if you have never had a NICRA. It applies to modified total direct costs. For many mid-sized orgs it lands close to what a negotiated rate would.

If you do negotiate, the cognizant agency is usually the federal awarder giving you the most direct funding. They will want a rate proposal, audited financials, and a cost allocation plan.

A higher rate is not the goal. A defensible rate you can document on every award is.
