Four reports a board member will ask for in any given quarter, ranked by how often they ask:

1. "How are we doing against budget this year?" Operating budget, actual to date, variance by line. Every meeting.

2. "What is the status of our biggest grants?" The top five awards by dollar value, with period of performance dates, percent spent, and any compliance flags. Most meetings.

3. "How is the donor pipeline looking?" Major gift prospects by stage, year-over-year giving comparison, retention rate. Quarterly.

4. "Where do our restricted funds stand?" Net assets with donor restrictions, by purpose, with current balance and release schedule. Annually, plus any time a major restricted gift is anticipated.

If your monthly close cannot produce the first two without anyone "rebuilding" anything, you have a working system. If the third needs a special pull from the CRM, that is normal. If the fourth requires a finance-team Excel project, the system underneath is not built for the work.
