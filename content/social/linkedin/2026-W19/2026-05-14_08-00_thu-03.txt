If a federal program officer called this morning and asked for your Q2 SF-425 numbers, how long until you could read them out?

Be honest. Are they in the accounting system, a working spreadsheet, or three places that do not match?

The FFR (Federal Financial Report, formerly SF-425) wants cash receipts, cash disbursements, federal share of expenditures, and obligations as of the report date. Cash basis, accrual basis, or both, depending on the award.

An org can rebuild it given a week. The actual job is being able to answer in 10 minutes, the day the call comes, with numbers your auditor will not flag in the fall.

That gap (between "we can produce it" and "we can produce it on demand") is where compliance risk hides.
