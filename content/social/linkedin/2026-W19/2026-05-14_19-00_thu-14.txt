Five things a federal program officer can ask for with no warning, that should not require a fire drill.

1. Current cumulative draw against the award, by budget category. Federal share only.

2. The last three months of payroll allocation across this award, with time and effort certifications attached.

3. A list of any cost transfers between budget categories during the period, with the supporting memo for each.

4. Sub-recipient invoices and the monitoring activity you performed before paying them.

5. The most recent SF-425 (FFR) you submitted, plus the reconciliation between it and the GL.

If any of these takes more than 30 minutes to assemble, the underlying record-keeping has drifted from what 2 CFR 200 expects. The call is a signal, not the problem.
