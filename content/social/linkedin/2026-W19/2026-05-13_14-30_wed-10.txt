Five things to check before submitting a Grants.gov application.

1. SAM.gov registration is active and the UEI matches the application package exactly.

2. The Authorized Organization Representative is the person who will actually sign, not the grant writer.

3. The budget narrative justifies every line, including indirect. If you are using the 15% de minimis rate, name it.

4. The project narrative addresses every selection criterion in the NOFO, in the order the NOFO listed them.

5. You have submitted at least 24 hours before the deadline. Grants.gov queue times spike on the last day, and a "received" timestamp after the cutoff is not received.

None of these are about writing quality. They are about the package being technically valid. Most rejections at the screening stage are technical, not substantive.
