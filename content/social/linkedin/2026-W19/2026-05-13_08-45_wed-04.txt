Six things that trip up first-time federal grantees.

1. SAM.gov UEI registration takes longer than you think. Start 60 days before submission, not 7.

2. Pre-award costs are not allowable without prior written approval. The award letter has to say so.

3. The 15% de minimis indirect rate is not automatic. You elect it in the budget narrative.

4. Period of performance dates are not the calendar year. Read the award letter twice.

5. Drawdowns through PMS or ASAP have their own cadence. Build it into your close calendar.

6. The SF-425 (FFR) is quarterly for most awards. Box 12 needs your indirect cost detail.

None of this is hard once you have done it. All of it is expensive the first time.
