Pre-award costs: expenses you incur before the federal award's official start date that you want to charge to the grant.

The default rule under 2 CFR 200.458: not allowable.

The exception: 90 days before the start date, only with prior written approval from the awarding agency. Anything earlier needs explicit agency approval as well.

Where this bites mid-sized nonprofits: you got the verbal "you're getting it" call in March. Start date is April 1. You started hiring the program manager March 15. Those two weeks of payroll are not allowable unless you have the prior approval letter on file.

Get the letter. Save the letter. Attach it to the award record so the auditor finds it without asking.
