Every grant cycle generates four versions of the same question: program officer, auditor, board, funder CFO. Same data, four formats.

The program officer asks how the funds were used. The auditor asks where the supporting documents live. The board asks what we learned. The funder's CFO asks for a budget vs actual.

The work is not collecting the data. The work is reformatting the same data four ways, every quarter, for every active grant.

When people say "grant management software", what they usually need is one structured record that can produce all four views without rebuilding from scratch each time. Reporting is not a feature. It is the consequence of having the right data model.
