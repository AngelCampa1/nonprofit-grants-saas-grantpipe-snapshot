When a funder asks how a grant has been spent, the answer often requires reconciling the donor CRM and the GL. The variance is rarely zero, and it is rarely small.

We wrote a Donor-to-Grant Reconciliation Template for the moments before that email goes back to the funder. It walks through:

- Tying donor CRM gift records to GL revenue by fund
- Reconciling pledge schedules against booked receivables
- Splitting one funder gift across multiple program funds
- A sign-off block for both Development and Finance

It will not fix the system count problem. It will keep the next funder email from being a guess.
