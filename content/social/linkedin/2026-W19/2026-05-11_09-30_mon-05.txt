Eight things that should be in your audit binder before fieldwork starts.

Not "in a folder somewhere." In the binder. Tabbed. Page-numbered. With a cover memo that points to where each item lives.

1. Award documents. The original notice, every modification, the budget on file, the approved indirect cost rate agreement.

2. The general ledger detail tied to each award, by month, with reconciling notes if a journal entry moved spending between funds.

3. Drawdown history. Date, amount, system reference number, and the period covered.

4. Time and effort records. For every employee charged to a federal award, a certification covering the audit period.

5. Procurement files for any purchase over the threshold. Three-quote evidence, sole-source memo if applicable, conflict of interest disclosure.

6. Sub-recipient monitoring file if you pass dollars through. Risk assessment, executed agreement, monitoring evidence, sub-recipient single audit.

7. Match documentation. Cash match has bank evidence. In-kind match has valuation memos.

8. Filed reports. SF-425 / FFR for every quarter, Performance Progress Report on the funder's cadence, and the audit findings response from last year.

If any of these live across QuickBooks, three Google Sheets, the donor CRM, and a shared drive, the binder is not the work product. The reconciliation is.
