For the Grants Manager who is the only person in the org who reads the award letters carefully.

The period of performance dates rarely match a funder's reporting calendar. The budget categories on a federal award rarely match a chart of accounts. And the cost principles in Subpart E disallow expenses that look perfectly normal in a private-funder budget.

The problem is not your knowledge. The problem is that the knowledge lives in your head and a stack of PDFs in a shared drive.

When you take vacation, the Performance Progress Report deadline becomes a roulette wheel. When you leave, the institutional memory leaves with you.

A grant management system is not a substitute for your judgment. It is a place to put the judgment so the org can read it on the day you are not at your desk.
