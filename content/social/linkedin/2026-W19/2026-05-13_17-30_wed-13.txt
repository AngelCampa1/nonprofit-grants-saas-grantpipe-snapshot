$1,000,000.

That is the federal expenditure threshold that triggers a Single Audit under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

If your federal expenditures in a fiscal year cross that line, you owe a Single Audit. A separate engagement, with its own report on internal controls, its own report on compliance, and its own Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards (SEFA).

The line is on expenditures, not awards. A $1.2M three-year award that spends $400K per year stays under each year. A $400K award plus a $400K pass-through plus $50K in smaller awards crosses it.

Orgs can cross by accident, in a year where a pass-through draws down faster than expected. Model the threshold before the fiscal year ends.
