Eight items. That is the full contents of the Auditor Evidence Checklist.

1. Award document and any amendments.
2. Approved budget at award date and current revised budget.
3. General ledger detail by award, period of performance to date.
4. Drawdown or reimbursement records with dates and amounts.
5. Procurement files for any single purchase over $10,000.
6. Time-and-effort certifications for personnel charged to the award.
7. Sub-recipient monitoring file (if applicable).
8. SF-425 or FFR submissions for every reporting period.

If any one of those eight is hard to assemble, that is the workflow problem to fix this summer. Not in October when the auditor schedules.
