For the Executive Director walking into Q3 board prep with a federal pass-through award you are not entirely sure is on track:

The two questions to answer before you sit down with the binder.

First, what is the cumulative spend against the period-of-performance budget, by category, today? Not last month-end. Today.

Second, what is the next reporting deadline, who is preparing it, and what evidence is already attached?

If you cannot answer both in under five minutes, the binder is going to take longer than the meeting itself.

The fix is not a better binder. It is the source data living in one place, with the reporting cadence as a calendar, not a memory.
