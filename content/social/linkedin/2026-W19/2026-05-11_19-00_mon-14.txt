Six things in a federal grant closeout package, due 90 days after the period of performance ends.

1. Final FFR (SF-425), with the cumulative figures and the unobligated balance reported in Box 12.

2. Final Performance Progress Report. What the funder asked for at award, what you actually delivered, and where the variances are.

3. Final invoice or drawdown, reconciled to the GL down to the penny.

4. Equipment inventory if the award funded equipment over the federal threshold, with disposition instructions requested or applied.

5. Sub-recipient closeout confirmations if you passed dollars through.

6. Final indirect cost reconciliation if your NICRA is provisional.

Miss the 90-day window and the funder can deobligate. Deobligation does not just mean the dollars go away. It can show up in the next NOFO scoring.
