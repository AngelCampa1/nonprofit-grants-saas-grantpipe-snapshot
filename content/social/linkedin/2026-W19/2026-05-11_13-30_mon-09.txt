NICRA: a Negotiated Indirect Cost Rate Agreement, in writing, with your federal cognizant agency. Sets the percentage you can charge to indirect.

15% de minimis: the default rate available under 2 CFR 200 if you do not have a NICRA and have never had one. Applies to modified total direct costs.

If you are leaving money on the table at 10%, NICRA is worth the work.
