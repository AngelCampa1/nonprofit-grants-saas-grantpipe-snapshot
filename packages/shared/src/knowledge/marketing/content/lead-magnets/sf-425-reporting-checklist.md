## Before You Start: What You Need to Have in Hand

Before opening the SF-425 form in the submission system, gather:

1. The notice of award - for the FAIN (Federal Award Identification Number), federal agency name, and authorized award amount
2. A grant-specific expenditure report from your accounting system - covering the period from award inception through the last day of the reporting period
3. A draw history from the payment system (PMS, ASAP, or agency portal) - showing cumulative federal cash received
4. Your approved indirect cost rate (NICRA) or documentation of your de minimis rate election
5. Program income records for the reporting period (if applicable)
6. Matching/cost share documentation (if a match is required by the award)

---

## Section 1: Header Fields (Lines 10a-10d)

**Line 10a: Federal Agency and Organizational Element**

_What it is:_ The full name of the federal awarding agency and the specific bureau, office, or division that awarded the grant.

_Where to get it:_ The notice of award. Copy exactly as written.

_Common mistake:_ Using the program office name without the parent agency. Enter the full agency name (e.g., "U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration").

---

**Line 10b: Federal Grant or Other Identifying Number Assigned by the Federal Agency**

_What it is:_ The FAIN - the unique identifier assigned to your award by the federal agency.

_Where to get it:_ The notice of award. It is typically in a format like SM-20-001 or 2021-VA-BX-0012.

_Common mistake:_ Using an internal award number, a grant application number, or the SAM.gov opportunity number. The FAIN is specific to your award, not the funding opportunity.

---

**Line 10c: Recipient Organization (Name and Complete Address, Including Zip Code)**

_What it is:_ Your organization's legal name and registered address.

_Where to get it:_ Your organization's SAM.gov registration. Use the legal name that matches the EIN.

_Common mistake:_ Using a DBA name or program name instead of the legal organizational name.

---

**Line 10d: Employer Identification Number (EIN)**

_What it is:_ Your organization's nine-digit federal tax identification number.

_Where to get it:_ Your IRS determination letter or form 990. Must match what is registered in SAM.gov.

---

## Section 2: Financial Status Fields (Lines 10e-10j)

**Critical rule for all financial fields: All figures are CUMULATIVE for the entire period of performance - from the first day of the award through the last day of the current reporting period. Not just the current quarter or reporting period.**

---

**Line 10e: Total Federal Funds Authorized**

_What it is:_ The total amount of federal funds authorized under this award as of the reporting date - including any incremental funding actions or amendments.

_Where to get it:_ The most recent notice of award or award modification. If the award has been incrementally funded (common for multi-year awards), use the total authorized through the current date, not the full project period ceiling if not yet fully funded.

_Common mistake:_ Using the total project period budget when only partial-year funding has been authorized.

---

**Line 10f: Federal Share of Expenditures**

_What it is:_ The cumulative federal share of expenditures from the beginning of the period of performance through the end of the current reporting period. This is the amount of federal funds you have spent against this award.

_Where to get it:_ Run a grant-specific expenditure report from your accounting system showing all expenditures coded to this award from the award start date through the last day of the reporting period. Sum the total. Verify that indirect costs are included and correctly calculated.

_Critical:_ This is cumulative, not periodic. If you submitted a prior SF-425 showing $150,000, this report should show a number larger than $150,000 (unless you made prior-period corrections, which must be explained).

_Common mistakes:_

- Entering only the current period's expenditures instead of cumulative
- Omitting indirect costs or applying them incorrectly
- Using draws (cash received) instead of expenditures (costs incurred)
- Including amounts not yet incurred (future projections)

---

**Line 10g: Federal Share of Unliquidated Obligations**

_What it is:_ Costs that have been incurred (goods received, services rendered, commitments made) but not yet paid as of the reporting date.

_Where to get it:_ Your accounts payable or accrual records as of the reporting date. Outstanding vendor invoices, payroll accruals, and committed subrecipient costs that have not been paid.

_Common mistake:_ Including projected future expenditures. Line 10g is actual commitments made, not a budget projection. On a final SF-425, this should typically be zero.

---

**Line 10h: Total Recipient Share Required**

_What it is:_ The total match or cost-sharing requirement as specified in the notice of award. This is a fixed number from the award - it does not change unless the award is formally modified.

_Where to get it:_ The notice of award. If a specific dollar match or percentage match is required, calculate the total dollar amount based on the award.

_Common mistake:_ Leaving this blank when a match is required. The field is mandatory if there is a match requirement.

---

**Line 10i: Recipient Share of Expenditures**

_What it is:_ The cumulative documented match or cost share that has actually been provided and documented as of the reporting date.

_Where to get it:_ Your match tracking records. Match must come from allowable sources, be documented with the same rigor as direct costs, and not have been used as match on any other federal award.

_Common mistakes:_

- Using projected match instead of documented match
- Including match from sources that are not allowable (federal funds, costs already claimed on other awards)
- Not tracking match throughout the year and reconstructing it at reporting time

---

**Line 10j: Program Income**

_What it is:_ Gross income earned directly as a result of the federally supported activities during the reporting period. Must be reported regardless of how it is accounted for.

_Where to get it:_ Review program operations for any income generated through federally funded activities - participant fees, product sales, service fees. Track against the program income method specified in the award (additive, deductive, or matching).

_Common mistake:_ Leaving this blank when the organization has earned program income. Program income is tested in single audits and omitting it is a compliance finding.

---

## Section 3: Pre-Submission Reconciliation Checklist

Complete all of the following before submitting the SF-425.

**Line 10f Reconciliation**

- [ ] Ran a grant-specific expenditure report from the accounting system for the period from award inception through the last day of the reporting period
- [ ] Confirmed the report total matches line 10f exactly
- [ ] Verified indirect costs in the report were calculated at the approved rate (NICRA or de minimis) on the correct base
- [ ] If this is not the first SF-425 for this award, confirmed the 10f total is larger than the 10f total on the last submitted report (or has a documented explanation for any decrease)

**Cumulative Verification**

- [ ] Confirmed that all figures on the form are cumulative from award inception - not just for the current reporting period
- [ ] Cross-checked the current cumulative total against the running total maintained in the draw tracking record

**Program Income**

- [ ] Confirmed whether program income was earned during the period
- [ ] If program income exists, verified it is reflected correctly in line 10j and accounted for per the method in the award agreement

**Matching**

- [ ] If a match requirement exists, confirmed that line 10i reflects actual documented match as of the reporting date

**Submission System**

- [ ] Confirmed the submission system for this award (PMS, ASAP, or agency-specific portal - check the notice of award)
- [ ] Confirmed login credentials are current for the submission system
- [ ] Verified the reporting deadline for this period

**Post-Submission**

- [ ] Saved the submission confirmation with the submission date
- [ ] Filed the confirmation in the grant file
- [ ] Retained the expenditure report supporting line 10f alongside the confirmation

## Common Errors Reference

| Error                                     | Why It Happens                                                  | How to Prevent                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Line 10f shows current period only        | Finance staff use period report instead of cumulative           | Pull inception-to-date report; label all SF-425 supporting documents clearly                          |
| Indirect costs omitted from 10f           | Grant-coded transactions don't capture indirect                 | Include indirect cost charges in the grant coding setup at award start                                |
| Line 10j blank when program income exists | Program income not tracked; assumption that it's not applicable | Review program operations for fee income at each reporting period                                     |
| 10f doesn't match the GL                  | Late journal entries after the report was pulled                | Pull the supporting expenditure report on the day before submission and confirm the GL hasn't changed |
| Wrong FAIN on the form                    | Grant numbering confusion with prior-year awards                | Copy the FAIN directly from the notice of award; do not reconstruct from memory                       |
| Late submission                           | Deadline not calendared                                         | Calendar the SF-425 deadline at award setup for the full reporting schedule                           |

---

**Note:** This checklist reflects the SF-425 Federal Financial Report form structure as of the time of writing. Form requirements, line items, and submission systems can change. Verify the current form version and instructions at grants.gov before preparing a submission, and confirm agency-specific requirements with the awarding agency's grants management office.
