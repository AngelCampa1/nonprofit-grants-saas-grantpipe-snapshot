## 2 CFR 200.308 Prior Approval Reference

Before submitting a budget modification request, confirm whether prior approval is required. Under 2 CFR 200.308, federal grant prior approval is required for:

- Changes in the scope or objectives of the project
- Changes in a key person specified in the application
- The absence for more than three months (or a 25% reduction in time) of the approved project director or principal investigator
- Need for additional federal funding
- Transfer of amounts budgeted for indirect costs to absorb increases in direct costs
- Inclusion of costs that require prior approval (certain equipment, foreign travel, entertainment)
- Any subaward, transfer, or contracting of substantive programmatic work not included in the approved plan
- Budget modifications that exceed the award's specific rebudgeting threshold (check the Notice of Award - the standard is commonly 10% of total award for scope-affecting changes, but agency-specific rules vary)

**This template is for modifications that require prior approval.** Modifications below the threshold that do not affect scope do not require a formal request - but they must be documented internally in the cumulative modification log (Section 3).

## Submission Checklist

Before submitting:

- [ ] Confirm this modification meets the prior approval threshold or criteria for the specific award
- [ ] Confirm the cumulative modification total does not exceed the threshold without prior approval having been obtained
- [ ] Complete all sections of this template
- [ ] Attach any supporting documentation (quotes, invoices, position descriptions if personnel change is involved)
- [ ] Confirm submission channel (agency portal, email to grants officer, postal mail)
- [ ] If submitting by email: address to both program officer and grants officer/administrator
- [ ] Include grant award number in the subject line of any email submission
- [ ] Save a copy of the completed request in the grant file before submission
- [ ] Set a follow-up date 10 business days from submission to check approval status if no response received

**Internal pre-submission review:**

- [ ] Executive Director or development director has reviewed and approved the request
- [ ] Finance director has reviewed the budget before/after comparison for accuracy

---

## After Approval

When the modification is approved:

- [ ] File the approval letter/email in the grant file
- [ ] Update the approved budget in the grant management system to reflect the revised amounts
- [ ] Update the approved budget in the accounting system
- [ ] Update the cumulative modification log (Section 3) with the approval date and status
- [ ] Notify program lead and finance staff of the revised budget

**Do not spend from the modified budget categories until the approval is received and filed.**
