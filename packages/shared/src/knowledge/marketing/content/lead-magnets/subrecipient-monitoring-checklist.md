## Before You Start: Confirm Subrecipient Classification

The monitoring requirements in this checklist apply to subrecipients - not contractors. Before issuing any payment to a downstream organization, determine which category applies using the 2 CFR 200.331 five-factor test.

**Subrecipient indicators (all five factor into the classification):**

- The entity determines who is eligible for the federal assistance
- The entity's performance is measured against federal program objectives
- The entity has responsibility for programmatic decision-making
- The entity is responsible for federal program compliance requirements
- The entity uses the funds to carry out its own program, not provide goods/services

**Contractor indicators:**

- Provides goods or services in normal business operations
- Provides similar goods or services to many purchasers
- Operates in a competitive market environment
- Is not subject to federal program compliance requirements

Document the classification determination in writing before the sub-award is issued.

## Part 1: Pre-Award Risk Assessment

**Required under 2 CFR 200.332(b). Must be completed before the sub-award is made.**

**Financial Stability**

- [ ] Reviewed the subrecipient's most recent financial statements (audited if available)
- [ ] Assessed the organization's financial stability: adequate liquid reserves, no significant debt concerns, no indication of financial distress
- [ ] Documented the financial stability assessment in the risk file

**Prior Audit History**

- [ ] Searched fac.gov for the subrecipient's single audit history (if applicable)
- [ ] If single audits exist, reviewed findings from the most recent two years
- [ ] Assessed whether prior findings are relevant to the programs under the proposed sub-award
- [ ] Documented the audit history review with the date searched and findings noted

**Experience with Federal Awards**

- [ ] Assessed the subrecipient's prior experience administering federal awards of similar type and size
- [ ] For first-time federal grantees or organizations new to the specific program: noted as higher risk on the experience factor
- [ ] Prior performance with your organization (if applicable) factored into the assessment

**Financial Management Systems**

- [ ] Assessed whether the subrecipient's accounting system can track federal funds separately from other revenue
- [ ] For higher-risk organizations: reviewed the subrecipient's financial policies and procedures
- [ ] Noted whether the subrecipient has experience with the relevant compliance requirements (e.g., 2 CFR 200 cost principles, specific program requirements)

**Key Personnel Qualifications**

- [ ] Assessed the qualifications of individuals who will manage the sub-award (program and financial staff)
- [ ] Noted any staff vacancies in key positions that create risk

**Overall Risk Rating**

- [ ] Assigned an overall risk rating (low / medium / high) based on the factor-by-factor assessment
- [ ] Documented the rationale for the rating
- [ ] Determined the monitoring approach based on the risk rating (see Part 3)
- [ ] Completed and filed the pre-award risk assessment before issuing the sub-award

## Part 2: Sub-Award Agreement Requirements

**Required under 2 CFR 200.332(a). Must be in the sub-award document.**

The following provisions must be included in every sub-award agreement. Use this as a review checklist before executing.

**Federal Award Identification**

- [ ] Name of the federal awarding agency
- [ ] Name and contact information of the federal award officer at your organization
- [ ] Federal Award Identification Number (FAIN) of the prime award
- [ ] Federal award title (program name as it appears in the notice of award)
- [ ] CFDA/Assistance Listing number for the program
- [ ] Award year
- [ ] Whether the award is R&D (if applicable)

**Sub-Award Terms**

- [ ] Period of performance for the sub-award (start and end dates)
- [ ] Approved budget for the sub-award (by category or in total, per the agreed scope)
- [ ] Description of the program or project to be carried out
- [ ] Performance goals and metrics the subrecipient is accountable for

**Compliance Requirements**

- [ ] Statement that the subrecipient is subject to all requirements applicable to the prime award under 2 CFR Part 200
- [ ] Any program-specific compliance requirements from the original award's notice of award or the OMB Compliance Supplement
- [ ] Cost principles applicable (2 CFR 200 Subpart E for nonprofit subrecipients)
- [ ] Financial management requirements, including allowable costs and documentation standards
- [ ] Procurement standards applicable to the subrecipient's purchases under the sub-award

**Reporting Requirements**

- [ ] Financial reporting schedule (frequency, format, due dates)
- [ ] Programmatic reporting schedule (frequency, format, due dates)
- [ ] Requirement to report any significant problems, delays, or potential compliance issues

**Monitoring and Access**

- [ ] Authorization for the pass-through entity to conduct monitoring activities, including site visits
- [ ] Access to records provisions - the subrecipient must permit access to records pertinent to the sub-award
- [ ] Record retention requirement (minimum three years from the pass-through's submission of its final expenditure report, or longer per program requirements)

**Single Audit Notice**

- [ ] If the subrecipient may expend $1,000,000 or more in federal awards, notification that it is required to have a single audit and must submit it to fac.gov within required deadlines
- [ ] Requirement for the subrecipient to provide access to its single audit or inform the pass-through entity of audit results if requested

**Other Required Provisions**

- [ ] Termination provisions
- [ ] Conflict of interest provisions
- [ ] Requirement to provide prompt notification of any legal matter, audit, or compliance issue

## Part 3: Ongoing Monitoring Activities

**Required under 2 CFR 200.332(d). Monitoring intensity based on risk level.**

**For All Subrecipients: Financial Report Review**

- [ ] Received financial reports for all required reporting periods
- [ ] Reviewed each financial report for: mathematical accuracy, consistency with prior reports, expenditures within approved categories, and spending rate relative to the period elapsed
- [ ] Documented the review date and reviewer for each financial report
- [ ] Followed up on any late reports within the stated follow-up period (typically within 10 business days)
- [ ] Followed up on any discrepancies or unusual items noted in the review

**For All Subrecipients: Performance Report Review**

- [ ] Received programmatic reports for all required reporting periods
- [ ] Reviewed performance reports for: activities consistent with the approved scope, participants served meeting eligibility requirements, progress toward stated goals
- [ ] Documented the review and noted any performance concerns

**For Medium and High-Risk Subrecipients: Documentation Desk Review**

- [ ] Requested and reviewed supporting documentation for a sample of expenditures
- [ ] Verified that sampled expenditures have adequate source documentation (invoices, receipts)
- [ ] Verified that personnel costs in the sample have supporting T&E records
- [ ] Documented the sample selection, items reviewed, and findings

**For High-Risk Subrecipients: Site Visit**

- [ ] Scheduled and conducted a site visit within the first year of the sub-award (or more frequently as warranted)
- [ ] Site visit agenda covered: program activities observed, client/participant files reviewed, financial records sampled, staff interviews conducted
- [ ] Site visit report prepared and sent to the subrecipient within 30 days of the visit
- [ ] Any concerns from the site visit were followed up in writing
- [ ] Site visit documentation filed in the subrecipient monitoring file

## Part 4: Single Audit Verification

**Required under 2 CFR 200.332(f). For subrecipients that may expend $1,000,000+ in federal awards.**

- [ ] Identified which subrecipients may expend $1,000,000 or more in federal awards annually
- [ ] Searched fac.gov for each qualifying subrecipient's single audit submission for the relevant fiscal year
- [ ] Documented the search: organization name, date searched, submission found or not found
- [ ] If a submission exists: reviewed the findings section for any findings related to programs funded through your sub-award
- [ ] If a submission does not exist for an organization that should have one: contacted the subrecipient to investigate; escalated if no response or no audit was conducted
- [ ] If findings exist related to your program: assessed whether the findings require modification of the monitoring plan or any corrective action request
- [ ] Filed the fac.gov verification documentation in the subrecipient monitoring file

---

## Part 5: Documentation for the File

**Maintain all of the following in a dedicated subrecipient file for each sub-award:**

- [ ] Subrecipient vs. contractor determination memo
- [ ] Completed pre-award risk assessment
- [ ] Executed sub-award agreement (all required provisions verified)
- [ ] All financial reports received (with review notes)
- [ ] All programmatic reports received (with review notes)
- [ ] Documentation sampling records (if conducted)
- [ ] Site visit reports (if conducted)
- [ ] FAC.gov single audit verification records
- [ ] Any written communications about monitoring concerns or findings
- [ ] Corrective action documentation if any monitoring findings were identified

**The subrecipient file must be retained for the same period as the prime award records - minimum three years from the date the pass-through entity submits its final expenditure report.**
