## How to Use This Tracker

This tracker is designed for organizations managing 3 or more active grants with mixed reporting requirements. It captures every field that distinguishes a reporting system that produces on-time, accurate submissions from one that produces scrambles and late filings.

**Setup instructions:**

1. Enter every active grant in the Grant Register (Section 1)
2. For each grant, enter every required report type in the Deadline Register (Section 2)
3. Assign a primary owner and backup owner to every deadline
4. Calculate and enter the prep start date for each deadline
5. Enter the submission method and portal/contact information
6. Review the tracker at the start of each month to confirm upcoming prep periods are active

**Maintenance:**

- Update the Status field after each submission
- Add new grants immediately upon award - before spending begins
- Update deadline dates when amendments, no-cost extensions, or other modifications change the reporting schedule
- Review and update backup owners annually or when staff changes occur

## Section 2: Reporting Deadline Register

### Column Definitions

**Grant ID:** Reference to Section 1.

**Report Type:** Use consistent terminology across all grants:

- _Interim Financial_ - financial-only report (typically SF-425 for federal grants)
- _Interim Programmatic_ - narrative/progress report
- _Combined Interim_ - narrative + financial in a single submission
- _Annual Report_ - full annual narrative + financial
- _Final Financial_ - final SF-425 or equivalent
- _Final Programmatic_ - final narrative report
- _Special Conditions Report_ - reports required by specific award conditions

**Reporting Period:** The time period covered by the report (e.g., "Q3 2025: Jul 1 - Sep 30, 2025").

**Due Date:** Exact due date from the award document. Do not estimate.

**Prep Start Date:** The date work should begin on this report. Use the guidelines below.

**Primary Owner:** Named individual responsible for completing and submitting the report. Not a role - a person.

**Backup Owner:** Named individual who assumes responsibility if the primary owner is unavailable.

**Submission Method:** How the report is submitted (portal name, email, postal mail).

**Portal / Contact:** URL for portal submissions or email address for email submissions.

**Status:** Not Started / In Progress / Submitted / Accepted / Overdue

---

### Prep Lead Time Guidelines

Use these guidelines to calculate Prep Start Dates:

| Report Type                              | Prep Lead Time                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Interim Financial only (SF-425)          | 10 business days before due date    |
| Interim Programmatic only                | 5-10 business days before due date  |
| Combined Interim (narrative + financial) | 12-15 business days before due date |
| Annual Report (foundation)               | 15-20 business days before due date |
| Final Financial (SF-425)                 | 20 business days before due date    |
| Final Programmatic                       | 15 business days before due date    |
| Final Combined                           | 25 business days before due date    |
