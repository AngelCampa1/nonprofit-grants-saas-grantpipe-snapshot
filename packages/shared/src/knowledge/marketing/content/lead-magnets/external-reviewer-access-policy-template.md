## How to Use This Template

This template provides the policy structure. You will need to fill in specific details for your organization: the names of responsible staff, the approval thresholds, and the access mechanism your organization uses (the GrantPipe Auditor & Funder Portal, a shared folder, or another method).

After completing the template, have it reviewed by your Finance Director and Executive Director. If your organization has a board oversight committee for finance or compliance, that committee should approve the policy. File the approved policy with your organizational governance documents and include it in your compliance binder.

Review this policy annually, or whenever your access management tools or procedures change.

## Purpose

This policy governs how [ORGANIZATION NAME] grants external parties access to the organization's grant management records and related documentation. External parties include independent auditors, government program officers, foundation program officers, and other authorized reviewers.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that:

1. External reviewers have access to the records they are entitled to review
2. External access is limited to the scope relevant to each reviewer's engagement
3. Access is time-bounded and ends when the review is complete
4. Every external access event is documented in a way that supports the organization's compliance obligations
5. Unauthorized access to grant records is prevented

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all external parties who are granted access to GrantPipe grant management records, shared folders, or physical grant files maintained by [ORGANIZATION NAME]. It does not apply to access by employees or authorized board members, which is governed by the organization's internal role and permission policy.

---

## Definitions

**External Reviewer:** Any individual or organization outside of [ORGANIZATION NAME] who is granted access to grant records, including:
- Independent certified public accountants conducting annual audits or single audits
- Federal agency program officers conducting grant monitoring
- State agency program officers conducting contract monitoring
- Foundation or corporate foundation program officers conducting grant monitoring
- Pass-through entity monitors reviewing subaward compliance
- Other parties with a documented need for access to specific grant records

**Grant Records:** Documents and data related to specific grants or contracts, including award documents, approved budgets, financial reports, expenditure records, time-and-effort documentation, programmatic reports, and supporting evidence.

**Access Session:** A defined period during which a specific external reviewer is authorized to access specific grant records.

**Access Log:** The record of which documents or records an external reviewer accessed during an access session, including timestamps.

---

## Authorization Requirements

External access to grant records requires authorization from a designated approver.

### Approver by Reviewer Type

| Reviewer Type | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| Independent auditor (annual or single audit) | Finance Director or Executive Director |
| Federal agency program officer | Finance Director or Executive Director |
| State agency program officer | Finance Director or Grants Manager |
| Foundation program officer | Grants Manager (notify Finance Director) |
| Pass-through entity monitor | Finance Director or Grants Manager |
| Other authorized reviewer | Executive Director |

Authorization must be documented before access is granted. Authorization documentation includes: the name and organization of the reviewer, the purpose of the review, the grants in scope, the access period, and the name of the approver.

---

## Access Scope

External access must be limited to the minimum records necessary for the reviewer's stated purpose.

### Scope by Reviewer Type

**Independent auditor (annual audit):**
- Records supporting the financial statements for the audit period
- Restricted fund documentation
- Organizational governance documents relevant to audit procedures
- Specific grant records for any grants selected for sample testing

**Independent auditor (single audit):**
- All records relevant to major programs under audit
- SEFA supporting documentation
- Prior year corrective action records
- Does NOT include: donor records, records for grants not in the audit scope, personnel records beyond what is needed for T&E testing

**Federal or state program officer:**
- Records for the specific grant(s) under monitoring
- Does NOT include: other grants, donor records, organizational financial records beyond the specific grant's expenditure documentation

**Foundation program officer:**
- Records for the foundation's specific grant(s)
- Does NOT include: other funders' grants, donor records, organizational financials beyond the grant record

Requests for access beyond these defined scopes require Executive Director approval and documentation of the business reason.

---

## Access Duration

All external access sessions must have a defined end date.

### Standard Access Periods

| Reviewer Type | Standard Access Period |
|---|---|
| Independent auditor fieldwork | Duration of scheduled fieldwork, plus [5] business days |
| Federal monitoring visit | Duration of visit, plus [5] business days |
| Foundation monitoring review | Duration of review, plus [5] business days |
| Document request (no site visit) | [14] calendar days from the date access is granted |

The approver may extend an access period for documented business reasons. Extensions must be authorized in writing (email is acceptable) by the same approver who authorized the original session.

Access that extends beyond the standard period without documented authorization is a policy violation and must be reported to the [Finance Director / Executive Director].

---

## Access Mechanism

[ORGANIZATION NAME] uses [MECHANISM: GrantPipe Auditor & Funder Portal / shared folder access / other] for external reviewer access to grant records.

**If using GrantPipe Auditor & Funder Portal:**

1. The authorized approver, or a staff member delegated by the approver, creates the portal session in GrantPipe
2. The session is scoped to the grants in the reviewer's scope and the document categories relevant to their review
3. The session expiry date is set in accordance with the standard access period table above
4. The portal link is sent to the reviewer directly
5. The access log is reviewed at session close and retained as part of the grant file

**If using shared folder access:**

1. The authorized approver creates a separate, access-controlled folder for the review
2. Only documents relevant to the reviewer's defined scope are placed in the folder
3. Access is restricted to view-only where the mechanism permits
4. The approver manually revokes access at the end of the access period
5. Revocation is documented (screenshot, system log, or email confirmation)

---

## Access Revocation

Access must be revoked when:
- The access session expires
- The review is completed before the session expiry date
- The reviewer's engagement is terminated for any reason
- The approver determines that continued access is no longer appropriate

**For GrantPipe portal sessions:** Sessions expire automatically at the configured date. Early revocation can be performed by the approver or delegated staff member.

**For other access mechanisms:** Revocation is a manual step that must be completed by the approver or delegated staff member within [1] business day of the trigger condition.

The staff member responsible for access revocation must confirm that revocation is complete and document the confirmation.

---

## Access Logging and Documentation

Every external access session must be documented in the access log maintained with the grant file.

**Minimum documentation for each session:**
- Reviewer name and organization
- Reviewer type (auditor, program officer, etc.)
- Grants in scope
- Authorization date and approver
- Access start and end date
- Access mechanism used
- Summary of documents or records made accessible
- For GrantPipe portal sessions: the system activity log export
- For other access mechanisms: the access revocation confirmation

The access log for each grant is retained as part of the grant file for the same period as other grant records.

---

## Policy Exceptions

Requests for access that do not fit the parameters of this policy (expanded scope, extended duration, or access for reviewer types not listed) must be reviewed and approved by the Executive Director before access is granted.

Exception approval must be documented with the reason for the exception, the nature of the deviation from policy, and the controls applied in lieu of the standard policy requirements.

---

## Policy Violations

Granting access without authorization, granting access beyond the approved scope, or failing to revoke access at the end of the session period are policy violations. Violations must be reported to the Finance Director and Executive Director. Depending on the circumstances, violations may require notification to relevant funders or auditors.

---

## Annual Review

The Policy Owner will review this policy annually and update it to reflect changes in the organization's access management tools, reviewer relationships, or compliance obligations. Material changes require re-approval by the original approver.
