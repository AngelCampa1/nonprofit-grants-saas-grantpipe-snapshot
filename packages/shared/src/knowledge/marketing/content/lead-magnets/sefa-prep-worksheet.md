## What the SEFA Must Include - 2 CFR 200.510(b)

The Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards must list, for each federal program:

1. The federal agency name
2. The applicable major program cluster (where applicable per the OMB Compliance Supplement)
3. The Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance (CFDA) number, now called the Assistance Listing number
4. The award identification number (FAIN or contract number)
5. Total federal expenditures for the fiscal year
6. Whether the organization provided federal awards to subrecipients and the aggregate amount passed through

The SEFA covers federal expenditures in the organization's fiscal year - not the federal government's fiscal year, and not the grant period. An award that spans three fiscal years will appear on three SEFAs.

## Part 2: Cluster Program Identification

The OMB Compliance Supplement identifies programs that must be reported together as clusters. Clusters are significant because they are often treated as a single program for risk-based audit planning.

**Common clusters that affect mid-sized nonprofits:**

| Cluster Name       | Programs included                                               | Notes                           |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| TRIO Cluster       | Student Support Services, Talent Search, Upward Bound, etc.     | Education-focused nonprofits    |
| CDBG Cluster       | CDBG Entitlement, State-administered CDBG                       | Housing / community development |
| WIA / WIOA Cluster | Adult Program, Youth Activities, Dislocated Workers             | Workforce development           |
| Child Care Cluster | Child Care and Development Block Grant, state-matching programs | Early childhood organizations   |
| Head Start Cluster | Head Start, Early Head Start                                    | Early childhood organizations   |

**Check the current OMB Compliance Supplement** (published annually at whitehouse.gov/omb) for the complete cluster list. Cluster membership changes; do not rely on prior-year SEFA cluster designations without verifying against the current supplement.

**Cluster identification checklist:**

- [ ] Reviewed the current OMB Compliance Supplement cluster list for all active programs
- [ ] Confirmed cluster membership for each program against the Supplement (not last year's SEFA)
- [ ] Calculated cluster totals as the sum of all program expenditures in the cluster
- [ ] Identified the largest individual program within each cluster (required for certain disclosures)

## Part 3: Period-of-Performance Matching

SEFA expenditures must match costs incurred within the organization's fiscal year - not the grant period.

**Common period-of-performance errors:**

**Error 1: Including costs outside the award period**

The award period is the authorized period of performance. Costs incurred after the award end date (absent a no-cost extension) are not allowable and should not appear on the SEFA.

_Check:_ For each award, confirm the last date of the award period. Verify that no expenditures coded to the award in the GL fall after that date unless a no-cost extension was formally approved.

**Error 2: SEFA period does not match fiscal year**

The SEFA covers your organization's fiscal year (e.g., October 1 - September 30), not the award period or the federal fiscal year. An award running April 1 through March 31 of the following year should appear on two SEFAs - split at September 30.

_Check:_ Confirm that the SEFA total for each award represents only expenditures within the fiscal year being reported. Pull the award-specific GL by fiscal year start and end dates.

**Error 3: Missing late-posted entries**

Journal entries posted after the close of the fiscal year but before the SEFA is prepared may have been coded to the prior year. Verify that the final GL pull for SEFA preparation includes all late entries correctly dated.

_Check:_ Pull the SEFA-supporting GL report after the books are closed and all year-end entries are posted. Do not use a report pulled before year-end close.

## Part 4: Subrecipient Pass-Through Disclosure

Organizations that pass federal funds through to subrecipients must disclose:

1. Identification of each award under which pass-through funds were provided
2. The aggregate amount passed through during the fiscal year per award

**Pass-through checklist:**

- [ ] Identified all subawards issued during the fiscal year
- [ ] Confirmed whether each subaward constitutes a pass-through of federal funds (vs. a procurement contract)
- [ ] Calculated aggregate pass-through amounts by federal award
- [ ] Verified that subrecipients were informed of the federal award information required by 2 CFR 200.332(a)

**Contractor vs. subrecipient determination:**

Not all payments from a federal award to an outside organization are pass-through funds. 2 CFR 200.331 distinguishes subrecipients (who carry out a portion of a federal program) from contractors (who provide goods or services for the organization's own use). Only subrecipient pass-through amounts are disclosed on the SEFA.

## Part 5: Pre-Audit Reconciliation Checklist

Complete before delivering the SEFA to your auditors.

**GL Reconciliation**

- [ ] Total SEFA expenditures reconcile to the federal awards line on the general ledger
- [ ] Each award-level SEFA total ties to the award-specific GL expenditure total
- [ ] No award-level variance between SEFA and GL is unexplained
- [ ] Prior-year comparative SEFA totals agree with the prior-year audited SEFA (where applicable)

**Award Completeness**

- [ ] Compared SEFA award list against the federal grant register / grants management system for completeness
- [ ] Verified that awards with zero expenditures during the year are excluded (inactive awards with no FY activity are not reported)
- [ ] Confirmed that awards closed in prior years with no current-year activity are excluded

**CFDA / Assistance Listing Numbers**

- [ ] Confirmed every CFDA number against the current Assistance Listings database (sam.gov)
- [ ] Flagged any CFDA numbers that changed due to program consolidation or renaming
- [ ] Verified that no two awards share the same FAIN (a duplicate FAIN indicates a data entry error)

**Documentation Ready for Auditors**

- [ ] Award-level GL expenditure reports for each SEFA line item
- [ ] Subaward documentation for all pass-through amounts
- [ ] Cluster identification documentation citing the OMB Compliance Supplement
- [ ] Prior-year SEFA (for trend analysis and comparative)

## Common SEFA Errors Reference

| Error                       | Root cause                                                            | Prevention                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Missing award               | Award not in grants register; coded under a general account           | Maintain a grants register updated at each award; reconcile to SEFA annually             |
| Wrong CFDA number           | Copied from application rather than notice of award; program renaming | Confirm CFDA on the notice of award; check sam.gov for current Assistance Listing number |
| Period mismatch             | Fiscal year totals include prior-year costs or exclude late entries   | Pull GL by fiscal year date range after books close                                      |
| Cluster misidentification   | Using prior-year cluster list; Supplement changes not reviewed        | Check the current Supplement every year before SEFA preparation                          |
| Pass-through amount omitted | Subrecipient payments not tracked against the federal award           | Tag all subaward payments with the federal award at origination                          |
| R&D misclassification       | Research grants not identified as R&D for SEFA                        | Review award purpose and confirm R&D designation with program officer if unclear         |
