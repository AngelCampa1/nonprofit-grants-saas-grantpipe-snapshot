## What North Carolina Nonprofits Actually Need

Three local realities shape the North Carolina software decision:

1. **Strong regional foundation philanthropy** - The Duke Endowment, Z. Smith Reynolds Foundation, BCBSNC Foundation, Foundation For The Carolinas, and Triangle Community Foundation anchor a deep North Carolina foundation network. Restricted grants come with funder-specific reporting expectations.
2. **Federal pass-through grants** - HUD, HHS, USDA, and DOJ dollars routed through North Carolina state agencies (DHHS, DPI, Department of Commerce) inherit 2 CFR 200 compliance. Single Audit applies at $1,000,000 in federal expenditures for fiscal years ending September 30, 2025 or later.
3. **NC Charitable Solicitation License** - The NC Secretary of State Charitable Solicitation Licensing Section issues the CSL under N.C. Gen. Stat. Chapter 131F. License renewals are annual, with the audit threshold at $1,000,000 in gross contributions.

A grant management platform that handles all three is unusual. Most platforms handle one well and the other two as an afterthought.

## The Buyer Categories

North Carolina nonprofits typically fall into one of four software-buyer profiles:

### 1. Donor-led organizations under $1M with light grant activity

Two or three small grants per year, mostly local family foundations. The donor CRM is the central system. Grant tracking can live in a basic deadline calendar.

**Realistic options:** Little Green Light ($49-$159/month) for the donor CRM, supplemented by a spreadsheet for grants. Total annual software cost: under $3,000.

### 2. Mid-market organizations $1M-$5M with active grant compliance

Five to fifteen active grants, mix of foundation and federal pass-through. Multiple restricted funds. CSL audit may apply.

**Realistic options:** GrantPipe (published self-serve pricing) for unified donor and grant management, or Bloomerang plus a separate grant tool. Total annual software cost: $3,000-$7,000.

### 3. Federal grant-heavy organizations $2M-$10M

Federal pass-through dollars dominate revenue. 2 CFR 200 compliance is the central operational requirement. Single Audit applies. Restricted fund tracking is non-negotiable.

**Realistic options:** GrantPipe or a federal-specific platform like Fluxx. Salesforce NPSP with a grants module is viable with $20,000+ implementation.

### 4. Large institutions $10M+

Enterprise scale. Salesforce ecosystem typical. Implementation $50,000-$200,000+.

**Realistic options:** Salesforce NPSP + grants module, Fluxx, or Blackbaud's Grants Management.

## Federal Pass-Through Considerations

North Carolina state agencies pass through federal funds under 2 CFR 200 and the FAR. The compliance requirements travel with the money. A grant management platform supporting federal pass-through must:

- Track restricted fund balances per award
- Document allowable cost determinations
- Maintain time-and-effort certifications for personnel charged to grants
- Produce expenditure reports aligned with the funder's reporting schedule
- Generate the Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards (SEFA) for Single Audit

If federal grants are not a current concern, this checklist is overkill. If federal grants are or will be part of revenue, this is the buying criteria.

## NC Charitable Solicitation License Alignment

The CSL is filed annually with the NC Secretary of State Charitable Solicitation Licensing Section. The audit threshold under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 131F-6 is $1,000,000 in gross contributions. Below the audit threshold, organizations submit financial statements appropriate to their size - many smaller organizations submit Form 990.

A grant management system does not file the CSL for you. But the gross contribution data, restricted fund balances, and program expenditure detail it tracks feed the CSL renewal application directly. Pulling this data from a clean system at year end is the difference between a 4-hour CSL renewal and a 40-hour CSL renewal.

## Implementation Realities

Realistic ranges:

- **Little Green Light** - 1 to 2 weeks self-service
- **Bloomerang** - 2 to 4 weeks with vendor support
- **GrantPipe** - 2 to 4 weeks for unified donor and grant setup
- **DonorPerfect** - 4 to 8 weeks
- **Salesforce NPSP with grants** - 3 to 9 months with implementation partner

Implementation cost for Salesforce NPSP starts at $20,000 and routinely exceeds $100,000 for the grant compliance configuration. For a North Carolina nonprofit under $5M, that figure is the entire annual software budget for five years on a mid-market platform.

## Decision Framework

Three questions to settle the choice:

1. **How many active grants will you manage at once?** Under three, donor-CRM-plus-spreadsheet is workable. Three or more, the unified platform pays back within the first audit cycle.
2. **Will you cross $1,000,000 in federal expenditures?** If yes, Single Audit support is non-negotiable. If no, the requirements are simpler.
3. **What is your gross contributions for the CSL audit threshold?** Above $1,000,000 you need audit-ready financials. Below that the CSL renewal is straightforward.

For most mid-market North Carolina nonprofits, the GrantPipe unified-platform model - donor management and grant compliance in one system, no enterprise implementation cost - is the realistic fit. For organizations with no current grant activity, a focused donor CRM is the lower-cost path. For organizations approaching $10M, the enterprise tier becomes the right conversation.
