## BLUF

Pennsylvania charities register with the Department of State's Bureau of Corporations and Charitable Organizations on Form BCO-10 before soliciting and renew annually. Renewals are due 315 days after fiscal year end. Fees scale by gross annual contributions, from $15 at the smallest tier to $250 at the top. Audit, review, or compilation attachments apply based on contribution level, with audits required above $1,000,000.

## TL;DR

- Who: most charities soliciting contributions in Pennsylvania.
- Due: 315 days after fiscal year end.
- Fee: tiered by gross annual contributions; $15 at the low end, $250 at the top tier.
- Audit required: above $1,000,000 in gross contributions.
- Review or compilation: at mid and lower tiers, respectively.
- URS accepted: with PA-specific supplement and fee.

## What BCO-10 is

BCO-10 is the Charitable Organization Registration Statement required by Pennsylvania's Solicitation of Funds for Charitable Purposes Act (10 P.S. Sec. 162.1 et seq.). The Bureau of Corporations and Charitable Organizations inside the Department of State administers the program. The filing establishes the right to solicit contributions from Pennsylvania residents and must be renewed every year.

## Who must file

Most charitable organizations that solicit in Pennsylvania file, including out-of-state charities whose mailings or online appeals reach PA donors. Religious organizations, certain educational institutions, hospitals, and a few other categories have statutory exemptions. An organization with under $25,000 in gross annual contributions that does not use a paid solicitor still registers, but at the lowest fee tier and with reduced financial attachment requirements.

## The 315-day deadline

Renewal is due 315 days after the end of the fiscal year. For a calendar-year filer, that puts the due date around November 11. Extensions are available by request; they extend the filing deadline but do not extend the underlying audit or review engagement. Filing after the deadline without an extension triggers late fees and can place registration in delinquent status.

## Fee tiers

The BCO-10 fee scales with gross annual contributions:

- Under $25,000: $15
- $25,000 to $100,000: $100
- Mid-range tiers: $150
- Above $1,000,000: $250

Verify the current tier against the PA Department of State schedule before filing; the schedule is updated periodically. Fees do not scale by expenses or total assets; only gross contributions drive the tier.

## Audit, review, and compilation thresholds

The CPA attachment requirement also scales with gross annual contributions:

- Above $1,000,000: audited financial statements required
- Mid-tier contributions: reviewed financial statements required
- Lower tiers: compiled financial statements required
- Smallest tier: no CPA attachment required

Thresholds can shift when the Department of State updates the regulatory schedule. Check the current schedule for the fiscal year being reported. A small organization that grows past $1,000,000 must engage an audit early; a CPA audit started the month before filing is due rarely finishes on time without rush billing.

## URS as an alternative

Pennsylvania accepts the Uniform Registration Statement (URS) in place of the BCO-10 body. Organizations filing in multiple URS-participating states often use the URS plus a PA-specific supplement to reduce duplicate data entry. The fee and PA schedule of attachments still apply.

## Common rejection reasons

- Missing CPA attachment at the contribution tier
- Fee check for the wrong tier
- Missing federal Form 990 attachment
- Incorrect fiscal year reported
- Renewal filed after the 315-day deadline without an extension
- Registration lapsed from a prior year and not cured
