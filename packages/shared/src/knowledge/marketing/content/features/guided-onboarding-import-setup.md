## The problem

A software switch can grow too large.

Teams start with donor records. Then funds, grants, pledges, and old balances
come into scope. If no one sets a boundary, launch turns into a long cleanup
project.

## How GrantPipe solves it

Audit-Ready and Enterprise teams get guided onboarding, import, and setup.

GrantPipe helps your team plan the first import. We review the setup shape with
you. Then we keep launch tied to the workflows you need first.

Your team still owns the source files. You still review the final records.

## What the guided setup covers

Guided setup focuses on the first launch path:

- Which files to import first.
- Which fields need cleanup before import.
- Which funds and grants need review after import.
- Which workflows your team should start with.

It does not create a custom integration. It does not replace source-file
cleanup. It keeps the first launch small enough to finish.

## What happens first

The first step is a setup plan.

Your team names the records that matter most for launch. That may be contacts,
gifts, grants, funds, opening balances, or pledges. GrantPipe helps you choose
the first files and the order they should move in.

The goal is not to move every old note. The goal is to make the first week of
work possible inside GrantPipe.

For many teams, contacts come first. Then gifts can match those contacts. Funds
and grants come next when finance needs the work in the same record. Opening
balances and pledge schedules come after the source files are ready.

## What your team prepares

Your team still owns the source files.

That matters because your staff know the old data. They know which donor names
are duplicates. They know which fund names are current. They know which old
fields no longer matter.

GrantPipe can help review the shape of the file. We can point out missing
columns, confusing names, or a launch scope that is too large. Your team still
fixes the source. Your team still confirms that the final records are right.

This keeps the setup honest. It avoids a false promise that software can clean
every old file without staff review.

## What GrantPipe reviews with you

Guided setup is a review path, not a custom build.

GrantPipe helps review the import order, required fields, and first workflow
set. We also help connect the setup plan to the work your team needs soon:
grant tracking, restricted fund review, reimbursement work, or audit prep.

After import, the review moves from files to records. Your team checks sample
donors, gifts, grants, funds, balances, and pledges. If something looks wrong,
you fix the source file or the record before your team relies on it.

The output is a launch scope your team can use. It should say what moved, what
was checked, what still waits, and which workflow comes next.

## How it fits with self-serve import

GrantPipe also has a self-serve import studio.

Use the studio to upload CSV files and preview rows. Use guided onboarding when
your team needs help planning the order, checking the setup, and keeping the
rollout bounded.

Starter and Growth teams can still use the self-serve import studio. They can
upload CSV files, preview rows, and commit records when the file is ready.

Audit-Ready and Enterprise add guided help around that path. The extra help is
for teams with more review risk, more fund structure, or a launch that needs
closer setup control.

## What waits until later

Guided setup should not turn launch into a cleanup project.

Old custom fields can wait if no one needs them in week one. Old notes can wait
if the current record is enough for launch. Complex reports can wait if the
first job is to track grants, funds, and deadlines in one place.

That does not mean those records are ignored. It means they get a later phase.
The first phase should prove the core workflow before the team adds more data.

## What this does not include

Guided onboarding does not include custom API migration. It does not rebuild an
old CRM. It does not clean every source file for you. It does not promise a
launch date before your files are ready.

It gives your team a clearer path through setup. You still choose what data
matters. You still approve the final records. GrantPipe helps keep the launch
scope from growing beyond the work your team can review.
