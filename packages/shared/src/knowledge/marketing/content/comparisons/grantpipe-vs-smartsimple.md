## Definition

SmartSimple Cloud is an enterprise-configurable grants and research management platform serving large foundations, government agencies, and research institutions. GrantPipe is a unified donor + grant + restricted fund + compliance system for mid-sized nonprofit recipients ($500K-$10M).

## BLUF

The SmartSimple comparison usually shows up on a procurement list because someone Googled "grants management software" without specifying recipient or funder, mid-market or enterprise. The two products are not really competing for the same buyer. SmartSimple is strongest at enterprise scale on either side of the grant relationship; GrantPipe wins for the mid-market recipient nonprofit that needs donor, grant, and compliance work in one operating record.

## Where SmartSimple still fits

SmartSimple is strongest on configurability and breadth. A large foundation with multiple grant programs, a multi-stage review process, and integrations with finance and reporting systems can shape SmartSimple Cloud to fit. A research institution managing federal research awards across hundreds of investigators benefits from the depth of the platform's research administration modules.

For those profiles, the implementation cost is justified because the organization has the staff, budget, and procurement model to support it.

## Where GrantPipe wins

GrantPipe wins for the mid-sized nonprofit where the executive director, development director, and finance director are three people who also carry most of the operational work. That is the normal shape of a $500K-$10M nonprofit running federal, foundation, and individual giving in parallel.

In that environment, the expensive problem is not the depth of an enterprise grant module. It is the recurring cost of splitting donor context, grant status, restricted balances, and compliance deadlines across separate tools.

## Configurability is not always a feature

A common mid-market procurement mistake is treating "highly configurable" as an unambiguous positive. Configuration is work. Configuration requires admin staff or paid services. Configuration drift erodes consistency over time.

GrantPipe is configurable in the places mid-market nonprofits actually need (custom fields on contacts, donations, grants; report templates; user roles), but the recipient workflow is shaped out of the box. SmartSimple's strength is letting a large customer build their own platform; that strength becomes a tax on a smaller customer.

## Pricing reality

SmartSimple is quote-based enterprise contracting. Public procurement disclosures and review platforms indicate annual contracts of $25,000-$100,000+ depending on user count, module selection, and customization scope. Implementation is typically 3-9 months and a separate line.

GrantPipe is flat-rate SaaS - Starter, Growth, and Audit-Ready published tiers - with no implementation retainer. The economics reflect the different target customers.

## Federal compliance

For a recipient nonprofit, 2 CFR 200 obligations are non-negotiable:

- SF-425 due 30 days after each calendar quarter, final SF-425 90 days after period end
- 2 CFR 200.332 subrecipient monitoring with risk assessment
- FFATA subaward reporting above $30,000 to USASpending.gov via FSRS
- 3-year record retention under 2 CFR 200.334
- Single audit at $1M in federal expenditures
- 15% de minimis indirect cost rate available without negotiation

GrantPipe treats these as first-class objects in the recipient workflow. SmartSimple can be configured to track them, but the configuration is the customer's project.

## How to choose

Choose SmartSimple if:

- You are a foundation, government agency, or research institution
- Annual budget for the system is $25K+ and implementation timeline of 3-9 months is acceptable
- You have admin staff or services budget to configure and maintain the platform
- Your scale justifies enterprise-grade depth

Choose GrantPipe if:

- You are a $500K-$10M nonprofit recipient
- Donors, grants, restricted funds, and compliance need to share an operating record
- Self-serve setup and flat monthly pricing match your reality
- Federal grant compliance is a workflow concern, not a configuration project
