## How AmpliFund Pricing Works

AmpliFund prices by tier, user count, and modules. Essentials starts in the $15,000 to $20,000 range. Professional typically sits at $25,000 to $35,000. Enterprise crosses $40,000 and can exceed $50,000 for government or large nonprofit deployments.

Since the Bonterra acquisition, AmpliFund is part of a larger portfolio. That changes the sales conversation in practice: quotes sometimes bundle related Bonterra products, which can inflate the total if the buyer is not careful about scoping.

## What Each Tier Actually Covers

**Essentials ($15,000-$20,000/year)** covers the core grant lifecycle: tracking awards, budget versus actuals, and standard reporting. For a small nonprofit managing a handful of federal grants, it is the baseline.

**Professional ($25,000-$35,000/year)** adds user capacity, subrecipient monitoring (for pass-through entities), and accounting integrations. Most mid-sized nonprofit customers land here.

**Enterprise ($40,000-$50,000+/year)** adds unlimited users, custom configurations, and a dedicated success manager. Typically state agencies and large nonprofits.

**Government / Pass-Through (custom)** is the quote for state and county grant offices distributing federal pass-through funds to subrecipients.

## The Hidden Costs

Beyond the base subscription:

- **Implementation fee**: $5,000 to $20,000 depending on scope
- **Data migration**: quoted separately for historical grants and expenditures
- **Additional user seats**: billed per seat on higher tiers
- **Premium support tiers**: 10-20% add-on
- **API and advanced integrations**: gated to Professional or Enterprise
- **Annual price escalation**: 5-8% at renewal
- **Portfolio bundling**: Bonterra quotes sometimes include additional modules
- **A separate donor CRM**: AmpliFund does not include donor management

For a mid-sized nonprofit evaluating AmpliFund Professional, realistic first-year cost with implementation and a donor CRM is $32,000 to $40,000.

## What You Actually Pay

AmpliFund is powerful. For large nonprofits and government agencies running substantial federal grant portfolios, the depth is defensible.

For mid-sized nonprofits, the math rarely works. The combined cost of AmpliFund plus a donor CRM plus the admin time to reconcile both against accounting consistently exceeds $25,000 per year in year one. That is 10x GrantPipe's entry tier.

The buying question is not "is AmpliFund good software?" (it is). The question is whether your nonprofit needs enterprise-grade federal grant depth, or whether you need a focused mid-market product that handles federal grants alongside donors.

## Budgeting the First Year Realistically

Model three buckets: subscription, implementation, and process overhead. AmpliFund's visible number is the first bucket. Implementation is a discrete one-time line. Process overhead is ongoing: admin time, integration maintenance, and the donor CRM subscription you still need.

If your total first-year budget for donor and grant software is under $20,000, AmpliFund will not fit. If your budget is above $50,000 and your federal grants are substantial, AmpliFund earns the seat.
