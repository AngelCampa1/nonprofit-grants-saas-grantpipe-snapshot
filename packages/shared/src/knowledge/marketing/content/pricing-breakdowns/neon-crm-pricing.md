## How Neon CRM Pricing Works

Neon CRM starts at $99/month. Neon One describes the CRM plan as revenue-based, which means the starting price is only the entry point. The public pricing page also lists product add-ons as subscription percentage increases: memberships and volunteers at +10%, and events at +20%.

That model is useful for nonprofits that want a broad suite and are comfortable scaling the subscription as their program mix grows. It is less simple for a team trying to compare CRM cost in one spreadsheet cell, because the right number depends on revenue, products, and add-ons.

## Where Costs Accumulate

The most important budgeting step is to separate the base CRM from the modules that become operationally necessary.

Events and membership management are common nonprofit needs, but they still change the bill. A nonprofit that starts with donor management and later adds events or membership workflows should expect the subscription to move. A grant-funded nonprofit should also test whether Neon CRM's grant management coverage is enough for restricted fund compliance, not just grant notes or deadlines.

## Grant Compliance Gaps

Neon One lists grant management in Neon CRM feature coverage. That is a real improvement over treating Neon as donor-only software, but it should not be read as automatic support for the full post-award lifecycle.

Restricted fund balances, budget-to-actual reporting by award, reimbursement documentation, closeout tasks, and audit exports are the workflows to test. If those workflows live in spreadsheets after implementation, the real cost includes the time and risk of maintaining those side processes.

## GrantPipe: Published Pricing for Grant-Active Teams

GrantPipe's pricing is structured differently:

- **Starter ({{grantpipe.price.starterMonthly}}):** Donor CRM, grant pipeline tracking, compliance calendar, 990 export templates, email support
- **Growth ({{grantpipe.price.growthMonthly}}):** Everything in Starter, plus spend-down threshold alerts, the compliance report pack, and proposal and report drafting
- **Audit-Ready ({{grantpipe.price.auditReadyMonthly}}):** Everything in Growth, plus evidence packages, advanced fund accounting, financial statements and board-ready outputs, guided onboarding, and the Auditor & Funder Portal

GrantPipe publishes flat pricing. Grant compliance is not an add-on. Grant pipeline tracking, the compliance calendar, and basic restricted-fund visibility ship on every plan; the full restriction lifecycle and compliance report pack on Growth; advanced fund accounting on Audit-Ready.

## How To Budget The First Year

A safer budgeting approach is to model year-one cost in three buckets: subscription, implementation effort, and process overhead. Subscription is the visible number. Implementation effort includes migration, cleanup, onboarding, and any partner help. Process overhead is the hidden cost of exports, spreadsheet reconciliation, and report reformatting that continues after launch.

For Neon CRM, include the $99/month starting point, revenue-based tiering, product add-ons, implementation effort, and any grant compliance workarounds. For GrantPipe, compare the published plan price against the cost of replacing separate donor, grant, fund, document, and reporting workflows.

Ask the same question for every vendor in the shortlist: what work still happens outside the system after launch? If grant files, restricted balances, reimbursement notes, or audit support still live in shared drives and spreadsheets, the CRM price is only one part of the operating cost.
