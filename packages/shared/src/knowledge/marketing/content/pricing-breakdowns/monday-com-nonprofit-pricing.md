## Monday.com's Pricing Structure

Monday.com prices by seat and tier. The tiers are Free (2 seats, no real utility for teams), Basic ($9/seat/month annual), Standard ($12/seat/month annual), and Pro ($19/seat/month annual). Enterprise pricing is custom. Monthly billing adds approximately 18% to each tier.

For nonprofits, the 70% discount through Monday.com's nonprofit program changes the math considerably. The discount applies to paid plans and requires verification as a registered 501(c)(3). It is not automatic - the organization has to apply and receive approval.

## What Each Tier Actually Gives You

**Free (2 seats)** is largely a trial tier. Two seats and three boards cannot support a development operation with multiple staff and multiple active grants.

**Basic ($9/seat/month, ~$2.70 with discount)** is the entry point for a real team. Unlimited seats, unlimited items, and basic dashboards. No automations or integrations at meaningful volume. For deadline tracking and status boards, it is functional.

**Standard ($12/seat/month, ~$3.60 with discount)** adds timeline views, guest access, and 250 automation and integration actions per month. The Gantt view is useful for grant timelines. Automations at 250 actions/month are limiting for organizations with active workflows.

**Pro ($19/seat/month, ~$5.70 with discount)** is where the platform becomes genuinely capable: 25,000 automation and integration actions, private boards, time tracking, and full dashboard functionality. For a nonprofit that wants to build a comprehensive grant tracking system in Monday.com, Pro is likely the required tier.

## The Real Cost for a 5-Person Development Team

At the Basic tier with nonprofit discount: 5 seats x $2.70/month x 12 months = **$162/year**.

At Standard: **$216/year**.

At Pro: **$342/year**.

These numbers look dramatically lower than purpose-built nonprofit software. The comparison only holds if Monday.com can do what the organization actually needs. And that is where the analysis gets more complicated.

## What $162-$342 Per Year Does Not Include

Monday.com's price is for project management boards. The following are not included at any price tier:

**Restricted fund tracking.** There is no financial data layer. A grant's restricted fund balance cannot be tracked, enforced, or reported in Monday.com. See [restricted fund tracking](/features/restricted-fund-tracking) for what that capability requires.

**Grant compliance reporting.** SF-425 filings, budget-vs-actual by grant, and compliance documentation packages for auditors all require financial data that Monday.com does not have. These reports have to be assembled from your accounting software.

**Donor CRM.** Individual donor records, giving histories, major gift pipeline, and donor retention analytics are not features of Monday.com. A separate donor CRM subscription - Bloomerang, Little Green Light, or similar - is required for a complete development operation, adding $99 to $300+/month.

**[Grant pipeline management](/features/grant-pipeline-management)** that includes financial data and compliance documentation is a different product category than what Monday.com provides.

## The True Total Cost

A nonprofit using Monday.com Pro plus a donor CRM plus manual compliance work:

| Line item                                                               | Annual cost |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Monday.com Pro (5 seats, nonprofit discount)                            | $342        |
| Donor CRM (entry tier)                                                  | $1,188      |
| Staff time for manual compliance reporting (est. 6 hrs/month at $31/hr) | $2,232      |
| **Total**                                                               | **$3,762**  |

GrantPipe at the Starter tier uses published annual pricing for unlimited users, with grant compliance, restricted fund tracking, and donor CRM included. No additional donor CRM subscription, no hours of manual compliance assembly.

The Monday.com discount is real. The capability gap is also real.

## When Monday.com Is the Right Purchase

Monday.com's discounted pricing makes sense for nonprofits that:

- Need project management and deadline tracking, not financial compliance
- Have separate systems handling grant finances and donor management
- Are managing a small grant portfolio with no federal compliance requirements
- Want a tool all staff can adopt quickly without training
