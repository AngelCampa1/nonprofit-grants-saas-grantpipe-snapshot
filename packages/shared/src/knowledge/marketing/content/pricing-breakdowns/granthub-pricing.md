# GrantHub Pricing: Foundant Grant Tracker Cost Breakdown

GrantHub occupies a specific niche in the grant management software market: pre-award pipeline tracking for grant seekers at small to mid-sized nonprofits. It does deadline management, funder relationship tracking, and grant calendaring well. It does not do post-award compliance, restricted fund accounting, or donor management.

Understanding GrantHub's pricing requires understanding its scope. Organizations that purchase GrantHub expecting full grant lifecycle management - from pipeline through compliance reporting - will find gaps that require additional tools.

## What GrantHub Does

GrantHub is Foundant Technologies' entry-level grant management product. Its core features:

- **Grant deadline calendar:** Track application deadlines, report due dates, and funder outreach timing
- **Funder database:** Store funder contacts, past grant history, and relationship notes
- **Grant pipeline:** Visualize the status of active grant applications
- **Document storage:** Attach grant documents, applications, and correspondence to funder records
- **Basic reporting:** Reports on grants applied for, awarded, and pending

What GrantHub does not include in its core offering:

- Post-award expenditure tracking
- Restricted fund accounting
- Federal compliance reports (SF-425, SEFA)
- Donor/constituent CRM
- Indirect cost rate tracking
- Time-and-effort certification

Organizations that need any of these capabilities typically add Foundant GLM (Foundant's more expensive full-lifecycle platform) or a separate compliance tool.

## Pricing Structure

Foundant does not publish GrantHub list pricing on its website; pricing is provided through a sales consultation. Based on G2 reviews and user-reported pricing data:

| Tier         | Approximate range  | Typical fit                       |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Entry        | $1,500-$2,500/year | 1-2 users, under 25 active grants |
| Mid          | $2,500-$4,000/year | 3-5 users, 25-75 active grants    |
| Organization | $4,000+/year       | 5+ users, 75+ active grants       |

Pricing scales by user count and active grant volume. The price curve bends noticeably around 50 active grants and three or more users - the point where many organizations start comparing GrantHub's cost against comprehensive platform alternatives.

## Hidden Costs

Beyond the annual subscription:

**Implementation:** Foundant typically provides onboarding assistance; additional setup consulting is available at hourly rates. Organizations with complex funder databases or grant history needing migration may spend several hours of paid setup time.

**Data migration:** Moving grant history from spreadsheets, Salesforce, or another system into GrantHub requires data preparation. This is typically a staff-time cost rather than a vendor fee, but it is real.

**Add-on features:** Some reporting and integration features that users expect to be standard require add-on subscriptions or partner integrations with additional costs.

**Multiple tools:** The most significant hidden cost for organizations with federal awards is that GrantHub does not replace the need for a compliance tool. Organizations paying for GrantHub plus a spreadsheet-based compliance system (or a separate compliance tool) are paying for two systems that don't communicate.

## When GrantHub Makes Sense vs. When to Consider Alternatives

**GrantHub is a good fit when:**

- Pre-award pipeline management is the primary need
- Grant volume is under 30 active grants
- Federal compliance requirements are minimal
- Budget is tight and full-lifecycle features are not yet required

**Consider alternatives when:**

- The organization has federal awards requiring SEFA, SF-425, or NICRA compliance
- Donor management is needed alongside grant management
- Restricted fund tracking is required for board or auditor reporting
- The team has outgrown the user limit and per-seat costs are climbing

GrantPipe covers the full lifecycle - pipeline, post-award compliance, restricted fund tracking, and donor CRM - at a flat annual price that doesn't increase per user. For organizations currently using GrantHub plus spreadsheets for compliance, GrantPipe typically consolidates both into a single system at comparable or lower total cost.
