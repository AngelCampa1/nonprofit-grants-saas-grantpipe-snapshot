GrantHub Pro is gone. The question now is what to replace it with - and that question is less straightforward than it first appears, because the answer depends entirely on what you were actually using GrantHub to do.

## The Two Types of GrantHub Users

Most GrantHub Pro users fell into one of two categories.

**Category one: Pipeline managers.** These organizations used GrantHub primarily to track grant applications, manage deadline calendars, and maintain funder contact records. The pipeline view gave them visibility into the grant pursuit workflow. They managed post-award compliance separately - either in their accounting system, in compliance spreadsheets, or both.

For Category one users, almost any modern grant tracking tool replaces the visible function GrantHub served. The question is whether they want to continue managing post-award compliance separately or solve both problems at once.

**Category two: Hybrid managers.** These organizations used GrantHub for pipeline visibility and relied on compliance spreadsheets alongside it for post-award management. The spreadsheets were a known limitation - functional but fragile, increasingly burdensome as the grant portfolio grew.

For Category two users, a direct GrantHub replacement that only replicates the pipeline tracking function re-creates the limitation. The replacement they actually need handles post-award compliance as well.

Most organizations that have reached the point of evaluating GrantHub replacements are Category two users. The value of the migration window is the opportunity to close the gap - not just to find a system that does what GrantHub did.

## What to Evaluate

Before evaluating vendors, be clear about your requirements. Rate each of the following by importance for your organization:

**Pre-award grant discovery** - finding new grant opportunities, prospect research, compatibility matching. If this is critical, prioritize tools with strong funder databases.

**Application pipeline management** - tracking applications by stage, deadline calendars, submission history. Every tool on this list covers this adequately.

**Post-award restricted fund tracking** - modeling the approved budget by category, tracking expenditures against it, running balance at any point in the grant period. This is the compliance core. Most donor CRMs and lightweight trackers do not cover it.

**Compliance report generation** - producing SF-425 quarterly reports, foundation narrative-plus-financial reports, and custom funder formats from actual expenditure data. Significant time savings if your compliance reports currently require manual data extraction.

**Donor CRM** - individual donor records, giving history, pledge management, retention analytics. If you need a separate donor CRM, factor that cost into any comparison.

**Migration support** - does the platform offer tools or documentation for importing GrantHub export files? How smooth is the data transition?

## The Recommendation Summary

**Choose GrantPipe if:** You need post-award compliance management alongside donor CRM, and you want to replace both GrantHub and your current donor CRM with one system. Best fit for mid-sized nonprofits ($500K-$10M budget) managing 5-20 active grants with at least some restricted funds.

**Choose Instrumentl if:** Pre-award grant discovery is your primary challenge and you are willing to manage a separate donor CRM and compliance workflow. Best fit for organizations with large grant application volumes (30+ per year) where finding the right opportunities is the bottleneck.

**Choose Salesforce Nonprofit if:** Your organization is large, has dedicated Salesforce administrators, and needs a platform that can be configured to match highly complex workflows. Budget $20,000-$50,000+ for implementation. Not appropriate for organizations that want to reduce system complexity.

**Choose Neon CRM if:** Your primary challenge is donor management and your grant compliance requirements are genuinely minimal. Understand that the grant management gap from GrantHub will persist.

**Do not choose Submittable** as a GrantHub replacement. It is a grantmaker-facing platform. You may interact with it as an applicant - you would not own a subscription to it.
