## What Nimble AMS Is Built For

Nimble AMS is an association management system - software designed specifically for professional associations, trade groups, and membership organizations. Its core functions are membership records, chapter management, event registration, and certification tracking. It runs on top of Salesforce, inheriting that platform's data model and integration ecosystem.

For the right organization, Nimble AMS is a legitimate product. A national professional association managing 10,000 members across regional chapters, with certification programs, annual conferences, and member benefit tracking, has a genuine use case for what Nimble AMS provides.

That organization is not a service delivery nonprofit.

## The Salesforce Problem

Every Nimble AMS customer is also a Salesforce customer. That means Salesforce licenses (typically $25-$75/user/month), a Salesforce administrator on staff or on retainer, and the ongoing reality that any configuration, integration, or support question operates inside the Salesforce ecosystem's complexity.

Salesforce implementation projects for nonprofits - even on the NPSP or Nimble AMS track - routinely cost $15,000-$75,000 in year-one consulting fees. That is before the annual subscription and licensing cost.

For a mid-sized nonprofit that needs donor relationship management and grant compliance, this cost structure is purchasing capabilities they will never use in exchange for complexity they will spend years managing.

## Where Nimble AMS Falls Short for Service Nonprofits

**Association features you do not need.** Membership tiers, chapter administration, certification tracking, member benefit management - these are not features that a housing nonprofit, legal aid organization, or community health center uses. You pay for them regardless.

**Grant compliance is an afterthought.** Nimble AMS is not designed for grant lifecycle management. The Salesforce NPSP foundation has basic grant tracking capabilities, but restricted fund allocation, expenditure tracking against specific grant budgets, and compliance report generation require additional custom development or separate tools on top of an already expensive stack.

**Implementation timeline.** Salesforce-based implementations take time. Nimble AMS implementations are typically 6-12 months, requiring external consultants for configuration. Organizations do not start realizing value until the implementation is complete.

## What GrantPipe Offers Instead

GrantPipe was designed from the ground up for mid-sized service delivery nonprofits - the organizations that manage both donor relationships and active grant portfolios without a membership program to complicate the picture.

The platform is a single unified codebase with no external platform dependencies. No Salesforce licenses. No Salesforce administrator. No consulting firm required for standard onboarding.

**What is included from day one:**

- Donor CRM with contact management, giving history, segmentation, and retention analytics
- Grant lifecycle management from application through award, allocation, and closeout
- Restricted fund tracking against specific programs and grant budgets
- Compliance reporting for funder requirements without secondary tools
- Audit trail connecting grants, funds, and transactions

Most organizations are operational within 2-4 weeks without external help.

## Pricing Reality

Nimble AMS total year-one cost for a mid-sized organization - Salesforce licenses, Nimble AMS subscription, implementation consulting - is typically $15,000-$50,000+.

GrantPipe Starter has published pricing, with self-serve onboarding included. The price difference is not a matter of scale or features at the margin. It is a fundamental difference in the problem each platform is solving.

## When Nimble AMS Is the Right Choice

Nimble AMS makes sense for professional associations that manage member benefit programs, chapter structures, and certification tracking alongside fundraising. If membership is the central relationship in your organization - not donor relationships or grant compliance - Nimble AMS on Salesforce is a reasonable product to evaluate.

For the vast majority of 501(c)(3) service delivery nonprofits that are considering it as a general-purpose CRM, the answer is almost certainly no: the association features are overhead, the Salesforce dependency adds ongoing cost and complexity, and the grant compliance layer is underdeveloped relative to what a mid-sized organization actually needs.
