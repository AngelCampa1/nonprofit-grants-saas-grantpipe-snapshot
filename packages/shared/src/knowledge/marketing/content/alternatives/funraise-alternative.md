## What Funraise Does Well

Funraise entered the nonprofit market as a cleaner alternative to form builders that were not designed for fundraising. Its donation pages load quickly, the peer-to-peer fundraiser setup is accessible to non-technical staff, and the campaign management layer gives development teams a reasonable view of what is performing.

For nonprofits at an early stage of formalization - building their first structured donor program, running their first major campaign - Funraise is a workable tool. The onboarding is faster than enterprise platforms, and the donor-facing experience is better than generic form builders.

## Where Funraise Stops Short

The limitation becomes visible when an organization outgrows the campaign layer and needs the relationship management layer.

**Donor relationship depth.** Funraise tracks donors in the context of their transactions, not as long-term relationships with giving patterns, major gift capacity, and relationship history. Moves management, giving forecasts, and development officer assignments require more than transaction records.

**Grant management.** There is no grant tracking in Funraise. Award records, restricted fund allocation, expenditure tracking against grant-specific budgets, compliance report generation - none of this exists. Nonprofits with active grant portfolios run a separate system for compliance.

**Reporting depth.** Funraise's reporting is campaign-centric. Board-level financial reporting, FASB-compliant net asset presentations, and funder-required compliance documents require exports and additional work.

## The Operational Turning Point

The moment most nonprofits start looking for a Funraise alternative is not when they are unhappy with the donation form experience. It is when they realize the platform cannot answer questions that have become urgent:

- How much of our revenue this year came from restricted grants vs unrestricted individual giving?
- Which grant is our largest program salary charged against, and how does that reconcile to the funder's budget?
- What is our first-time donor retention rate by acquisition channel?

These questions require a system of record for the full organization's financial activity, not just the fundraising transaction layer.

## What GrantPipe Offers Instead

GrantPipe is designed for mid-sized nonprofits that have reached the point where they need both sides of nonprofit financial management in one place.

**Donor side:** Full contact management with relationship history, giving timelines, major gift tracking, segmentation, acknowledgment workflows, and donor retention analytics.

**Grant side:** Application tracking, award management, restricted fund allocation, expenditure tracking against grant-specific budgets, compliance report generation, and audit trail from award through closeout.

The two sides share the same underlying data. Development staff see donor records. Finance staff see grant compliance. Leadership sees both in integrated dashboards.

## Pricing Context

Funraise's published paid plans cover fundraising features before custom enterprise pricing begins. GrantPipe Starter has published pricing for the full platform with no per-transaction fees on top.

The comparison is not straightforward because the tools cover different scopes. A nonprofit that needs both Funraise's campaign tools and a full CRM is looking at Funraise plus a CRM - potentially $300-$600+/month combined. GrantPipe at published Starter pricing is a tighter, lower-cost alternative for organizations whose fundraising model does not depend on peer-to-peer campaigns.

## When Funraise and GrantPipe Can Coexist

If peer-to-peer and event fundraising are central to your revenue model, and Funraise is performing well there, GrantPipe can serve as the CRM and compliance layer alongside it. The integration point is importing gift records from Funraise into GrantPipe as the system of record for donor relationships and grant management.

This is a more complex setup to maintain, but it is a reasonable path for organizations that need both category leaders rather than a compromise platform that does neither perfectly.
