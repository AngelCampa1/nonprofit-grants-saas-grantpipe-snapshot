## What WizeHive Does Well

WizeHive Zengine is a configurable grants platform. Think of it as the Salesforce of grant management: powerful, flexible, and requiring implementation before it is useful. Grantmakers with non-standard application forms, multi-stage review panels, or unusual award workflows find that flexibility valuable.

The company has a long track record with corporate foundations, state grant programs, and associations that run their own grant cycles. Its review panel and scoring workflows are genuinely strong.

## Who This Alternative Is For

You are probably evaluating WizeHive because a consultant or a grantmaker peer recommended it. If you are a grant-receiving nonprofit with a $500K to $10M budget, the fit is usually wrong. WizeHive is priced and configured for organizations with six-figure software budgets and in-house administrators to maintain the platform.

## Where WizeHive Falls Short for Grant-Receiving Nonprofits

**Enterprise-only pricing.** WizeHive does not publish rates. G2 reviewer estimates place annual contracts in the $15,000 to $40,000 range. Mid-sized nonprofits rarely find that cost proportionate to the workflow problem they are solving.

**Configuration burden.** Zengine is a platform, not a product. Every customer configures the forms, workflows, and reports themselves or pays WizeHive to do it. Implementations commonly run 90 days or longer.

**No donor CRM.** WizeHive focuses on the grant side entirely. Individual donors, recurring gifts, and donor retention analytics live in a separate system, which means another subscription and another integration point.

**Grantmaker bias.** The product's language, workflows, and sales motion are built around the organization cutting checks, not receiving them. Grantseekers can use it, but they are not the primary customer.

## When to Stay With WizeHive

Stay with WizeHive if you are running a complex grantmaking program that genuinely requires configurable application forms, review panels, and award workflows. Stay with WizeHive if you have the budget for enterprise software and an administrator who can own the platform.

Switch to GrantPipe if you are a grant-receiving nonprofit that wants donor management and grant compliance in one focused system, with a price you can see before you talk to sales.

## Migration Path

Because WizeHive is configured rather than packaged, migration depends on how your instance was built. Exports are usually available for contacts, applications, and awards. GrantPipe's import templates accept CSV for all three. The real migration work is deciding which of your WizeHive custom fields to keep and which were workarounds for features GrantPipe includes natively.

## Questions to Answer Before Switching

1. How much of your WizeHive configuration reflects real workflow needs versus patches for missing features?
2. What does your total software stack cost today once you add the donor CRM and accounting system WizeHive does not include?
3. How long does a funder financial report take to produce today? If the answer is more than an hour, a focused product will save real time.
