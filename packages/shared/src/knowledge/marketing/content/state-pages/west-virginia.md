West Virginia has approximately 12,000 registered nonprofits, with the largest concentrations in Charleston, Huntington, Morgantown, and Parkersburg. The state's nonprofit sector operates in a funding environment shaped by its Appalachian designation, its heavy reliance on state DHHR contracts for social services, and a private foundation sector anchored by the Claude Worthington Benedum Foundation and the Clay Foundation.

## ARC Grants: Statewide Access, Complex Compliance

West Virginia is the only state where every one of its 55 counties qualifies for Appalachian Regional Commission funding, which gives nonprofits statewide access to ARC grants regardless of their location. This geographic universality is a meaningful funding opportunity, but ARC's compliance requirements are more complex than many small West Virginia nonprofits expect when they first receive an award.

ARC grants typically require matching funds, often 50% or more of the total project cost. Multi-year ARC awards require annual progress reports, expenditure documentation tied to approved project budgets, and documentation that matching funds were actually spent on the project. For a small nonprofit managing an ARC grant alongside a DHHR service contract and a Benedum Foundation award, the matching fund tracking and multi-year budget reconciliation work adds compliance obligations that do not exist with standard foundation grants.

## State Registration Requirements

West Virginia requires registration with the Secretary of State's office before an organization may solicit donations from West Virginia residents. Annual renewal is required, and organizations with revenues above $25,000 must submit financial statements. Organizations above $500,000 must submit audited financials. The registration requirement applies regardless of whether the organization receives ARC or DHHR grants.

Nonprofits receiving DHHR grants are subject to additional agency-specific audit requirements. West Virginia DHHR is one of the state's largest grant-making agencies, and DHHR program officers conduct site visits and expenditure reviews on active contracts. A compliance finding on a DHHR contract can affect renewal and competitive standing for future DHHR awards.

## Major Grant Programs in West Virginia

West Virginia-specific grant programs that mid-sized nonprofits commonly receive include DHHR grants for human services programs, WV Dept. of Education grants for educational support programs, Appalachian Regional Commission grants (all 55 counties eligible), and grants through the Claude Worthington Benedum Foundation and the Clay Foundation in Charleston. The Benedum Foundation is the largest private funder in West Virginia and accounts for a disproportionate share of the state's private grant dollars.

Federal grants from HHS, HUD, USDA Rural Development, and EPA flow to West Virginia nonprofits in health, housing, rural development, and environmental remediation program areas. The state's ongoing economic transition away from coal creates significant federal community development funding opportunities for nonprofits working in workforce development and economic diversification.

## Why Software Matters for West Virginia Nonprofits

West Virginia nonprofits managing ARC multi-year grants alongside DHHR contracts and Benedum Foundation awards need a compliance system that can track matching fund obligations, multi-year budget reconciliation, and funder-specific reporting requirements in a single place. Organizations that track ARC matching requirements in spreadsheets and DHHR expenditures in separate systems create reconciliation problems that surface during audits.

Grant management software that handles restricted fund tracking across ARC, federal, state, and foundation grants reduces the per-grant compliance overhead that grows as West Virginia nonprofits diversify their funding portfolios. For organizations managing ARC grants for the first time, software that documents matching fund obligations and tracks expenditures against approved project budgets reduces the risk of compliance findings that can affect future ARC competitiveness.
